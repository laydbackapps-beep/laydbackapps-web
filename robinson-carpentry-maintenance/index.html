Questions

Before you book

Do you work to a fixed price?+

Yes. The scope is measured first and quoted in writing, so the price for that scope does not move unless you change it.

Do you take on small jobs?+

Maintenance and small repairs are part of the business, not an afterthought — a sticking door or a rotted fascia is a normal booking.

Can you work around other trades?+

Yes. On renovations the timber work is sequenced with the plumber, electrician and plasterer so nothing gets pulled apart twice.

What materials do you use?+

Timber specified to the job — structural grades where it carries load, and a suitable treated or hardwood finish where it is exposed to weather.

The trade

Robinson Carpentry & Maintenance works both ends of that: the structural timber behind the walls, and the maintenance that keeps it right years later.
