Process

Coat by coat

How a room goes from bare to finished

The order that keeps edges sharp and the finish flat.

01

Walk-through & quote

02

Surface prep

Sanding, filling and masking so coatings actually bond.

03

Prime & cut in

04

Rolled and laid off for an even, low-sheen surface.

Touch-ups, a walk-through together, and the site left tidy.

Questions

Before you book

The things people ask first

Do you quote before any work starts?

Yes. Every job is measured and quoted up front, and the quote covers prep and materials so there are no surprises mid-job.

How long does an interior repaint take?

Most single rooms are two to three days once prep is done; a full home interior runs one to two weeks depending on size and access.

Do you handle exterior and roof coatings too?

Yes, weatherboards, render, trim and metal roofs are all part of the scope, so one team takes the whole exterior.

Which suburbs do you cover?

Warners Bay and the Lake Macquarie suburbs, through to Newcastle and the surrounding areas.
