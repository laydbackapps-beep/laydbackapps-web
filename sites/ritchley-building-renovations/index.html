How a build runs

  1. Site visit and scope

    What is there now, what has to change, and what the plan actually requires. Access, parking and where materials can sit all get sorted here.

  2. Quote against the drawings

    The number is built from the drawings and the site, not a square-metre rate. Allowances are written down rather than left vague.

  3. Programme and trades

    Order of work, how long each stage takes, and which trades are booked when — so the house is not left open waiting on a sequence mistake.

  4. Build and inspect

    Work is done to the approved drawings, with framing and structural stages ready for inspection before they are closed up.

  5. Defects and handover

    Walk through together, list what needs attention, and fix it before the final account is settled.

Questions that come up

Do you work from someone else's drawings?

Yes. Plans, engineering and permits can come from your designer — the quote is priced against those documents.

Can you organise the other trades?

For a full renovation, yes. Plumbers, electricians and plasterers are sequenced as part of the programme.

Do you take on small jobs?

Deck repairs, doors and smaller carpentry are fine. Anything structural gets assessed on site first.

How far do you travel?

Geelong and the Bellarine, plus the Surf Coast for the right job.

Send the plans

Address, what the job involves and whether drawings exist. If you have photos of the space, mention it and we will ask for them.
