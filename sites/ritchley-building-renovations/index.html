What we build

Work we take on, and how far we take it.

Residential building and structural carpentry. Every job is quoted from the drawings and the site, not from a square metre rate over the phone.

01

New builds

Set-out, footings, slab, frame, brickwork, roof and fit-out. Full builds run to the engineer's details and the certifier's inspections, with the program written before we start.

02

Renovations

Whole-house and single-room renovations where the existing structure has to be opened, supported and put back together correctly before anything is finished.

03

Extensions

Rear additions, second storeys and extra rooms, tied into the existing dwelling with proper footings, floor structure and roof connections.

04

Decks and outdoor structures

Timber and composite decks, pergolas, carports and verandahs. Bearers, joists and fixings sized for the span, not guessed.

05

Structural work

Beams, lintels, load-bearing walls, floor systems, underpinning and re-stumping. Temporary propping planned and documented before a wall comes out.

How we work

Four stages, in order, no shortcuts.

Building is a sequence. Get the early stages right and the finishing stages look after themselves.

Stage 01

Set out

Site measured, levels taken, profiles set and the job checked against the approved drawings before any excavation.

Stage 02

Structure

Footings and slab poured to spec, frame stood plumb and braced, roof on. Engineer's details followed exactly.

Stage 03

Envelope

Brickwork, cladding, windows, external doors, flashings and roof plumbing. Weather tight, then inspected.

Stage 04

Finish

Linings, joinery, tiling, fit-off and paint. Defects listed and cleared, site cleaned, keys handed over.
