Concrete, landscaping and fencing built to hold.

Newcastle Concrete, landscaping & fencing is a Hunter-based crew covering the three stages that usually get hired out separately — the slab, the yard around it, and the fence that finishes the boundary. One crew, one schedule, from formwork to final clean-up.

Concreting

Driveways, paths, slabs and shed or tank pads — formed, poured and finished, with control joints cut and the site left clean.

Decorative finishes

Coloured, stencilled and exposed-aggregate finishes for driveways, alfresco areas and paths that need more than plain grey.

Landscaping

Garden beds, levels, edging and retaining work that ties the new slab into the rest of the yard.

Fencing

Boundary, side-access and garden fencing built to line up with the concrete and landscaping stages, not fight them.

Site preparation

Excavation, formwork and base preparation done properly before anything is poured or planted.

Repairs & patching

Cracked or sunken paths and slabs cut back, re-poured and finished to match what is already there.
