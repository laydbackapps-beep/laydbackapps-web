Landscaping & Garden Services

Practical earthworks, structural retaining, and enduring plant palettes tailored to Hunter Region blocks.

Retaining Walls & Terraced Levels

Timber sleeper, sandstone block, and concrete sleeper retaining walls engineered with proper aggregate backfill and ag-line drainage to turn sloped yards into usable flat lawn.

Garden Design & Native Softscaping

Soil conditioning, mulched garden beds, coastal-hardy natives, feature trees, and low-maintenance screening plants selected for your block’s sun aspect.

Turf Preparation & New Lawns

Rotary hoeing, laser leveling, premium turf underlay, and fresh Sir Walter Buffalo, Kikuyu, or Zoysia turf laid tight with starter fertiliser.

Paving, Pathways & Garden Edging

Stepping-stone side access paths, compacted gravel courtyards, corten steel edging, and timber garden borders that keep lawn and garden beds cleanly separated.

How Every Garden Project Runs

One dedicated team from initial site levels through to final plant-out and clean-up.

1. On-Site Walk & Levels

We meet you on site, check access widths, fall, stormwater runoff, and existing soil conditions.

2. Itemised Written Quote

Clear breakdown of earthworks, retaining materials, soil volumes, turf, and plant schedules.

3. Earthworks & Structure

Excavation, drainage lines, retaining walls, and hard edging completed before topsoil arrives.

4. Planting, Turf & Handover

Conditioned soil, specimen planting, fresh turf rolled smooth, and a clean driveway on departure.
