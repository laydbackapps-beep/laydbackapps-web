How a run works

Four steps, one phone call

Water is heavy and time matters on a hot day, so the run is planned before the truck leaves.

01

Tell us the job

What the water is for, how much you think you need, and where it has to end up — tank, pool, site or trough.

02

Check the access

Driveway width, overhead lines, turning space and hose distance. If the truck cannot reach, we say so before booking.

03

Book a time

Loads are scheduled around the run, so you get a window rather than a maybe. Big fills are split into consecutive loads.

04

Deliver & fill

Water placed where it is needed, hose packed up, and the tank level confirmed with you before the truck leaves.

Questions

Asked before every load

How much water do I need for a pool?
It depends on the pool volume — a typical backyard pool is several loads. Tell us the dimensions and we can work the litres out before you book.
Can you fill a tank that is away from the driveway?
Usually, if the hose run is reasonable. Send a photo of the access and where the tank sits and it can be confirmed before the truck is sent.
Is the water suitable for drinking tanks?
It is carted water, not bottled drinking water. For a potable tank the tank itself should be cleaned and the water treated as your supplier advises — ask when booking.
Do you clean the tank as well as fill it?
Yes. Sludge and sediment are pumped out, the tank is cleaned, then refilled — which is the sensible time to do both.
How far do you travel?
Paterson, Maitland, Dungog and across the Hunter. Longer runs are quoted with the travel included so there is no surprise on the day.
