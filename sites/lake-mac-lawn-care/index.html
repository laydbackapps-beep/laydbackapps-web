Services

Mowing and everything that goes with it

Six things that make up almost every job we do. Take the lot on a round, or just the piece you don't want to do yourself.

01

Mowing & edging

Lawns cut at the height that suits the grass, edges trimmed along paths and fences, and every hard surface blown clean before we leave.

02

Hedging & pruning

Hedges squared back to size or shaped to the line, shrubs cut back off paths and windows, and the cuttings carried out to the trailer.

03

Garden maintenance

Beds weeded, mulch topped up, edges re-cut with a spade so the lawn and the garden read as two separate things again.

04

Turf & topsoil

Ground levelled and screeded, fresh turf laid and watered in the same day, with a week of follow-up watering advice that actually works.

05

Green waste removal

Grass, prunings and pulled weeds loaded onto the trailer and gone the same visit — no pile left sitting on the lawn for the council.

06

Garden edges & small retaining

Timber edges and low retaining walls set straight and level, with drainage sorted behind them so the bed doesn't wash out.

Questions

The things people ask before they book

Still not covered? Ring 0431 593 426 and you'll get a straight answer, not a sales call.

Do you take the green waste away?
Yes — every visit. Grass, prunings, pulled weeds and hedge cuttings go onto the trailer and off site the same day.
How often will you come?
Weekly or fortnightly while the lawn is growing, then monthly through winter when it slows down. You set which cycle you want.
Can you do a one-off clean-up?
Often, yes — an overgrown yard, a tidy-up before a sale or an end-of-lease garden. Those are quoted on the site, not by the hour.
What do you need to give a price?
The address and a rough idea of the block — front and back, roughly how big, and how long since it was last done.
Are you insured, and do you have an ABN?
Fully insured and ABN 73 550 056 977. Happy to send the certificate before we start if you need it on file.
What happens if it rains?
The visit moves to the next dry day, usually within the same week. We'll text you rather than leave you guessing.
