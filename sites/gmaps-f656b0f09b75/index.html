About

Hunter NSW / Est. 2018

SMS Formwork Newcastle delivers board-formed concrete walls with tie-hole precision across the Hunter region. We specialise in structural and architectural formwork for commercial, infrastructure, and residential projects where finish quality and dimensional accuracy are non-negotiable.

Every pour is planned around the tie-hole grid — the organizing logic that aligns structure, services, and architectural intent. Our crews work to millimetre tolerances on tie placement, ensuring clean cone recovery and consistent surface expression across every panel.

Based in Toronto NSW, we operate across Newcastle, Lake Macquarie, Maitland, and the wider Hunter Valley.

Method

Tie Grid Planning

Every pour is set out before a shutter is built — hole spacing, cone type, and panel modules established so structure and finish follow the same grid.

Shutter Assembly

Form faces assembled, sealed, and set true panel by panel; ties, bracing, and joints checked so pour lines land where the grid says they will.

Pour & Stripping

Concrete placed in coordinated lifts with vibration control, then stripped, cones recovered, and tie holes patched cleanly before handover.
