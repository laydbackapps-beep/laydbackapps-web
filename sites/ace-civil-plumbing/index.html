Method

How a drainage run gets built

The same sequence on every job, whether it is a lot connection or a trunk main.

  1. 01

    Set-out and excavation

    Alignment pegged, levels confirmed, trench excavated to design line and grade under laser control.

  2. 02

    Bedding and pipe lay

    Granular bedding placed and compacted. Precast or uPVC segments lowered, socketed and aligned joint by joint.

  3. 03

    Jointing and testing

    Rubber ring or solvent joints made, hydrostatic or air pressure test to AS/NZS 2566, CCTV inspection recorded.

  4. 04

    Backfill and reinstatement

    Selected backfill in layers, compacted to spec. Surface reinstated to pavement, turf or verge.
