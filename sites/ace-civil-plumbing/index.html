01 / Scope of works

What we build underground

Six kinds of work, each quoted on site against the levels and the tie-in point — not over the phone. Council work, new builds and lot connections.

ItemWorksWhat's included

Water mains & cut-ins

Mains tapping and live cut-ins, meter sets including 100 mm ultrasonic meters, thrust blocks and poly service feeds to the lot.

Sewer property connections

Junction cut-in to the main, grade set and pipe bedded, opened for inspection before any backfill goes on.

Stormwater & drainage

Pit-to-pit pipe runs, grated pits, headwalls and tie-ins into the council system, laid to fall.

Pits, excavation & reinstatement

Rubble pits, trench excavation, spoil removal, compaction and surface reinstatement back to council standard.

Subdivisional works

Water, sewer and stormwater reticulation for lot servicing, staged so each stage can be handed over clean.

Inspection & repair

CCTV runs on sewer and stormwater lines, locating, and repair of failed or blocked sections.

03 / Stages

Four stages, one inspection point

Nothing gets covered until it has been checked — that is the part that saves the second visit.

Set out

Locate existing services, confirm levels and agree the tie-in point before the machine starts.

Excavate

Trench and pit to depth, with the bedding material and support the ground needs.

Lay & test

Pipe, joint and pressure test, opened for inspection while it is still visible.

Reinstate

Backfill, compact and restore the surface — turf, concrete or road, to council standard.

Set out → excavate → lay & test → reinstate · inspection before backfill
