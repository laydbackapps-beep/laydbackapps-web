01

What we do.

Renovation work is the trade, end to end — the wet areas are waterproofed and tiled properly, the framing is straightened, and the finishes are set out so the room actually works.

01

Kitchen renovations

Full kitchen rebuilds — cabinetry set out to the room, splashback and floor tiling, and the plumbing and power moved to where the bench actually wants them.

02

Bathroom renovations

Wet areas taken back and rebuilt: new sheeting and waterproofing, floor and wall tiling to falls, then the screen, vanity, tapware and fittings set in straight and square.

03

Wall & floor tiling

Large-format, subway and mosaic tiling for walls and floors — level, set out from the room's true lines, with the cuts kept where they are not the first thing you see.

04

Granny flats

Secondary dwellings and extensions built and finished — framing, sheeting, waterproofing, tiling and the fit-out, so the flat is usable and rentable as its own space.

05

Decking & pergolas

Timber and composite decking and pergolas framed, set out and fixed properly — bearers, joists and posts sized and spaced, and the boards laid to drain.

06

Timber framing & waterproofing

Structural framing for renovations and extensions, plus the membrane and waterproofing that keeps a bathroom from quietly rotting the floor out from under it.

How a job runs.

From the first call to the last tile, here is the order a renovation runs in.

01

Get in touch

Call or message with what you want to change and a rough idea of the room. Photos help us price it accurately before we visit.

02

Measure & quote

We measure the room, check what is behind the walls and under the floor, and put the scope and price in writing.

03

Strip & set out

The old fit-out comes out, the framing and floor are checked and straightened, and the new layout is set out on site.

04

Waterproof & tile

Sheeting and waterproofing go in first, then the floor and wall tiling to the right falls and set-out, square to the room.

05

Fit off & hand over

Screen, vanity, tapware and cabinetry fitted, silicone and seals done, and the site cleaned down before we hand it back.

04

Why the finish matters more than the price.

A renovation is judged by the grout lines, the falls in the floor and whether the door still closes in five years. That is where the time goes.

Wet areas done once

Waterproofing and falls are the parts nobody sees and everything depends on. They get done properly the first time, or the whole job is wasted.

Set out square to the room

Tiling is set out from the room's true lines, not the old wall, so the job looks level even where the house is not.

One tradesman, one standard

You deal with the person doing the work, so decisions get made on site rather than passed between three different crews.

Straight, written quotes

Scope and price in writing before the first tile comes off, with variations priced before they are built.

05

Asked before we start.

The questions that come up on almost every renovation quote.

How long does a bathroom renovation take?

A straightforward bathroom is typically a couple of weeks on site once the strip-out is done — waterproofing needs time to cure before tiling starts, and that curing time is not something worth rushing. You get a timeframe with the quote.

Do you do the waterproofing as well as the tiling?

Yes. Waterproofing is part of the bathroom and wet-area work — the sheeting and membrane go in before any tiling, so the job is watertight at the source rather than relying on the grout.

Can you work to a budget?

Tell us the number at the start and we will tell you what it realistically buys. Moving plumbing and changing the layout costs far more than finishes, so that is usually where a budget is won or lost.

Do you do kitchens as well as bathrooms?

Yes — full kitchen rebuilds, splashback and floor tiling, and the plumbing and electrical moved to suit the new layout. Kitchens and bathrooms are the two rooms that decide what a renovation is worth.

Will I need to move out during the work?

For a single bathroom, usually not — if there is a second bathroom in the house, most people stay put. For a kitchen or a whole-home renovation we will tell you honestly what will be usable and when.

Is the quote free?

Yes. We come out, measure the room and give you a written price. No charge for the quote and no obligation to go ahead.
