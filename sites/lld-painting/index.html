How we work

Prep first, then paint

Most of a good repaint is the work nobody sees: the washing, sanding, filling and masking before the first coat.

Prep before paint

Surfaces washed, sanded, filled and primed, because paint over a shortcut shows up in the first summer.

Clean, sharp edges

Masked lines and cut-in edges that stay straight, with no roller spatter on the trims or floors.

Rooms left tidy

Drop sheets down while we work, gear packed up and the room handed back clean and usable.
