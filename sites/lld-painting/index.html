Where the quality comes from

Two jobs that look the same, and are not

A cheap quote and a proper one usually differ in one place: how much time goes into the surface before the first coat.

What a rushed job does

Paint straight over the old surface, fill without sanding back, and skip the primer because it saves a day.

  • Filler that shrinks and shows as a shadow
  • Peeling on chalky or glossy surfaces
  • Brush marks and lap lines under certain light
  • Colour that does not cover in two coats

What we do instead

Wash, scrape, fill, sand, spot-prime and then coat — with drying times respected rather than compressed.

  • Surfaces sound and dust-free before coating
  • Correct primer for timber, render or previously painted
  • Cut lines taped and inspected, not eyeballed
  • Colour checked in the room's own light
The bit that takes the time

Preparation, in plain terms

Paint does not hide a bad surface, it magnifies it. Once a wall is coated in a low-sheen finish, every ridge of unsanded filler and every raised edge of old paint shows up in raking light — and by then the only fix is to sand it back and start again.

So the prep is done as a stage in its own right: patched and left to cure, sanded flat, dusted down, and spot-primed where the surface is bare or porous. On exterior work that means treating mould and chalky paint before anything is applied, because a coating that cannot bond will fail no matter how good the product is.

Masking and protection are part of the same stage — floors covered, fittings removed where they can be, edges taped and then checked. It is slower than cutting in by eye, and it is the reason a finished room does not have a line of paint on the skirting.

Questions

Asked before most quotes

Do you supply the paint?
Yes, and it is included in the quote at a known quality level. If you want to supply your own, that is fine — the quote is adjusted and the product is confirmed before the day.
How many coats?
Two coats over a properly primed surface is standard. A strong colour change, or a surface that has not been painted in years, can need a third — that is priced up front, not raised at the end.
Do you move furniture?
Small items are moved and covered. Large furniture is best cleared beforehand; if that is not possible, it is worked around and discussed at the quote.
Can you paint while we are living there?
Yes, room by room. The schedule is set so one space is finished and usable before the next is started.
What about weather for exterior work?
Exterior painting is scheduled around forecast rain and temperature, because coating in the wrong conditions is how a job fails early.
