Stone that stays put

Custom Sandstone builds sandstone retaining walls, garden walls, feature walls, steps and entry walls, pillars and caps, and handles wall repairs and rebuilds across the Central Coast.

Every wall is built the same way: split-face sandstone, proper base preparation, drainage behind the wall, and courses laid to bond.

What we build

Five things, done properly

S-01

Retaining walls

Structural split-face walls with drainage, geogrid where specified, and engineered footings.

S-02

Garden & feature walls

Freestanding boundary and feature walls, capped and bonded, built to suit the site.

S-03

Steps & entry walls

Sandstone treads on compacted base, with entry flank walls tied in, risers and treads to code.

S-04

Pillars & caps

Pier construction with bullnose or flat caps - gate piers, wall ends, letterbox piers.

S-05

Wall repairs & rebuilds

Dismantle failed sections, rebuild footings, re-lay stone to match existing coursing.

How a wall goes up

Five steps, no shortcuts

01

Enquiry & site visit

Walk the site, check levels, access, and soil. Discuss scope and stone options.

02

Stone selection

Choose grade, colour, and split-face texture from local quarry stock.

03

Base & drainage

Excavate, compact road base, lay ag-pipe and geotextile behind wall line.

04

Lay, level & bond

Stack courses with offset joints, check plumb and level every course.

05

Finish & tidy

Cap walls, backfill, compact, clean site. Walk-through with client.
