Fortnightly through the growing season and monthly when it slows — weekly for a lawn that is being brought back.

Do you do one-off clean-ups?
Yes — overgrown yards, end-of-lease tidy-ups and pre-sale clean-ups are quoted from a look at the yard.
Do you bring your own equipment?
Mower, whipper-snipper, blower and hedge trimmer all come with us. We only need access to water if the job needs it.
