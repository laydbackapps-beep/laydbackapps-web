Cross-Section — Method

How It Runs

Measure & quote — On-site fall check, outlet count, downpipe locations. Fixed price, no hourly surprises.

Material order — COLORBOND® cut to length, brackets spaced to AS/NZS 3500.3, expansion allowance built in.

Install — Fascia straight, gutter fall true, downpipes plumb. Silicone every joint, rivets at 500mm centres.

Test & handover — Water test every run. Certificate of compliance where required. Clean site, no offcuts left.
