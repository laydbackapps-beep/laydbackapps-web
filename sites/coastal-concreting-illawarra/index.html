04

Asked before the pour.

Do you do council crossovers?
Yes — Coastal Concreting Illawarra is an approved concretor for Shellharbour and Wollongong council crossovers, so the driveway apron and footpath crossing are done to council spec.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes — we are fully licensed and insured. Ask us for the details with your quote.
What concrete surfaces do you finish?
Broom, trowel and standard finishes are the everyday picks for driveways and slabs. If you have a finish in mind — including decorative or exposed-aggregate looks — tell us and we'll talk through what suits the job.
How long before I can walk or drive on it?
Concrete keeps hardening for weeks. As a rule you can walk on it after a day or two and get a car on it after about a week, but the exact timing depends on the mix and the weather — we'll tell you when we hand the job over.
Which areas do you cover?
Shellharbour, Wollongong, Albion Park and around the Illawarra. If you're nearby and not sure, give us a call.
Do you give a free quote?
Yes — we look at the site, then give you a firm written quote before any work starts.
