Fascia covers

Repairs & maintenance

Questions

Before you book a quote

Why aluminium instead of steel or Colorbond?
This close to the coast, salt air eats steel guttering. Aluminium doesn't rust, so it holds up far better in coastal conditions.
Do you do repairs or only full replacements?
Both. A leaking joint, a sagging run or a blocked outlet can often be fixed on its own. If the gutter has gone through, we'll say so.
Can you fit leaf screens to my existing gutters?
Usually, yes — as long as the gutter itself is sound. If the channel has rusted or the fall is wrong, screening it just hides the problem.
How long does a re-gutter take?
Most single-storey homes are done in a day or two, depending on the length of the runs, the roof pitch and the access. You'll get a timeframe with the quote.
Is the quote really free?
Yes. We come out, look at the roof and the gutters, and give you a written figure. No charge and no obligation.
Do you need access to the roof?
Yes, so please mention anything that affects access — steep ground, a carport in the way, a locked gate, or solar panels on the roof. It helps us quote accurately.
