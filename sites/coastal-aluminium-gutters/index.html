Guttering and roofing work, done to suit the house

Aluminium is the right call on the coast: it does not rust, it handles the salt air, and it can be run in long lengths so there are fewer joins for water to find. Every job starts with a look at the roof, the fall of the gutter and where the water actually needs to go.

Re-guttering

Old, sagging or rusted gutters pulled down and replaced in aluminium. Long runs, correct fall to the outlets, and new brackets as needed so the gutter keeps its shape through a big downpour.

Downpipes

Downpipes in the right size and position — including changes so water is taken away from the footings instead of dumping under the house or into a garden bed.

Fascia covers

Fascia that is weathered, chipped or soft gets covered with a folded aluminium fascia cover, so the edge of the roof is sealed up and the line of the house comes back.

Leaf screens

Leaf screen across the gutter run for homes under trees. It keeps the leaf litter and the gutters from turning into a garden bed in a wet season.

Re-roofing

Re-roofing and roof replacement, plus the roofing work that goes with it — flashings, valley and ridge work, and tying the new roof into the gutter line properly.

Leaks & repairs

Tracking down where a wet patch is really coming from — a failed seal, an overflowing gutter, a blocked outlet — and fixing the cause rather than patching over it.
