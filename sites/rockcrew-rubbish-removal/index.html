What we take away

One load or a whole yard. Point at what has to go and it leaves with us, sorted for the tip, the green waste pile or the charity bin where it can.

  1. 01

    Household rubbish

    Kitchen and laundry clutter, broken appliances, mattresses, bagged general waste from a clean-out or a move.

  2. 02

    Yard and green waste

    Branches and prunings, grass clippings, palm and shrub cuttings, storm debris after a blow through.

  3. 03

    Garage and shed clean-ups

    Old shelving, paint tins, tools that never sold at the garage sale, the lot cleared out and swept up.

  4. 04

    Furniture removal

    Couches, beds, wardrobes, dining settings. Carried out of the room, down the steps and onto the truck.

  5. 05

    General rubbish

    Mixed loads from a property, a rental turnover or a deceased estate, big or small.

How a clean-out runs

Straightforward from the first message, because nobody wants a job that drags on over three phone calls.

Step one

Tell us what is there

A message with the address, what needs to go and a photo or two is enough for us to size the job up.

Step two

We price the load

You get a price for the load before we hook the trailer up, so there is nothing to argue about on the day.

Step three

We load and go

Truck and trailer in, everything onto the tipper, and the space left broom clean behind us.

On the job

Yards, verges and gutters cleared out. Green waste separated from the general load as it goes on.
