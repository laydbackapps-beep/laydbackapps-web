03

Asked on every build.

Do you only build decks?
No, decks and pergolas are a specialty, but MWD takes on all aspects of carpentry: fix-outs, bathroom and home renovations, roof and wall framing, flooring, doors and windows, cladding and sheds.
What timber do you build with?
Hardwood and blackbutt are the favourites for decks; we will talk through options for your job and budget.
Do you cover Port Stephens?
Yes, Corlette, Nelson Bay, Salamander Bay and around Port Stephens.
Do you do repairs and re-oiling?
Yes, deck repairs, board replacement and oiling, not just new builds.
Do you quote before starting?
Always, a firm written quote before any work starts.
