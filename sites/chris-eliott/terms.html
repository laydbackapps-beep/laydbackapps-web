Terms of Use

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Quotes and scope

Work is quoted after the property has been looked at. A quote covers the scope written on it; anything outside that scope is discussed with you before it is carried out.

Enquiries

Submitting the enquiry form is a request for a quote, not an order for work. Work starts once the scope and the price have been agreed.

Changes on site

If something found once work starts changes the scope — framing that needs replacing, access that needs rework — you are told before the extra work is done.

Site access

Safe access to the property and the work area is required for the job to proceed as quoted.
