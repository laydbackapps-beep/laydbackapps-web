02 — On the sheet

The order a residential build runs in

Four stages, and the work at each one has to be right before the next starts. It's also how a job gets priced — which stage you're at changes what's involved.

01 Set out02 Frame03 Lock-up04 Fit-out
  1. 01
    Set outThe building is positioned and levelled on the block before anything is fixed down.
  2. 02
    FrameSlab, frame and roof structure — the stage where dimensions and bracing matter most.
  3. 03
    Lock-upRoof, windows, doors and cladding, so the building is closed in and weather-tight.
  4. 04
    Fit-outLinings, joinery, wet areas and finishes, through to handover.
