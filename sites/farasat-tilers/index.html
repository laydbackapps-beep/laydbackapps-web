FAQ

Questions before you book

How do I get a quote?

Message the page with the space, your plans or a few photos, and the tiles you're considering. You'll get a price back before any work is booked.

Do you tile bathrooms and wet areas?

Yes — full bathroom walls and floors, shower recesses and falls to the drain are core to the work.

Can you lay large-format tiles?

Yes. Much of the work shown here is large-format and marble-look, laid flat with tight, even joints.

Do you do floors as well as walls?

Both. Floors, walls, full-height walls and single feature walls are all part of the job.

Can you build shower niches and recesses?

Yes — niches and recessed shelves are built into the wall and tiled so the edges sit flush and crisp.

What tile finishes do you work with?

Marble-look, stone-effect and porcelain, in the large formats shown in the gallery above.
