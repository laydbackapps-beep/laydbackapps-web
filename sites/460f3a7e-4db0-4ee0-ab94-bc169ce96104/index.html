How a renovation runs

Four steps, every job, so you always know what happens next and who is on site.

  1. First call

    Tell us the rooms, the timeline and what you want out of the job.

  2. Walk-through

    On site, checking the existing structure, services and access.

  3. Scope & price

    A written scope with allowances, and a price for the work.

  4. Build & handover

    Programmed trades, site kept tidy, signed off room by room.

Working across the Hunter

T J Nielsen Building works on homes through the Hunter region of NSW, handling renovations, bathrooms and interior work from the first walk-through to handover.

One builder stays with the job. That means the same person setting out the walls, checking the waterproofing and signing it off with you at the end.

What a renovation covers

  • Strip out, set out and structural openings.
  • Framing, plasterboard, cornice and trims.
  • Wet area waterproofing before tiling.
  • Tiling, cabinetry, fixtures and fittings.
  • Kitchen and laundry plumbing and electrical rough-in.
