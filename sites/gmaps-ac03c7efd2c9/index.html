In practice

What good concreting comes down to

A

Groundwork first

A slab is only as good as what's under it — ground prepared, formwork set square and level before any pour.

B

The right mix

Concrete specified for the job at hand, placed promptly and finished while it's workable.

C

Fall and drainage

Water is the enemy of concrete. Surfaces are finished to fall so water runs off, not pools.

D

Curing patience

Concrete gains strength as it cures. Rushing the finish shows years later — we let it take its time.
