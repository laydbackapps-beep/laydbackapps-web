Plastering across Brisbane and surrounds

We handle new-build linings, renovation extensions, plasterboard repairs, cornices and decorative features, ceilings and partitions, and full set/stop/sand finishing. Every job is measured, sheeted, and finished to a true line.

Based in McDowall, we work across Brisbane and surrounding suburbs - no travel surcharge within the metro area.

What we cover

S-01

New build linings

Wall and ceiling sheeting for new residential construction - plasterboard fixed, flushed, and ready for paint.

S-02

Renovation linings & extensions

Matching existing profiles, patching into old work, and lining new extension walls and ceilings.

S-03

Plasterboard repairs

Holes, cracks, water damage, and impact repairs - cut out, re-sheet, set, stop, and sand invisible.

S-04

Cornices & decorative features

Square-set, cove, colonial, and custom profiles - formed and fitted to a clean mitre.

S-05

Ceilings & partitions

Suspended and direct-fix ceilings, fire-rated and acoustic partitions, access panels integrated.

S-06

Setting, stopping & sanding

Three-coat finish - base, second, finish coat - machine-sanded to Level 4/5 readiness.

P-00

How a job runs

01

Enquiry & quote visit

Site walk, scope confirmed, fixed-price quote issued.

02

Set-out & measure

Laser level set-out, sheets ordered to minimise joints.

03

Sheet & fix

Plasterboard fixed to frame, staggered joints, screw pattern to code.

04

Set, stop & sand

Three coats applied, each sanded; final coat feathered to zero edge.

05

Final check & handover

Critical-light inspection, touch-ups, site left clean.
