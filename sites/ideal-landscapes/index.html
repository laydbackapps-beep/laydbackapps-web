Approach

Two halves of the same job

Most gardens go wrong where the drawing stops and the digging starts. Keeping both under one roof is the whole point — the person who sets the levels is the person who laid them out.

Part one

The design

We walk the block, work out how it drains, where the sun sits and how you actually want to use the space, then put that into a layout you can see before anything is dug.

  • Site measure, levels and drainage plan
  • Materials and finishes selected to the house
  • Planting chosen for the aspect and the soil
  • Staged so the budget goes where you notice it
Part two

The build

Same crew, same drawings. Set-out, excavation, sub-base and drainage done properly underneath, because that is the part nobody sees and the part that fails first.

  • Set-out and excavation to the levels on the plan
  • Compacted sub-base under every paved surface
  • Retaining designed for the height it holds
  • Site cleaned down and handed over ready to use
What we build

From a blank yard to a finished garden

Take the whole build, or bring us in for the piece you're stuck on.

01

Paving & hardstand

Sandstone, porcelain, concrete and travertine laid on a properly compacted base with the falls running away from the house, not into it.

02

Retaining walls

Concrete sleeper, sandstone block and rendered walls, engineered for the height they hold with drainage and ag-line behind the face.

03

Turf & lawns

Sub-grade prepared, levelled and rolled, then turf laid tight with the edges cut in and watered in the same day.

04

Planting & garden beds

Beds shaped, soil improved, plants set out by height and spread, mulched and staked where they need it.

05

Pool surrounds

Coping, paving and garden set around the pool shell with the levels and drainage worked out so water runs where it should.

06

Drainage & irrigation

Surface and sub-surface drainage, pits and charged lines, plus drip and sprinkler zones set up with a controller you can actually work.

Scope

What a typical project covers

Gardens & planting

Beds built and planted for the aspect, with soil and drainage sorted so things establish instead of sulking. Straightforward gardens are usually two to four days on site.

  • Soil improvement
  • Plant supply & set-out
  • Mulch & edging
Paving & retaining

The structural half. Excavation, sub-base, falls and the wall itself — usually one to two weeks, depending on access and how much spoil has to leave the site.

  • Excavation & spoil removal
  • Sub-base & drainage
  • Wall & paving install
Full garden build

Everything from a bare yard through to handover — design, levels, hard landscaping, planting and turf, run as one job with one point of contact.

  • Design & drawings
  • All trades coordinated
  • Handover walkthrough
Process

How the job runs, start to finish

Step one

Walk the block

We come out, measure, look at the levels and the drainage, and talk through how you want to use the space and what you'd like to spend.

Step two

Design and quote

A layout with materials and planting nominated, and a figure broken into stages so you can see what each part costs before committing.

Step three

Set out and build

Levels marked, excavation and sub-base done, then the hard landscaping, planting and turf in sequence — with the site kept tidy as it goes.

Step four

Handover

We walk it with you, sort anything that needs sorting, and leave you with watering and aftercare notes for the first few months.

Questions

Before you book

Do you design as well as build?
Yes — design and construction are the same business, which is why the levels on the plan are the levels on the ground.
Do you charge for a consultation?
The first site visit and a rough idea of cost are free. Detailed drawings and a staged quote come after that.
How far do you travel?
Sydney metro and the surrounding suburbs. Further out is possible — ring with the address and we'll tell you straight.
Can you work with an existing garden?
Often, yes. Some gardens need a new structure underneath and some just need the paving and planting redone — we'll say which one yours is.
How long will it take?
Straightforward gardens are days, paving and retaining are a week or two, and a full build runs several weeks. You get a programme with the quote.
Do you remove the spoil?
Yes — excavated material is carted off site as part of the job, not left in a pile on the nature strip.
