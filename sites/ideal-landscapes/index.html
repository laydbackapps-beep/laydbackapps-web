What we do

Design starts with the site: aspect, soil, drainage and how the family actually uses the space, rather than a template plan copied from a catalogue.

retaining

Construction covers paving, retaining, decking, turf and planting, with the hard landscaping done before a single plant goes in.

About Ideal Landscapes

Ideal Landscapes is a Sydney landscape construction and design business, working on residential outdoor spaces from the structure through to the planting.

Good landscaping is mostly what you cannot see afterwards — the levels, the drainage and the base under the paving — so those get built first.
