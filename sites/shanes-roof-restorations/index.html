The four stages

How a restoration runs

Each stage is finished before the next starts. Sealing over a roof that was not cleaned is the usual reason a restoration fails early.

01

Washed

The roof is cleaned back first — moss, lichen and dirt taken off so the surface can actually take a coating.

02

Repointed

Ridge caps and valleys are repointed, and failed mortar or bedding is replaced while the roof is open and dry.

03

Sealed

A sealer coat locks the surface down before colour goes anywhere near it, so the finish bonds evenly.

04

Painted

Coats are applied to a consistent film across the whole roof, so the colour reads the same from the street.

Before you call

Common questions

What does a roof restoration actually involve?
Four stages, in order: the roof is washed, the ridges and valleys are repointed, the surface is sealed, then painted. Skipping the wash or the seal is what makes a restoration fail early.
My roof only leaks in one spot — do I need the whole job?
Not necessarily. A single leak is often a repair — a cracked tile, failed flashing or open point. That is looked at first, because there is no sense restoring a roof if the problem is one localised fault.
How often does a roof need restoring?
It depends on the roof, the coating and how much shade and moss it carries. The honest answer is judged off the roof itself — once the surface is cleaned you can see whether the coating is sound or finished.
Can you match the colour of my roof?
Colour is matched as closely as the existing coating allows. An older roof weathers, so the match is judged against the roof once it has been washed, not from a colour chart on its own.
Do you work on tiled and metal roofs?
Yes — tiled and metal roofs are both part of the work, and each needs a different approach to cleaning, repointing and coating.
What do you need from me to quote?
A few photos of the roof from the ground, the rough size, and what the problem is — leaking, moss, faded coating or a full restoration. Call or send them through and a price follows.
