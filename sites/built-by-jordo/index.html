What I build

Structural and finishing carpentry, on houses that are being built, extended or fixed up.

Framing

Wall and roof framing set out from the plan, stood and braced square so the sheeting and trims have something straight to land on.

Decks

Bearers and joists fixed back to the house or on their own posts, decking laid with even gaps and the edges trimmed off clean.

Pergolas

Posts, beams and rafters set out to the span, brackets and fixings rated for the job, open or lined ready for a roof.

Fit-outs

Internal linings, doors and hardware, skirting, architraves and shelving, worked around the other trades so nothing gets spoiled.

Repairs

Water-damaged framing, rotten decking and bearers, failed door and window openings, cut out and rebuilt to match what is there.

Renovations

Walls taken out or moved, openings formed, new rooms framed and finished through to the trims and the final clean-up.

How I work

01

Measure, then price

The job gets looked at on site and priced from what is actually there. You hear about anything that changes before it is done, not after.

02

Set out and build

Set-out strings, levels and spans are checked first, then the work goes in square, plumb and fixed to the plan.

03

Finish and clean

Trims, hardware and the final fix ups are done on the same visit, and the offcuts and dust leave with me.

Do you work on new builds or just renovations?

Both. Framing on new work and fit-outs, and renovations where walls move and rooms get rebuilt through to the finishing.

Can you build a deck off an existing house?

Yes. The deck is set out off the existing floor level, with the bearers fixed back to the framing or taken down to posts, whichever suits the height.

Do you supply material or work from mine?

Either. Timber and hardware can be quoted in with the job, or I can build from material you have already arranged.

How do you handle other trades on the job?

The carpentry is sequenced around them. What gets fixed when is worked out before the job starts so nothing is closed up ahead of the electrician or the plumber.
