How a job runs

Same order every time, whether it is a deck or a full frame.

  1. STEP 01

    Set out

    I come out, look at the site and the plan, and set the lines out on the ground before anything is ordered or cut. If the set-out does not work, better to know that on day one.

  2. STEP 02

    Frame it

    Plates down and packed level, studs and trusses stood, bracing on before the frame is left for the night. Nothing gets sheeted until the frame is square.

  3. STEP 03

    Lock it up

    Sheeting, battens and flashings fitted so the building is closed and dry. This is where the water gets kept out, not later.

  4. STEP 04

    Finish it

    Doors hung, skirts and architraves on, decking laid, cabinetry scribed. The part of the job people actually touch every day.

  5. STEP 05

    Hand it back

    Site swept, offcuts gone, and a walk around together so any snag is written down and fixed before I call the job done.

What I check before I leave

The list I run on the way out the door. It is the same list whether the job was a door or a whole deck.

  • Square. Diagonals checked on every frame, deck and opening before it is fixed off.
  • Plumb and level. Studs, posts and jambs checked with the level, not the eye.
  • Fixing depth. Screws and nails into the member they are meant to be holding, not just through the face.
  • Gaps even. Decking gaps, board spacing and reveals run consistent across the job.
  • Cut ends clean. No torn timber left exposed on a deck or a pergola.
  • Site clean. Offcuts and packaging gone, and the area left so the next trade can walk in.

Questions I get asked

Do you work off plans or can you sort it out on site?
Both. If there are plans I work to them. If it is a deck or a pergola with nothing drawn, I set it out with you on site and confirm the sizes before anything is ordered.
Are you doing the whole job or just the frame?
Whatever suits. Some jobs I frame and hand over to the other trades, others I take through to the decking, doors and skirts. It is agreed before the first day.
Can you match the existing timber in my house?
In most cases. Send a photo of the profile and the room and I will tell you what will match and what will not before you commit to it.
How far do you travel?
Gold Coast and the surrounding areas. If it is further out, ask — if the job is the right size it is usually still worth the drive.
Do you do small repair jobs?
Yes. A rotten joist, a loose board, a door that will not close. Small jobs are how most people find a carpenter in the first place.
What do you need to quote?
The address, roughly what the job is, and any plans, photos or measurements you already have. From that I can tell you if I need to come out and look.
