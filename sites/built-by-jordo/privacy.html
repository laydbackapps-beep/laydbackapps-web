Privacy

Built By Jordo · carpentry · the Gold Coast · 0481 676 362

What we collect

When you send the quote request form on this site we receive the details you put in it: your name, phone number, suburb, the type of job and whatever you write about the work. Nothing else on this site asks you for personal information and there is no tracking of your visit against your name.

Why we collect it

Those details are used for one thing: to contact you about the job you asked about and to prepare a price. Your details are not sold, rented or passed to anyone else for marketing.

Who sees it

The request is delivered to Built By Jordo by email. It is kept only as long as it is needed for the quote and any work that follows from it.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete the details you sent at any time. Call 0481 676 362 and we will take them out of our records.

Contact

Questions about this page can go to Built By Jordo on 0481 676 362.
