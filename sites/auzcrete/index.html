How it runs

Start to finish on a slab.

Four stages, each signed off before the next one starts.

Site set-out and inspection

Laser-levelled grid, formwork to the engineer's marks, penetration sleeves marked and checked before the pour.

Sub-base and reinforcement

Compacted road base or sand bed, vapour barrier, top and bottom mesh chaired and tied to the specified cover.

Pour, screed and finish

Concrete placed to grade and laser-screeded, then bull-floated and finished to architectural tolerance: broom, trowel or exposed aggregate as specified.

Cure, saw-cut and handover

Seven-day wet cure, control joints cut to the engineer's schedule, site cleaned and the paperwork issued.
