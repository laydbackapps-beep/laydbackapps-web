What we do

Concrete work, set out and poured on site

From set-out and formwork through to the finish on the surface — driveways, paths, patios, slabs and crossovers.

01

Concrete driveways

New driveways and extensions formed, poured and finished on site — including swirl and broom finishes with dummy joints matched to existing concrete.

02

Paths & patios

Garden paths, alfresco areas and patio slabs set out, reinforced and poured to suit the levels of the block.

03

Slabs & footings

Shed, extension and house slabs, plus post footings and pads poured and set.

04

Exposed aggregate & finishes

Exposed aggregate, broom, swirl and colour finishes, with control and dummy joints cut to suit the slab.

05

Council crossovers

Driveway crossovers formed to council requirements and poured, then inspected and passed.

06

Formwork, reo & set-out

Set-out, excavation, timber formwork and reinforcing mesh prepared ahead of the pour.

How it runs

From set-out to a finished slab

The five stages of a Stoneline pour, start to finish.

  1. 01

    Get in touch

    Send a DM with the address and a couple of photos for a free quote.

  2. 02

    Set-out & excavation

    Levels set, the area cut to grade and the sub-base prepared.

  3. 03

    Formwork & reo

    Formwork built to the shape and fall, then reinforcing mesh placed and tied.

  4. 04

    Pour & screed

    Concrete poured, spread and screeded to level in one run.

  5. 05

    Finish & joints

    Broom, swirl, colour or exposed aggregate finish applied, joints cut and the slab cured.

Questions

Before you book a pour

What areas do you work in?

Lake Macquarie and the wider Hunter, NSW — recent jobs have been in Blacksmiths, Cardiff, Valentine and Paxton.

What concrete work do you take on?

Driveways, paths, patios, slabs, footings and council crossovers — new work and extensions to existing concrete.

Can you match existing concrete?

Yes. Finishes such as swirl and broom can be matched to the existing slab, with dummy joints set out to line up.

What finishes can you do?

Exposed aggregate, broom, swirl and colour finishes, with control joints cut to suit the slab.

How do I get a quote?

Message through Instagram with the address and a photo or two of the area for a free quote.

Do you do council crossovers?

Yes — crossovers are formed to council requirements and poured, ready for inspection.
