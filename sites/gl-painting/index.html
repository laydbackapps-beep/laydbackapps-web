What we paint.

Interior walls and ceilings

Rooms, hallways and whole houses. Patched, sanded and cut in properly so the finish reads flat in daylight.

Exteriors and facades

Render, weatherboard, brick and fascia. Washed, prepped for the surface, then coated for the weather it sits in.

Renovations and new builds

Working in behind other trades, with the program and the finish line agreed before we start.

Doors, trim and details

Frames, skirts, architraves and doors. The small surfaces that decide whether a room looks finished.

How a repaint runs.

Look at it and price it

We walk the job with you, count the rooms and note what needs patching. Written price comes after that, not before.

Prep

Washed, scraped, filled and sanded. Floors and joinery masked. Prep is most of the job and it is where a repaint lasts or doesn't.

Prime and cut in

Bare patches primed, edges cut in by hand, then the first coat rolled on.

Two coats and clean-up

Second coat, a walk-through with you, then the site left clean with the masking off.
