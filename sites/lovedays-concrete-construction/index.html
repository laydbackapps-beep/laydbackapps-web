What we pour

Domestic and light commercial concrete.

Every job runs the same way: set out to the drawing, sub-base compacted, formwork braced, steel tied on chairs, then the pour. Nothing is left to the day.

  • House and shed slabs

    Waffle and raft slabs, shed pads and carport slabs set to engineer's detail, with edge boards and levels checked before the first truck.

  • Driveways and crossovers

    Plain, broom finish or saw-cut pattern, built to the fall and the crossover your council wants, with tidy formed edges.

  • Footings and piers

    Strip footings, pad footings and piers dug, formed and steeled to the drawings, ready for the next trade to build off.

  • Exposed aggregate

    Seeded and washed back to the stone and colour you pick from a sample, so what is poured is what you saw.

  • Paths and alfresco slabs

    Pathways, patios and under-cover slabs with correct falls away from the house and edges run straight.

  • Cutting, grinding and sealing

    Control joints cut on time, edge grinding, and sealer applied to new or existing concrete.

How we work

The order that makes concrete last.

Concrete is unforgiving of shortcuts. This is the sequence every pour runs to, whether it is a garden path or a two-hundred square metre slab.

  1. Set out and levelsString lines, profiles and a laser level so edges, falls and heights are fixed before anything is cut.
  2. Excavation and compactionStripped to depth, sub-base placed and compacted in layers, ready for formwork.
  3. FormworkStraight, braced and oiled forms, checked for square and fall, so nothing bows under the weight of the mix.
  4. Steel and chairsReinforcement tied at the spacing on the drawing, sat on chairs so it finishes inside the slab.
  5. Pour and screedThe truck is placed, the mix spread and pulled down off a screed rail while it is still workable.
  6. Float and finishBull float, then hand float and trowel or broom to the finish chosen. Edges run and cleaned.
  7. Cut and cureControl joints cut on time, the slab cured, and the site left clean. Sealer where it is specified.
