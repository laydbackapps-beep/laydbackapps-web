How we work

The order that makes concrete last

Workmanship backed — If something is not right, Loveday's Concrete Construction comes back.

  • Set out and levelsString lines, profiles and a laser level so edges, falls and heights are fixed before anything is cut.
  • Excavation and compactionStripped to depth, sub-base placed and compacted in layers, ready for formwork.
  • FormworkStraight, braced and oiled forms, checked for square and fall. No bowing under the weight of the mix.
  • Steel and chairsReinforcement tied at the spacing on the drawing, sat on chairs so it finishes inside the slab.
  • Pour and screedThe truck is placed, the mix is spread and pulled down off a screed rail while it is still workable.
  • Float and finishBull float, then hand float and trowel or broom to the finish chosen. Edges run and cleaned.
  • Cut and cureControl joints cut on time, the slab cured, and the site left clean. Sealer where it is specified.
