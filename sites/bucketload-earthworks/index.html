What we take on

Six jobs that make up most of the week on a residential or small commercial site.

  1. 01

    Site cleans

    Offcuts, packaging, rubble and leftover material cleared off the block, loaded out and the ground swept down so the next trade has a clean surface to work on.

  2. 02

    Excavation

    Cut to the set-out level, with the machine working to the mark rather than the eye, and the spoil stacked or loaded as the job needs it.

  3. 03

    Site prep

    Ground stripped, stripped-back areas trimmed and the block brought to a consistent working level before footings or slab set-out.

  4. 04

    Earthmoving

    Bulk movement of soil and fill around the block — cut, carry and place, with tracks and skid steer gear matched to what the access will allow.

  5. 05

    Driveway prep

    The driveway line excavated and graded to the fall you want, ready for the base and the finish course to go on top.

  6. 06

    Spoil removal

    Excess soil and rubble loaded into trucks and taken off site, or left where you want it for backfill and raised pads.

How a site runs

The order the work happens in, from first cut to the block being handed back.

  1. STEP 1

    Strip and clear

    Topsoil and rubbish off the working area, with anything worth keeping stacked clear.

  2. STEP 2

    Excavate

    Cut down to the set-out level, machine working to the mark as the levels come up.

  3. STEP 3

    Cart the spoil

    Loaded out by truck, or placed where you want fill kept on the block.

  4. STEP 4

    Level and prep

    Ground brought to a consistent working level, driveway line graded to fall.

  5. STEP 5

    Hand back tidy

    Block swept, access left clear and the site ready for the next stage.

Questions we get

What does a site clean actually include?
Clearing the block of offcuts, packaging, rubble and leftover material, loading it out, and sweeping the working area down so the next trade has a clean surface.
Do you take the spoil away?
Yes. Excavated spoil is loaded into trucks and removed, unless you would rather keep it on site for backfill or a raised pad — either way it is decided before the machine starts.
Can you cut levels for a slab or a driveway?
Excavation and driveway prep are both worked to a set-out level. Give us the finished mark and we work back from it so the base goes down at the right depth.
What access does the machine need?
It depends on the block. Tell us what the entry and the working area are like and we will say up front which gear fits — a skid steer gets into places a bigger machine cannot.
How quickly can you get to a site?
Send the address and what needs doing and we will come back with the first day we can be on it. Quotes are priced from the site rather than over the phone.
What do you need from me to quote?
The address, roughly what the job involves — clean, cut, cart or prep — and any levels or plans you already have. That is usually enough to arrange a time to look.
