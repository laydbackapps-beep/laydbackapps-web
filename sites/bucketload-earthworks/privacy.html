Privacy

Plain English. This page covers the quote form on the Bucketload Earthworks site.

What is collected

Only what you type into the quote form: your name, phone number, and, if you add them, your email address, your suburb, what the job involves and the message you write.

Why it is collected

To get back to you about the job you have asked about, and for nothing else. There is no mailing list and no marketing use.

Who sees it

Bucketload Earthworks. The details are sent straight to the business through the site's quote form. They are not sold, rented or passed on to anyone else.

How long it is kept

For as long as the enquiry is live. When it is finished with, it is deleted.

Your choice

You can ask to see what has been kept, have it corrected, or have it removed at any time. Call 0405 018 819 or say so in your next message.

Fonts and tracking

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. The page loads a font file from Google Fonts, so Google sees the request for that file when the page opens.

What this site is

A page describing the earthworks Bucketload Earthworks does across Melton VIC. Nothing on it is a quote — jobs are priced once the block has been looked at.
