Privacy

Xcel Air, Lake Macquarie and Newcastle NSW

What we collect

When you send a quote request through this site we collect the name, phone number, email address and job details you type in. Nothing else is collected automatically beyond standard server logs.

Why we collect it

Only to reply to your enquiry and to arrange the work. We do not sell or rent your details and we do not add you to a marketing list without asking.

Who sees it

Your enquiry is emailed to Xcel Air. It is held in the mail account and job records for as long as needed to quote and complete the work.

Cookies

This site sets no tracking cookies. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of that request.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you at any time by contacting Xcel Air through the quote form or the Facebook page.

Accessibility

This site is built to work with keyboard, screen reader and reduced motion settings.
