What we do

Air conditioning work, start to finish

Installation, repairs and servicing for homes and businesses across Lake Macquarie and Newcastle.

Installation

A new split system or ducted system positioned, mounted and commissioned properly — sized for the rooms it has to cool.

Repairs

Units that have stopped cooling, blowing warm or leaking water — diagnosed and repaired, not just re-gassed and hoped for.

Servicing

Filters cleaned, coils checked, drains cleared and the system run through so it works efficiently season after season.

Split systems

Wall-mounted and multi-head split systems supplied and installed — one room, or several off a single outdoor unit.

Ducted systems

Ducted air conditioning looked after end to end, from airflow and zoning problems through to a full system replacement.

Maintenance

Ongoing servicing to keep the system running through a Lake Macquarie summer and a cold, damp Newcastle winter.

Systems & parts

The gear we work on every week

Indoor heads, ducted runs, outdoor condensers and the filters and coils inside them.

How we work

From your message to cold air

A short, straight job — no runaround.

Tell us what’s happening

The unit, the room, what it is doing (or not doing), and roughly what you want. A photo of the indoor and outdoor unit helps.

We size up the job

Whether it is a repair, a service or a new install, we work out what the space actually needs before anything is ordered or fitted.

Fitted, tested, explained

The work is done, the system is run up and tested, and you are shown what to keep an eye on so it keeps working.
