Gates and fences, bay by bay

Six things AGM Contracting fits. Each one is measured on site first, because a gate only works as well as the opening it hangs in.

  • Automatic gates

    Driveway entry

    Driveway gates with the motor, control box and safety gear matched to the weight and the run of the gate — swinging or sliding, in steel, aluminium or timber to suit the front of the property.

  • Sliding gates

    Tight entries

    Where the driveway has no room to swing a gate in, the track and frame are set up to run it sideways instead, with the opening measured so the gate clears the fence line once it is open.

  • Colorbond fencing

    Boundary line

    Panels, posts and rails set to a string line so the run stays straight and the sheets finish level along the top, which is what a neighbouring property sees from their side.

  • Timber fencing

    Plank and rail

    Vertical plank paling fences and post-and-rail runs, with posts set to depth so the fence does not lean out of plumb the first time the ground moves.

  • Gate automation & repair

    Back on the button

    Motors, remotes, safety beams and hinges looked at when a gate stops answering — a gate that has dropped on its hinge or dragged on a track is usually a fit and adjustment job, not a new gate.

  • Pool fencing

    Around the pool

    Pool surrounds and the gate into them, measured around the existing paving and coping so the fence panels and the gate frame finish on the line you want.

When a gate stops working

Most driveway gates that stop opening are still good steel — the automation or the hanging has gone out of adjustment. That gets looked at before anybody quotes a replacement.

  • Motors, control boxes and remote handsets
  • Safety beams, stop plates and auto-reverse settings
  • Hinges, rollers, tracks and the gate catching on the ground
  • New automation fitted to a gate that is already hung

Questions we get

Do you supply the gate and the automation together?
Yes. Automatic gates and sliding gates are quoted as the gate, the hanging and the automation together, because the motor has to suit the weight and the travel of the gate it is driving.
Can you repair a gate rather than replace it?
Usually, yes. Automation repair on its own is a normal part of the week — motors, remotes, safety beams and gates that have dropped or started dragging get looked at before anyone prices a new gate.
Colorbond or timber — how do we choose?
Colorbond where the fence needs to hold a long straight line and a consistent finish; timber where the look of the house suits plank or post-and-rail. Both are quoted, so you can see the two numbers side by side.
Do you do pool fencing?
Yes. Pool fencing is measured around the existing paving and coping, and the gate into the pool area is hung so it sits level and swings clear.
How is the fence set out?
The line is run out on the ground first and the posts are placed to it, rather than the fence following the lie of the ground. That is what keeps the top of a run level and the fence standing plumb.
How do you quote?
From the site. A gate has to be measured once the opening is there and the ground around it is clear, so the first step is a time to look at the job and price it properly.
