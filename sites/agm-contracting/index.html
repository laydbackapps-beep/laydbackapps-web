Automation

From measure to first press

Gate automation is a sequence, not a part number. This is the order the work happens in, whether the gate is new or the operator is going onto something that is already hanging.

  1. Measure

    Set out the opening

    Opening width, gate travel, where the operator sits and the ground the gate runs on, before the gate is fabricated.

  2. Fabricate

    Build to that measurement

    Gate frame and infill built to the measured opening in the workshop, then brought out to site.

  3. Hang

    Fix, hang, level

    Posts set, track or hinges fixed, gate hung and levelled so it runs without drag.

  4. Program

    Set limits, hand over

    Travel limits set, then the gate is cycled and walked through with you before we leave.

Questions

The practical ones

If your question is not here, call and describe the job. Photos help more than a paragraph.

Do you build the gate as well as automate it?

Yes. Sliding and swinging gates are fabricated to the measured opening, then the automation is designed, installed and programmed as part of that build. That keeps the gate, the hardware and the control settings matched.

What fencing do you install?

Rural, paling, picket, Colorbond and post-and-rail. New runs, replacement sections and repairs.

Can you handle the earthworks as well?

Yes. Excavation and concreting for post holes, gate pads and the ground works around the fence line are part of what AGM Contracting does, so they can sit in the same contract as the fencing.

Can you repair a gate that is already up?

On-site welding repairs, fabrication and maintenance on existing gates and fencing are part of the service. Send a photo or describe the fault when you call and we can tell you what the job involves.

Do you work outside the North West Coast?

Yes. Hobart and the rest of Tasmania are covered by arrangement, scheduled around existing work on the coast.
