Privacy Policy

This policy covers the information Supreme Fencing & Gate Solutions collects on this website.

What we collect

Supreme Fencing & Gate Solutions collects the contact details you submit through the quote form — typically your name, phone number, email address and a description of the fencing and gates work you need.

Why we collect it

Only to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote and arrange the work. We do not sell, rent or trade your information.

Who we share it with

We do not share your details with third parties for marketing. Information may be disclosed where required by law.

Storage

Enquiries are stored securely and kept only as long as needed for the quote and any resulting work, or as required by law.

Contact

Quote requests can be sent through the form on the main page and we will come back to you.
