Privacy

Plain English. This page covers the quote form on the AGM Contracting site.

What is collected

Only what you type into the quote form: your name, phone number, and, if you add them, your email address, your suburb, the kind of gate or fencing job you have and the message you write.

Why it is collected

So the job can be answered — a time arranged to measure up a gate or price a fence run. Nothing else.

Who sees it

AGM Contracting. The details are sent straight to the business through the site's quote form. They are not sold, rented or passed on to anyone else.

How long it is kept

For as long as the enquiry is live. Once it is finished with, it is deleted.

Your choice

You can ask to see what has been kept, have it corrected, or have it removed at any time. Call 0437 919 649 or say so in your next message.

Fonts and tracking

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. The page loads a font file from Google Fonts, so Google sees the request for that file when the page opens.

What this site is

A page describing the gates and fencing AGM Contracting fits. Nothing on it is a quote — gates and fences are measured on site first.
