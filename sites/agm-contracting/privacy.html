Privacy

Privacy policy · last updated 19 November 2026

Short version: this site collects nothing. Longer version below.

What this site stores

Nothing. There are no analytics scripts, no advertising pixels, no cookies and no tracking of any kind on this site.

The quote form

The form on the quote section does not send data to a server. It opens your own email application with the details filled in, and the email leaves from your own account to aaron@agmcontracting.com.au.

Email and phone

If you call or email AGM Contracting, the details you give are used to quote the job and arrange the work. Contact details and job notes are kept in the company email account. They are not sold, rented or shared with anyone else.

Photos and plans

Photos or documents you send are used to assess and quote the job. Ask and they will be deleted once the job is quoted or completed.

External links

Links to Facebook open a third party site with its own privacy policy. AGM Contracting does not control what that site collects.

Questions or deletion

Call 0437 919 649 or email aaron@agmcontracting.com.au to ask what has been kept about you, or to have it deleted.
