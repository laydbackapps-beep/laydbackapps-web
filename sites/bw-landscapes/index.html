About

Gardens that are planted to stay

BW Landscapes works on soft landscaping: garden beds cut in, soil turned over and improved, turf laid on prepared ground, retaining built to hold the levels and planting set out to fill in as it grows.

The order matters more than the finish. Levels and drainage come first, then the beds and edging, and the planting goes in last so nothing gets walked over twice.

  • Garden beds, planting and mulching
  • Turf laid on prepared, levelled ground
  • Retaining walls that hold the levels
  • Paths, stepping stones and gravel areas
  • Gardens built in one run, start to finish

Process

How a garden build runs

Set the levels, build the bones, then plant it.

  1. Walk & plan

    Talk through the garden, the levels and how the space gets used.

  2. Levels & soil

    Beds cut in, drainage sorted and the soil improved before planting.

  3. Build & plant

    Edging, retaining and paths built, then the plants set out and planted.

  4. Turf & mulch

    Turf laid and butted tight, mulch spread and the garden watered in.
