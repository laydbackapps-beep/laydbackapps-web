How a fence runs

Measured, quoted, built, cleaned up.

Four steps, and you know the price and the line before the first post goes in.

STEP 01

Tell us the run

Length, height, material and the access. A rough measure is enough for a starting conversation.

STEP 02

Firm quote

Measured, priced and written down, including the ground works if the site needs them.

STEP 03

Set out and build

Line set, posts set to depth, panels strained — clean lines and square corners throughout.

STEP 04

Clean up

Old fencing removed, spoil taken away, the site left tidy for the next trade or the kids.
