Method

Four stages, in order — the sequence is what keeps the levels true.

Stage 01

Walk the block

Access, levels and services checked before a rate is given.

Stage 02

Set out

Levels marked and the cut planned to keep the spoil where it is useful.

Stage 03

Cut & cart

Excavated to level, spoil removed or placed, base compacted.

Stage 04

Hand over

Batters trimmed, tracks swept, ready for the concreter or builder.
