How a cut runs

Four stages, in order. The sequence is what keeps the levels true and stops rework on the day the concreter arrives.

Stage 01

Walk the block

Access, fall, rock and services checked on site before a rate is quoted.

Stage 02

Set out

Levels marked and the cut planned so useful spoil stays on site and waste leaves.

Stage 03

Cut, drain, compact

Excavated to level, drainage run, base compacted in layers.

Stage 04

Hand over

Batters trimmed, tracks swept, levels marked for the concreter or builder.

On site

Cutting, carting and bringing levels up on sloped residential blocks.

The work

Mundaring Civil works residential blocks and small civil sites. When the ground is rock and the slope is steep, the plan changes on the day — we quote to the block as it is, then hold the levels.

Quotes cover the machine, the cartage and the compaction. Those are the three items that otherwise turn up later as extras, so they are priced in from the start.
