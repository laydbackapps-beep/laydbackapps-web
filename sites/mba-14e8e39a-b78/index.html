About

Built on the Central Coast

Jon Yates Carpentry operates from Chittaway Bay across the Central Coast region. Every project — whether a backyard deck, a structural renovation, or a timber repair — runs on the same principle: honest materials, precise execution, no shortcuts.

We work in spotted gum, ironbark, treated pine, and engineered timber systems. Decks and pergolas detailed for coastal exposure. Renovations that respect the existing structure while making it work for how you live now.
