T.01 — THE WORK

Groundworks and concrete, done in the right order.

Every job starts with the ground and ends with the finish. The steps in between are where a job is won or lost.

Site preparation

Clearing, levelling and boxing up a site so the pour starts on solid, even ground.

Excavation & earthworks

Cut, fill and grade — moving dirt until the falls and levels read right.

Slabs & foundations

Formed, reinforced and poured slabs laid square and flat, ready for what goes on top.

Driveways & hardstands

Concrete drives and heavy-duty areas poured to a thickness that suits the traffic.

Paths, edging & aprons

Footpaths, crossovers and edges that tie the concrete into the rest of the site.

Finishing & curing

Broom, trowel or saw-cut finishes — cured properly so the surface lasts.
