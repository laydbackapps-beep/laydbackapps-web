How a repair call-out works

Most faults can be identified on site — you get the diagnosis before any parts are ordered.

  1. 01

    Log the fault

    Tell us the inverter brand, the fault code if you have one, and when the system last worked properly.

  2. 02

    Test and diagnose

    We test the array, the isolators, the inverter and the battery, and tell you what has actually failed.

  3. 03

    Repair and report

    Repairs completed and the system re-tested under load, with the results explained in plain English.

Solar repair questions

Straight answers before we come out.

My inverter is showing a fault code. What now?

Write the code down or photograph the screen. Most codes point to an earth fault, a grid disturbance, an overtemperature condition or a failed string — the code tells us where to start testing.

Do you repair the inverter or replace it?

It depends on the fault, the age of the unit and whether parts are still available. If a repair is not sensible, we will say so and quote a replacement instead.

Can you work on a system you did not install?

Yes. We work on existing systems regardless of who installed them, including systems that are out of warranty.

Do you service battery storage?

Yes — battery systems and their inverters. Batteries that are not charging, not holding charge or regularly shutting down are tested and repaired or replaced.

What area do you cover?

Perth and the surrounding metropolitan suburbs. Send through your suburb and we will confirm the earliest available time.
