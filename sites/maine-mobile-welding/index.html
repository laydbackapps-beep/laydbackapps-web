Who turns up

One welder, one rig, straight answers

Maine Mobile Welding is a one-welder mobile service based at Wamuran. That means you deal with the same person from the first phone call to the last bead: the one who looks at the job, prices it and does the welding.

Jobs get quoted off the actual steel, not off a photo and a guess. If a repair is not worth doing, you will be told that instead of being sold a new gate. If it is a quick fix, it stays a quick fix.

Work is welded to hold: dressed back where it shows, braced where it carries load, and left tidy enough that the next person who looks at it cannot tell where the repair starts.

Questions

Before you ring

Do you come to me, or do I bring the job in?

Everything on this page is a mobile job. The welder, leads and gas go on the ute and get rigged up at your place, whether that is a farm gate at the front boundary or a shed at the back of the block. There is no workshop to drop anything off to.

What do you need on site?

Room to get the ute close, and a power point within reach of the job. Where power is not available, the welder runs off the ute. Steel that is going to be welded needs to be clean, dry and clear of anything that will catch a spark, so the area around the joint gets swept back first.

Can you weld steel that is already standing or bolted down?

Yes. Structural steel, shed columns, roof members, gate posts and frames are commonly welded in place. Anything carrying load gets braced before it is cut, and the weld is sized to what it is holding up rather than to what looks neat.

How quickly can you get out?

Booked work is usually scheduled within a few days. Breakdowns on a farm or a trailer that cannot move get priority, and weekend or early-morning call-outs are fine when something has to be back working.

What does a repair cost?

Small gate and trailer repairs are quoted as a fixed price once the damage is seen. Larger fabrication or structural jobs are quoted per job, and where the damage is hidden until the joint is opened up, it is done at an hourly rate agreed before the welder starts.

Which areas do you cover?

Wamuran and the surrounding areas. If you are just outside that, send the details through anyway and you will get a straight answer on whether the trip is worth it.
