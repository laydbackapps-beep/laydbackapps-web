Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

What we collect

Maine Mobile Welding collects the details you send through the enquiry form on this site — typically your name, phone number, email address, the type of job and a description of the work you need. If you phone or text instead, we keep the same details.

Why we collect it

Only to reply to your enquiry, work out a quote and arrange the job. We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not add you to any marketing list.

Who we share it with

We do not share your details with third parties for marketing. Information may be disclosed where required by law.

Storage

Enquiries are stored securely and kept only as long as needed to quote the work and complete any resulting job, or as long as the law requires.

Photos of your site

Photos you send to help us quote a job are used for that job only. Images of your property or equipment are not published without your permission.
