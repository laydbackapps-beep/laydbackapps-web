Privacy

This page explains what happens to the details you send through the quote form on this site.

What is collected

  • Your name, phone number, email address and the message you write
  • The job you describe in the form

Why

Only to answer your enquiry and prepare a quote. Nothing else.

Who sees it

The enquiry is sent to DC Tiling. It is not sold and it is not passed to advertisers.

Keeping it

Enquiry details are kept while the job is current, and for a reasonable period afterwards so past work can be referenced.

Your choices

Ask for a copy, or ask for it to be deleted, by calling DC Tiling on 0402 460 362.
