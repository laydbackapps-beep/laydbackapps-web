From measure-up to clean-down

Three things decide how a tiling job turns out.

  1. Measure and set out

    The space is measured, levels checked and the set-out planned before any tile is fixed, so the grout lines run true across the room.

  2. Prep and waterproof

    Surfaces are prepared and falls and waterproofing are sorted first, because the finish depends on what sits underneath it.

  3. Lay, grout and clean

    Tiles are laid, cut in around fixtures, grouted and cleaned down, with the room left ready to use.
