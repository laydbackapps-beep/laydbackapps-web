Preparation is where a slab is won or lost

DC Concreting NSW pours residential and light commercial concrete across New South Wales. The crew handles it end to end, set out, excavation, steel, formwork, pour and finish, so there is one point of contact from the first cut to the day you drive on it.

Get the base right and the slab lasts: levels checked, steel placed properly, falls set so water leaves the job, then a finish that suits how the surface gets used.

  • Free consultation, no obligation, just quality advice
  • Set out, excavation, steel and formwork in house
  • Falls and control joints planned, not improvised
  • Plain, broomed and exposed aggregate finishes
  • Residential and light commercial work
