Repaints and garage floors, Sunshine Coast wide

Domestic Repaint Services repaints homes across the Sunshine Coast, inside and out, and coats garage floors with epoxy. Preparation is the job: surfaces cleaned up, patched and sanded before a brush is picked up, then an even finish with clean lines.

Because garages and utility floors take a beating, that work gets its own approach: grinding back the old surface, filling the cracks and cutting in a proper epoxy system rather than a thin coat over the top.

  • Interior and exterior house repaints
  • Garage floor repaints and epoxy flooring
  • Preparation, patching and sanding done first
  • Clean cut-in lines on trims and ceilings
  • Rooms and furniture protected while we work
