04

Asked on every project.

How much does a new home build cost?
Every project is different, depending on size, specification and site. We provide firm written quotes after a site assessment, so you’ll know exactly where you stand.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes — TES Build holds full licensing and insurance cover. Our licence number and certificate of currency are available on request, whenever you need them.
Which areas do you cover?
Newcastle and surrounds — happy to quote further afield for the right project.
How long does a typical build take?
For a standard single-storey home, expect 6-9 months from slab to handover. Weather, approvals and selected finishes all influence that timeframe.
