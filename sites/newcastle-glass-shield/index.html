Surfaces

What the coating goes on

The same protection, applied differently depending on how the glass is used and how much water it sees.

Showers

Shower screens & glass

Hard water leaves mineral deposits that etch into untreated glass and go cloudy for good. Coated glass sheds the water before it dries on, which is where most of the scum comes from.

Best for
Frameless and semi-frameless screens, fixed panels, doors
Result
Wipe down instead of a chemical scrub
Balustrades

Balustrades & pool fencing

Outdoor glass takes salt, pool splash and rain on both faces. Coating it means fewer water marks and no mineral ring along the base where it meets the tiles.

Best for
Frameless balustrades, glass pool fencing, stair panels
Result
Rain mostly self-cleans the face
Windows

Windows, splashbacks & mirrors

Windows on the weather side get spots and grime that take a ladder and a lot of squeegee. Coated glass holds less of it, so the clean takes a fraction of the time.

Best for
Full window runs, kitchen splashbacks, bathroom mirrors
Result
Fewer spots, longer between cleans
Restoration

Glass that has already gone cloudy

If a screen has years of built-up scale, coating over it locks the mess in. We cut the deposit back first, then coat the clean glass so the finish is uniform.

Best for
Screens with water staining or hard-water scale
Result
A clear, even surface before the coat goes on
How it works

Four stages, one visit

01

Clean

Glass is cleaned from the edge out with a pH-neutral system. Anything bonded — scale, silicone smears, old product — comes off now, because whatever is left under the coating stays there.

02

Activate

The surface is primed so the coating can bond to the glass rather than sitting on top of it. This is the step most DIY kits skip and the reason most DIY coatings fail within a year.

03

Apply

The protection is applied in a controlled film and worked across the full face — corners, edges and the frame line included, not just the middle of the panel.

04

Cure

It's left to cure before it gets wet. After that it's permanent on the glass and you clean it normally; nothing needs re-applying on a schedule.

Aftercare

What you do after we leave

Let it cure

Keep the coated glass dry for the cure window we give you on the day. After that it's business as usual.

Clean it normally

Soap, a microfibre cloth and water. No abrasive pads, no heavy-duty scale removers — they cut the coating back.

Nothing to re-apply

The coating stays on the glass. There's no annual treatment and no subscription — we do the job once and that's it.

Questions

Before you book

How long does the coating last?
It bonds to the glass and stays there. There's no re-application schedule — the one treatment is the job.
Can you do glass that's already cloudy?
Often, yes. We remove as much of the mineral scale as the glass will give back, then coat it. Badly etched glass can't be fully restored — we'll tell you honestly before you pay for anything.
Does the glass look different afterwards?
No. The coating is clear and around a micron thick. There's no tint, no film and no visible edge.
Is it only for showers?
No — balustrades, pool fencing, windows, splashbacks and mirrors all get the same protection, applied to suit the surface.
Do I need to be a new build?
No. Coating new glass is ideal, but existing screens are the more common job. We just clean and restore first.
