How the work runs.

Four steps, in order, on every job.

01

Measure on site

The room is measured and the setting out is worked out: where the lines land, and where the cuts go.

02

Strip & prepare

Old finishes out, substrate checked, screed and levelling done where the floor needs it.

03

Set out & tile

Lines marked, tiles laid from the centre out, spaced and levelled as they go.

04

Grout & finish

Grouted, silicone to the junctions, floor cleaned down and the site left tidy.

Questions.

What comes up most before a tiling job starts.

What areas do you cover?

Sydney, NSW: bathrooms, kitchens, laundries and floor areas across the city and the surrounding suburbs.

Do you do both floor and wall tiling?

Yes. Floor and wall tiling, plus bathroom and kitchen renovations that include the strip out and the prep.

What helps get an accurate quote?

Photos of the area, roughly how many square metres it is, and what is on the floor or wall now. The quote is then worked out on site.

Do you remove the old tiles first?

Old tiles are stripped as part of the renovation, then the substrate is checked and levelled before the new tiling starts.

Get a quote.

Send through the room, the rough area in square metres and a photo or two, or call and we will sort it out on site.
