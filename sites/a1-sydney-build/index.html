05

Questions we hear.

Do you handle waterproofing certification?
Yes - internal wet-area membranes to AS 3740. Certificate provided on completion for bathrooms, laundries, balconies and planter boxes.
Can you match existing tiles for a repair?
We source closest-match tiles where possible. If an exact match is unavailable we discuss options - feature band, contrast border, or full re-tile of the affected plane.
What tile formats do you work with?
Porcelain, ceramic, natural stone, large-format (up to 1200x2400), mosaic sheets, terrazzo-look and textured external-rated porcelain.
Do you coordinate other trades for renovations?
Yes - we manage plumbing, electrical, carpentry and joinery coordination for bathroom and kitchen renovations so the program runs in sequence.
How long does a bathroom renovation take?
Typically 2–3 weeks from strip-out to final clean for a standard main bathroom. Complex layouts, custom joins or supply lead times can extend this - we confirm the program in the quote.
Do you provide written quotes?
Always - a detailed scope and firm price before any work starts. No day-rate surprises.
