What we do

Carpentry & building, start to finish

01

fit-outs

Framing is set out square and to the plan, because everything that follows — cladding, windows, lining — inherits those tolerances.

02

renovations

Decks are built with the bearer and joist sizing the span needs, fixed with the right hardware for treated timber and checked for the balustrade height the code requires.

03

framing

Renovations are sequenced so the trades do not stand on each other, and the site is left secure and weatherproof at the end of each week.

04

decks

Small structural repairs — rotten bearers, sagging pergolas, failed lintels — are assessed before they are quoted, since the fix depends on what is found.

05

repairs

Work is priced from a site visit with a written scope, and variations are agreed in writing before they happen.

06

Doors & Windows
