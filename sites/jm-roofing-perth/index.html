Questions

What people ask before booking

How often should gutters be cleaned?

Around Perth, most homes need it once or twice a year — more often if you're under trees or near bush. Before the winter rains and again ahead of summer is the usual rhythm.

Do you clear the downpipes too?

Yes. Downpipes are flushed as part of the clean, because a clear gutter still overflows if the pipe below it is blocked.

Can you find and fix a roof leak?

That's a big part of what we do — tracking where the water is actually entering, then repairing the flashing, tile or gutter causing it.

Do you charge for quotes?

No — quotes are free. Tell us the address and what's happening and we'll price the job before any work starts.

Which areas do you cover?

All areas of Perth, north and south, and we also travel to country areas. We're based in Bassendean.

Do you clean solar panels as well?

Yes — solar panel cleaning is on the list, and we clear the gutters around the array while we're up there.
