The finish

Chosen for the job

The surface is picked for how the slab is used — a drive takes a different finish to an entry, and the colour has to sit with the house.

Before you book

Common questions

What sort of concreting do you take on?
Slabs, driveways, paths and patios, exposed aggregate and decorative finishes — residential and decorative concreting across the Surf Coast, Bellarine and Geelong.
How is a job quoted?
After the site is seen — access, the base, the fall and the finish all change the price, so a figure over the phone would not be honest.
Can you match concrete I already have?
Colour and texture are matched as closely as the age of the existing concrete allows. Older concrete weathers, so it is judged on site before quoting rather than promised from a chart.
How long before I can use a new slab?
Foot traffic is usually fine the next day, with cars generally waiting about a week and anything heavy longer than that. Cure times are left with you at handover.
Do you do exposed aggregate and decorative finishes?
Yes — exposed aggregate and decorative finishes are both part of the work, washed back or worked evenly so the finish reads consistently across the whole pour.
How soon can a job be started?
It depends on the run of jobs and the weather, which decides a lot in concreting. Ask and you will be given an honest window rather than a date that slips.
