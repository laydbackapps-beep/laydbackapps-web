What we pour

Four kinds of job, all of them set out and finished by hand. If yours is not here, ask — most of it is.

Driveways

Set out to the crossover, formed, poured and finished — plain, exposed aggregate or patterned, with the fall running away from the house.

Paths and patios

Narrow runs and small slabs where the levels matter more than the area — along a side path, around a clothesline, out to a shed.

Shed & extension slabs

Mesh laid, formwork squared and the slab poured to the footprint, ready for the build to sit on.

Aggregate & decorative

Aggregate exposed by washing the surface back, or a pattern set into it — decided with you before the truck is booked.

Formwork set, mesh down, the truck on site — then the finishing starts.
