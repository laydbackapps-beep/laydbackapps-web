What we do

Painting, decorating and the preparation that makes a finish last, for homes and businesses around Lake Macquarie.

Interior painting

Walls, ceilings, doors and trim. Floors and furniture covered, edges cut in by hand, coats rolled out even.

Exterior painting

Weatherboards, render, brick, fascia and eaves. Washed, scraped, sanded and primed before any topcoat.

Preparation & plaster repair

Patching, filling, sanding and gap sealing. The part of a repaint the finish actually depends on.

Doors, trim & detail

Skirtings, architraves, sills and front doors finished sharp, with hinges and hardware masked off.

Decks & fences

Hardwood, treated pine and composite cleaned back, then stained or oiled to take the weather.

Decorating & colour

Feature walls, wallpaper and colour selection, worked out against your rooms, your light and what's already there.

A local painter, not a call centre

Pinnacle Painting & Decorating paints and decorates homes and businesses around Lake Macquarie. Interiors, exteriors, trim, decks and fences — quoted on what the job actually needs, then done in the right order.

A repaint lives or dies in the preparation. So the washing, scraping, filling and sanding happen first, every time, and the topcoats only go on once the surface is ready for them. Furniture and floors get covered, and the site is left tidy at the end of each day.

  • Interior and exterior repaints
  • Prep, patching and sanding before any topcoat
  • Floors and furniture covered, site cleaned up
  • Straight answers on scope, colour and timing
