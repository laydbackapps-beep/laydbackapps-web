How we build

Five stages from set-out to handover

01

Set-out & slab

Profiles set, levels checked, termite treatment, reinforcement placed and slab poured to the engineer's spec.

02

Frame

Floor system, wall frames and roof trusses erected plumb and straight - all connections fixed to code.

03

Lockup

Roof on, windows and external doors fitted, cladding fixed, building wrapped - weather tight.

04

Fit-out

Internal linings, joinery, wet areas waterproofed and tiled, services rough-in completed.

05

Completion

Final fix, paint, hardware, certifier inspection, defects cleared and keys handed over.
