The job is usually easy to describe and harder to do: something big, close to something you'd rather keep. We work around the house, the shed, the fence and the cars.

Tree removal

Standing trees taken down whole, or section by section where there's no room to drop them.

Pruning

Storm cleanup

Stump grinding

Land clearing

Overgrown blocks and fence lines cleared back to something you can work with.

Green waste

Three things decide how a tree comes down.

None of them are the tree itself. They're what's around it, and what it's standing in.

01 / Lean

Which way it wants to go

Lean, weight distribution and dead limbs set the direction long before a saw starts. It's the first thing we look at, not the last.

02 / Drop zone

What's underneath it

Fences, sheds, cars, clotheslines, the neighbour's roof. Where the pieces land is the whole plan; everything else follows from it.

03 / Ground

What's under the surface

Roots, services, septic and slope. A stump doesn't come out clean if nobody checked what runs past it.
