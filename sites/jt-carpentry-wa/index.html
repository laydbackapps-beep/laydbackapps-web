Frames, decks and trim. One crew.

"All aspects of carpentry" means you're not chasing three trades for one job. The same hands that set the frame hang the doors and fit the trim.

01 / Frame

Framing & structural

Wall and roof framing for new work, extensions and alterations. Walls plumbed and braced before the sheeting goes near them.

02 / Deck

Decks & pergolas

Subframes set to fall, joists spaced to the board, decking laid with the gap running even around posts, steps and corners.

03 / Inside

Built-ins & cabinetry

Robes, shelving and bench seats scribed to the wall that's actually there — Perth homes are rarely square, and the fit should still look it.

04 / Trim

Doors, skirts & architraves

Doors hung to even gaps, skirts and arcs mitred tight, hardware set so the latch catches on the first push.

05 / Outside

Cladding & screening

Timber cladding, batten screens and fascia detailed so water runs off and the shadow lines stay straight across the run.

06 / Fix

Repairs & alterations

Rotten boards, sagging gates, sticking doors, a wall that has to move — the small jobs most carpenters won't drive out for.

Set out first. Cut once.

A carpenter's mistakes are all in the first hour. Get the line right and the rest of the job only has one way to go.

Plumb & level · set out

Measured to the house, not the plan.

Plans assume walls are straight. They're not. Every fit-out gets measured against the room as it stands, then the set out is marked and checked before anything is cut.

  • Lines plumbed and squared, then verified a second time before fixing
  • Treated timber outside, dressed stock inside — never swapped to save a trip
  • Screwed and plugged where it will be seen, nailed only where it won't
  • Dust and offcuts cleared at the end of each stage, not left for handover

How a job runs.

Three stages, and you always know which one you're in. No fortnight of silence between the quote and the first day on site.

Stage 01

Look & quote

Send photos or book a look. You get a written scope — what's included, what isn't, and what it costs.

Stage 02

Set out & build

Materials on site, set out marked and checked against the house, then the frame or subframe built true.

Stage 03

Finish & hand over

Trim, hardware, final fix, then a walk-through with you before the tools go back in the ute.
