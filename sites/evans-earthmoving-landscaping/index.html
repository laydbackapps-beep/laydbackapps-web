What we do

Earthmoving and landscaping, start to finish

One crew on the machine and on the garden. That is why the levels, the drainage and the finish all match up when the job is handed over.

01

Site cuts and cut-and-fill

Stripping, cutting and filling a site to the level it needs to be. Get the base right and the driveway, the slab prep and the drainage all sit where they should.

02

Driveway construction

Gravel and compacted driveway build-ups, shaped and drained so the water runs off the surface and away from the shed, the house and the gate.

03

Dam and trench work

Dam work and trenching for water, power and drainage runs, dug to the depth and fall the job needs and cleaned up behind the machine.

04

Land clearing

Clearing scrub, regrowth and rubbish timber off a block so the site is opened up and ready for the next stage of the build.

05

Retaining walls

Retaining walls built off a properly cut and compacted batter, with the drainage behind them handled, so the wall keeps holding the levels it was built for.

06

Turf and garden works

Turf laying, soil prep, garden beds and planting to finish a site off. Landscaping done by the same crew that moved the dirt.

About us

A family business that runs both sides of the job

Evans Earthmoving & Landscaping is a family owned and operated business based at Dalwood in the Hunter. We take on the earthmoving and the landscaping as one job, so the site cut, the cut-and-fill, the drainage and the finished garden are all worked out together instead of being handed between trades.

Every job starts with a look at the block: how it falls, where the water has to go, and what the finished surface needs to be. From there it is machines for the dirt, then turf and garden works for the finish.

The phone is answered by the people who do the work, so you get a straight answer on what the job needs and what it will take.

Questions

Common questions

Do you do the earthmoving and the landscaping, or just one of them?

Both. Site cuts, driveways, dam and trench work, land clearing and retaining walls are the earthmoving side; turf and garden works finish the job off. Working across both means the levels we cut are the levels the landscaping is built on.

Where do you work out of?

We are based at Dalwood NSW 2335 and take on work on properties in the surrounding district.

Are you licensed?

Yes. NSW licence 463778C.

How do I get a price?

Ring 0411 863 721 or send the quote form on this page. Tell us the block, what you want done and roughly when, and we will come back to you with a price.

Do you take on clearing and cut-and-fill before a build?

Yes. Clearing scrub and regrowth, stripping and cutting the site and building the levels up where they need to be is the sort of work we do to get a block ready for the next stage.
