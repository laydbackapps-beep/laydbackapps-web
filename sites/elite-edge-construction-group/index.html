01

Measure and quote

Plans or a site measure, then a scope written down: what is included, what is excluded, and what the allowance is for anything unknown behind a wall.

02

Set out and frame

Levels taken, set out squared, framing and bracing installed, then inspected before anything is closed in.

03

Trades in sequence

Plumbing, electrical and waterproofing booked in the right order so linings and finishes go on after the rough-ins, not before.

04

Fit-out

Linings, cornice, architraves, doors and hardware hung and adjusted, then the site cleaned down.

05

Hand-over

Walk the job together, list anything still outstanding, and come back to finish it rather than leaving it as a promise.

Scope

What we build

Renovations, extensions and carpentry across Melbourne — from a single wall moved through to a full fit-out.

Extensions & additions

New rooms cut into the existing house: floor and wall framing tied to the original structure, roof tie-in flashed properly, and the old and new subfloor brought to the same level.

Framing & structural carpentry

Floor, wall and roof framing set out square and plumbed, bracing installed, beam and lintel work detailed to the engineer's plan, and the frame inspected before it is closed.

Renovations

Kitchens, bathrooms and laundries stripped back to the studs, framing corrected where an old house has moved, then linings, sheeting and fit-out done so waterproofing and tiling land square.

Decks, pergolas & carports

Bearers, joists and stumps sized to the span, spacing set for the decking board, and fixings chosen for treated timber or steel so nothing bleeds or backs out.

Doors, windows & cladding

Openings cut and framed, sills and flashings set with the falls right, and cladding or weatherboards set out evenly so the joints land where they should.

Fit-out & fix-out

Linings, cornice, skirting, architraves, doors hung and adjusted, and cabinetry installed — the stage where a square frame starts to show.
