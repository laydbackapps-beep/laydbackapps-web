Questions

Asked before a quote

Can you work from architectural and engineering drawings?
Yes — both. Plans, structural details and specifications are read together, and the quote is built against the items on them rather than a rough figure per square metre.
Do you coordinate the other trades on a renovation?
On full renovations, yes. Plumbing, electrical, tiling and finishing trades are sequenced and supervised so one trade is not undoing the work of another, and you are not chasing four people to find out the schedule.
How do you handle surprises found during strip-out?
Every renovation hides something. When it turns up, it is photographed, explained and priced before the work continues, so you make the call rather than discovering it on the invoice.
Do you do decks and pergolas on their own?
Yes. A deck is a proper structural job — bearers, joists, fixings and fall all matter — and it is priced and built the same way as any other part of the structure.
Are you licensed and insured?
Elite Edge Construction Group Pty Ltd is a registered company (ABN 27 685 937 544) working in Victoria, carrying the licensing and insurance required for the work undertaken.
How do I get a price?
Send the scope — what you want done, the suburb, and any drawings if you have them. If plans are not drawn yet, the job can still be scoped from a walk-through and a description of what you are after.
Do you take on smaller repair jobs?
Yes. Rot, water damage and failed fixings are worth fixing properly at the cause, and a small job done right saves a large one later.
