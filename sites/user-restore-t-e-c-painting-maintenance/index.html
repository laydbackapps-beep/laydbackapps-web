What we take on

One scope from repair to clean down — holes filled and set, edges sanded, masking on, coats rolled and laid off, tape pulled while the paint is still green.

Interior repaints

Walls and ceilings patched, sanded and coated — low sheen on the broad areas, washable where hands and steam land.

Exterior repaints

Weatherboards, render, eaves and fascias scraped back where they have let go, primed, then coated to take the weather.

Trims, doors & skirts

Architraves, skirting and doors sanded between coats and finished in enamel so they wipe clean instead of scuffing.

How a job runs

Before you book

Do you quote the whole job or price per room?
Either. Most jobs get one price for the full scope — prep through clean down — so nothing arrives as a surprise mid-way.
Can you repair damaged walls too?
Yes. Holes, cracks and loose sections are filled and set before painting, which is usually why a repaint looks right afterwards.
Do you do exterior work?
Yes — weatherboards, render, eaves and fascias are prepped and coated for the weather they will get.
