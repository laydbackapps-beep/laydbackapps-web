What gets rendered

Four jobs cover nearly every wall we are called to. If yours is not listed, describe it in the quote form.

Cement rendering

Sand and cement over brick or block, ruled, floated and sponged to the finish you pick, with corners set before the float goes on.

Acrylic rendering

Pre-bagged acrylic over blueboard or foam, meshed and cut in tight at reveals, sills and control joints.

Texture coating

Rolled and sprayed texture coats, applied evenly so the wall still reads flat in raking afternoon light.

Remedial re-render

Cracked, drummy or blown render cut back to sound substrate and patched, rather than skimmed over the top of the failure.
