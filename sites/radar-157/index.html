Finishes

Decorative, aggregate or colour

Three ways to finish a slab — and what each one gives you.

Decorative

Stamped and stencilled patterns pressed into the slab while it is green, then sealed once it has cured.

Exposed aggregate

The top paste washed back to reveal the stone underneath — a hard-wearing, non-slip surface.

Colour

Oxide and through-mixed colour so the tone runs through the concrete rather than sitting on top of it.

Before you book

Do you do exposed aggregate?

Yes — washed-back aggregate is one of our main finishes, along with stamped and stencilled pattern work.

Is the colour mixed through the concrete?

Both are possible, but through-mixed and oxide colour is how the tone is carried in the slab itself rather than applied over the top.

Do you pour slabs and shed floors?

We do — house, shed and structural slabs, set out, formed, reinforced and poured to level.
