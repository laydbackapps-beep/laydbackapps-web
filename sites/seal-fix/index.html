What sits under the tile.

A wet area is a stack of layers, and every one of them has a job. Skip a layer and the leak moves instead of stopping.

  1. 01Substrate cleaned back and checked for moisture
  2. 02Screed laid to fall, so water reaches the waste
  3. 03Primer, matched to the substrate
  4. 04Bond breaker and fillet at the wall to floor junction
  5. 05Membrane, lapped and turned up the wall
  6. 06Flood test and documented stages before any tile
  7. 07Adhesive and tile, laid to tolerance, then grout and seal

The membrane is the part nobody sees again. It gets the time.
