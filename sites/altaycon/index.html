What we do

Five scopes, one crew. Each stage is set out to line, built to tolerance and handed over ready for the next trade.

  • 01

    Structural and civil works

    Site works from set out through to structure: excavation, subgrade preparation, drainage and retaining, built to the drawings.

  • 02

    Slabs and footings

    Strip and pad footings, raft slabs and suspended slabs: formwork, reinforcement, placement and finishing.

  • 03

    Framing

    Timber and steel framing, floor and wall structure, set out square and braced for the trades that follow.

  • 04

    Fit-out

    Linings, partitions, fix-out and the finishing detail that gets a space ready for handover.

  • 05

    Project delivery

    One point of contact on site: sequencing, trades, staged delivery and sign-off to the program.

How a job runs

The order is fixed, because everything after it depends on what is under it being right.

Plan and set out

Drawings reviewed, levels confirmed and the site set out before anything is dug or poured.

Form and reinforce

Footings and slabs formed to tolerance, reinforcement placed and the pour checked before it starts.

Frame and build

Structure and framing carried up square and braced, ready for services and linings.

Fit out and hand over

Linings, fix-out and finishing done, the site cleared and the stage handed over.
