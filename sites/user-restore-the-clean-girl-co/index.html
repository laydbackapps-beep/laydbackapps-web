What gets done in each room

The standard every visit is held to, room by room — the bench, the grout, the skirting, and the spots that only show up when the light hits them.

Kitchen

Benchtops and splashback degreased · sink and draining board scrubbed · stovetop and range hood wiped · cupboard fronts done · bin emptied and relined.

Bathroom and ensuite

Shower screen and tiles, grout line scrubbed · vanity and toilet inside and out · mirrors polished streak-free · chrome dried down rather than left spotted.

Living and bedrooms

Before you book

What people ask before the first clean

Anything not here goes in the booking form — it comes back answered with your price, in the same reply.

Do I need to be home?
No. Plenty of cleans happen while you are at work — you can leave access instructions when you book and come home to it done.
Do I need to supply anything?
Everything needed to clean comes with the visit. If you would rather a specific product be used on a surface, say so when booking.
What about pets?
Fine — just mention them when you book so doors and gates are handled the way you want.
How far do you travel?
Based in Hamlyn Terrace, working across the Central Coast. Send the suburb with your booking and it will be confirmed before the visit.
Can I get a price first?
Yes. Send the suburb, the number of bedrooms and bathrooms, and whether it is a regular or a deep clean, and you will get a price back before anything is locked in.
