About O'Hara's Fencing

O'Hara's Fencing is a local fencing contractor operating across Jewells NSW and the wider Lake Macquarie area. We work with timber, steel, and aluminium to build and repair fences and gates for residential and commercial clients.

Every job starts with a site measure and a clear quote . Materials are sourced from Australian suppliers and installed to current standards. We carry public liability insurance and hold a current NSW contractor licence.
