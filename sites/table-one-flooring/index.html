How an install runs

Four stages. The first one is the reason the other three hold.

Stage 01

Measure and check

Rooms measured, door heights and skirting checked, and the slab tested for flat and moisture.

Stage 02

Prepare the subfloor

Grinding, patching and levelling so the boards sit flat without hollow spots.

Stage 03

Acclimatise and lay

Boards rested in the room, then laid to the longest run with expansion at the walls.

Stage 04

Trim and hand over

Scotia, thresholds and nosing fitted, the floor cleaned and the old flooring removed.

About the work

Floors fail at the edges and under the joins, so the measuring, the acclimatising and the subfloor get as much attention as the boards.

Quotes cover supply, underlay, trim and removal of the old floor where needed, so the price at the end matches the price quoted.
