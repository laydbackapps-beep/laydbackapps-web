What we do

The job is walked, explained and priced before anything starts.

Walls and ceilings

Rolled out evenly with the edges cut in by brush, furniture moved to the centre and covered before a drop sheet goes down.

Weatherboard exteriors

Boards washed, nails punched, gaps filled and the whole elevation primed before the first topcoat goes near it.

Trim and doors

Skirtings, architraves and doors sanded between coats so the finish is smooth to the hand, not just from a metre away.

Rendered surfaces

Texture evened out where it has crazed, then sealed and coated so it stops sucking the paint in.

Colour changes

Dark to light, light to dark — the number of coats that change actually needs is worked out before the job is priced.

Questions

Do you work weekends?
Saturdays are usually available. Sundays are kept for curing coats and weather catch-up.
Can you match paint already on the walls?
Yes, from a sample or a tin code, matched before the job starts.
What about lead paint on older homes?
Older weatherboards are tested where there is a suspicion, and the prep method changes accordingly.
