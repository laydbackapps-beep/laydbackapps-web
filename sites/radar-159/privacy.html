Privacy

Updated November 2026

What is collected

Only what you send through the enquiry form or by phone and email: your name, phone number, suburb and a description of the work you need. Site photos you send are used to price the job.

Why

To reply to your enquiry, quote the work, and arrange access if the job goes ahead. Nothing else.

Who sees it

Ace Construction Services, and nobody else for marketing. Details are not sold, rented or traded. Information is disclosed only where the law requires it.

Storage

Enquiries are held securely and kept for as long as needed to quote and complete the work, or as long as the law requires. Ask and your details are deleted.
