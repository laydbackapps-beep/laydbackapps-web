Quotes

Rates are quoted from the site, because access, levels and what sits under the surface change from block to block. A figure given over the phone is an indication only until the block has been seen.

Underground services

Service locations are to be confirmed and marked before work starts. Where a service is not marked, or sits outside the marked area, the customer is responsible for the outcome and any repair.

Spoil and fill

Whether spoil leaves site, stays on site, or is replaced with imported fill is agreed before the machine arrives. Carting is charged on the volume removed.

Access and weather

The block must be accessible for the machine and truck. Work can be delayed by rain or ground conditions that make the site unsafe to work or drive on, and start dates move accordingly.

Payment

Terms for deposit, progress and final payment are set out on the quote for each job.
