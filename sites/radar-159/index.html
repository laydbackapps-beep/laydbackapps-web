How a job runs

Four steps, same order every time. Nothing is ordered or dug until the access has been walked and the levels are agreed.

1 · Walk the block

Gate width, overhead lines, slope and where the spoil can sit. This is where most of the surprises get found, not on the day the machine arrives.

2 · Set out and confirm levels

String lines and a laser level, checked against the plan, before a bucket goes in the ground.

3 · Dig, trim and compact

Cut to level, trim the batters, compact in lifts where it is load-bearing.

4 · Clean up and hand over

Spoil gone, access reinstated, and a walk-through of what was done and what comes next.

Questions we get asked

The things that come up most often on Perth residential jobs.

Do you need to see the site to quote?

Yes for anything with levels or access involved. Photos help, but the quote is only reliable once the block has been walked.

Can you work around an existing house?

Yes, provided the access is there. Narrow side access changes the machine, not the job.

Who takes the spoil?

We do. Load counts and tip fees are agreed before the trucks start so there is no surprise at the end.

Do you work with builders?

Regularly. Set-out is taken from the engineer's plan and the levels are confirmed before the pour date is locked in.

Send the site details

Suburb, what needs doing and roughly when. If there is a plan or a photo of the access, mention it and we will ask for it.
