How a cut gets to level

Three stages, and the block is left the way the next trade needs to receive it.

A

Walk the block

Access, services, and the finished levels are checked on site. Levels are read from the survey pegs or the engineer's plan.

B

Cut and trim

Ground taken down to the mark, batters cut back at a grade that holds, and the edges cleaned up as the machine goes.

C

Cart and hand over

Spoil and sand trucked out, tracks swept, and the block left ready for the concreter or the builder.
