Services — what gets built

A boundary is quiet work. It keeps the dogs in, the weather out and the neighbourly negotiations short. This is fencing kept simple: steel and timber, gates that swing the way they should, and repairs that treat a leaning run of fence as a fix rather than a funeral.

Everything below is quoted on the job in front of us — a suburban boundary in Toronto reads differently to a long acreage run out toward Newcastle, and the quote reflects the ground it has to stand in.

  • Colorbond fencing

    Steel panel fencing for boundaries and side access, in the standard colour range.

  • Timber fencing

    Traditional timber fencing for front boundaries, backyards and acreage lines.

  • Gates

    Gates to suit new or existing fencing, sized for pedestrian and driveway access.

  • Repairs

    Repairs to leaning, loose or storm-damaged fencing — fixed panel by panel where the rest still has life in it.
