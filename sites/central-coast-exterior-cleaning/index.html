Our Services

Specialised exterior cleaning for every surface - from delicate render to heavy concrete.

House & Building Wash

Low-pressure soft wash for render, brick, weatherboard, and painted surfaces - removes mould, algae, and grime without damage.

Roof Cleaning

Non-abrasive treatment for tile, metal, and Colorbond roofs - kills organic growth at the source and prevents return.

Driveway & Path Pressure Clean

High-pressure surface cleaning for concrete, pavers, exposed aggregate, and stone - restores original colour and slip resistance.

Gutter Clearing

Vacuum and manual clearing for residential and commercial gutters - prevents water damage and overflow.

Commercial & Strata

Scheduled maintenance programs for apartments, offices, shopping centres, and industrial sites - compliant and reliable.

Deck & Fence Restoration

Gentle cleaning and prep for timber, composite, and coated surfaces - ready for oil, stain, or sealant.

Our Approach

A consistent process - every job, every surface.

Assess

On-site inspection to identify surface type, contamination, and access.

Prepare

Protect plants, furnishings, and adjacent areas; set up containment where needed.

Treat

Apply the correct chemistry, soft wash for organics, pressure for hard surfaces.

Rinse & Verify

Thorough rinse, final walk-through, and before/after documentation.
