What we do

Four finishes, one set of hands

Render holds a house together and plaster gives it a face. Both come down to prep, mix and patience — so nothing here gets rushed, and nothing gets handed to a crew you have never met.

No. 01

Cement render

Traditional and acrylic render systems over brick, block and blueboard. Mesh, beads and scratch coat set right, then floated and finished for the sun, salt and driving rain along this stretch of coast.

No. 02

Venetian plaster

Italian polished plaster, built up in thin coats and burnished back for depth and a soft sheen. Interior feature walls and panels, worked by hand until the surface reads like stone.

Interior
No. 03

Microcement

Seamless thin-coat finish over floors, walls and wet areas. No grout lines to scrub, sealed against water, and it wraps an odd-shaped room in one continuous sheet.

Floors, wet areas
No. 04

Acrylic & texture coatings

Flexible acrylic and rolled texture over panels and brickwork, colour matched before a brush goes near the wall. Built for movement and the coastal weather cycle.

Exterior
No. 05

Repairs & patching

Cracked, drummy or water-damaged render cut back to something solid, keyed, patched and blended so the repair disappears into the wall around it.

Repair
No. 06

Repaints & recoats

A tired rendered facade brought back with prep, a proper membrane coat and a finish colour that suits the house — no cutting corners on the surface underneath.

Straight off the wall

You can see the prep in the finish

Render does not fail at the surface — it fails at the substrate. Beads, mesh, curing and set-out are the reason a wall still looks straight a decade later. So that is where the time goes.

01Set out, beads and masking before a mix is even made.
02Scratch coat keyed into the substrate and left to cure.
