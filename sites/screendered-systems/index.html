SHEET 04 / NOTES

Practice notes

SCRendered Systems carries out rendering and solid plastering on homes, additions and renovations: full renders, texture coats, foam systems, repairs and mouldings.

Rendering is a finish that has to sit flat and hold together, so the substrate, the beads and the mix are sorted before the first coat goes on. Get those right and the wall reads as one surface and stays that way.
