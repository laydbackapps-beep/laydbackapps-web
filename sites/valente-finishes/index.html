What we do

Rendering work for homes and businesses.

Cement rendering

External walls rendered by hand and ruled true, with the coat built up properly so it cures without the map-cracking a thin coat gets.

Acrylic rendering

Flexible acrylic render for walls that move — applied over the right base and finished to the texture you want.

Texture finishes

Float, sponge, bagged and trowelled finishes chosen to suit the house rather than whatever is quickest.

Rendered feature walls

Garden walls, entry walls and internal features rendered and finished so the surface itself is the detail.

Patch & repair

Damaged or drummy render cut back, re-rendered and blended into the existing wall instead of patched with filler.

Commercial rendering

Shopfronts, fences and small commercial walls rendered around trading hours and left clean.

How a job runs

From the first look to the final clean up.

Look at the wall

We check the substrate, the existing render and the access before quoting — what is under the coat decides how it lasts.

Prepare and bead

Loose render comes off, beads go on and the wall is set out so the lines run straight and the corners are sharp.

Render and finish

The coat is applied, ruled and finished to the chosen texture, then the site is cleaned down.

About Valente Finishes

Valente Finishes is a rendering business — cement and acrylic render, texture finishes and rendered feature walls for homes and businesses.

Render is only as good as the prep and the ruling: beads set straight, the coat built to thickness and cured properly is what keeps a wall looking flat years later instead of showing every stud.
