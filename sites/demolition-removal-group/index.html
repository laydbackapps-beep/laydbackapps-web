What we do

Seven things we do properly

Everything on a demolition job, from the first strip-out to the last bin off site, one crew, one scope.

Residential demolition

Houses, duplexes, garages and outbuildings brought down and cleared, including slabs and driveways.

Commercial demolition

Shopfronts, warehouses and office interiors stripped back to shell for the next fit-out.

Asbestos removal

Asbestos sheeting, fencing, lagging and offcuts removed and bagged as part of the job.

Rubbish removal

Demolition spoil, green waste and general rubbish loaded out and disposed of.

Land clearing

Trees, scrub and old fencing cleared so the block is ready to work on.

Pavement & floor removal

Concrete, asphalt, paving and tiled floors broken out and carted away.

Strip-out

Interiors taken back to frame and slab, services made safe and everything loose removed.

One crew for the whole demolition job

Demolition Removal Group handles residential and commercial demolition, asbestos removal and strip-outs across Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane. The scope covers the strip-out, the material coming out and the site being left clear.
