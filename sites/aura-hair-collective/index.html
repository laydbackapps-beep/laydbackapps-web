Our salon, Our team

Good hair. Genuine care. A salon that feels like you.

Aura Hair Collective is a boutique salon in Carey Bay offering lived-in colour, balayage, cuts, styling and personalised hair care in a calm, welcoming setting.

Our team takes time to understand your hair, your routine and what you want to achieve. Every appointment is shaped around a result that suits you, feels comfortable and grows out beautifully.

Carey Bay salon questions

Everything you need to know before you visit.

Where is Aura Hair Collective?

Aura Hair Collective is at Shop 7/17 Laycock Street, Carey Bay NSW 2283, on the western shore of Lake Macquarie. Parking is available right outside the salon door.

What are Aura Hair Collective's opening hours?

The salon is open Tuesday 10am to 9pm for late-night appointments, Thursday 10am to 5pm and Friday 10am to 4pm. Saturday is by appointment request. Sunday, Monday and Wednesday are closed.

How do I book an appointment at Aura Hair Collective?

Book through the online calendar for live availability, or call 0476 427 024. For colour changes, include your hair history and inspiration so the right appointment length is reserved.

What hair services does Aura Hair Collective offer?

Aura offers lived-in blonde, balayage and foilayage, dimensional bronde and root melts, colour correction, ladies and men's cuts, blowouts, occasion styling and bridal hair.

Is there parking at Aura Hair Collective?

Yes. The salon is at Shop 7/17 Laycock Street, Carey Bay, with easy parking directly outside the studio door.

Still deciding what to book?

Send a short consultation enquiry with your hair history, or check live appointment times. We serve Carey Bay, Coal Point, Fishing Point, Rathmines, Toronto and the wider Lake Macquarie area.
