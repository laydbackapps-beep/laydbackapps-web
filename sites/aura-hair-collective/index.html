Founded on Craft · Owned by Alora

Built from the chair up by Alora.

After honing her blonde and balayage craft inside this very Carey Bay room from the beginning of its story as Mode Hair, Alora stepped into salon ownership — reimagining the studio as Aura Hair Collective.

Former owner Laura Dodds continues behind the chair on Thursdays and Fridays alongside Alora and the collective stylists, preserving five years of lakeside community while Alora leads the salon’s next chapter: effortless lived-in colour, unhurried consultations, and hair that still looks composed months after you leave.

“Same location, same amazing team, new era — a place where everyone not only walks away with amazing hair, but feels comfortable, confident and genuinely looked after.”

— Alora, Owner of Aura Hair Collective
