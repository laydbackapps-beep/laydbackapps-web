How a tree job runs

Trees are unpredictable: the plan gets worked out on site before anything starts moving.

  1. Send a photo

    Photos of the tree and what is around it (fences, sheds, powerlines) get the quote moving fast.

  2. Plan the drop

    We work out access, rigging and whether a crane or a climber is the safer way to bring it down.

  3. Cut, clear, tidy

    Tree removed or pruned, timber cut to your lengths and the site raked and left tidy.

Tree services questions

The usual ones, before anyone gets a saw out.

Will you take the wood away?

Up to you. We can cart all the green waste and timber away, or cut it to firewood lengths and leave it stacked for you. It changes the price, so tell us which you want when we quote.

Can you get to a tree in a tight backyard?

Usually, yes. Tight access is normal in this game, we rig and lower sections by rope or bring in a crane rather than dropping a tree where it cannot go.

How much does removing a tree cost?

It comes down to height, species, access and what is underneath. A tree with a clear drop zone is a very different job to one leaning over a shed, so photos and a site look are what give you a real number.

Do you grind the stump as well?

Stump grinding is a separate job we are happy to quote at the same time. Grinding goes below ground level so you can mow or replant over the top.

How soon can you get out?

Send a photo of the tree and roughly where you are. We will come back with the earliest time we can get to it, storm and hazard jobs get priority.
