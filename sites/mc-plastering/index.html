What we do

Plastering for walls, ceilings and everything between

Sheet, set and finish — the work that turns a frame and a stack of plasterboard into a surface you can paint.

01

New-build plastering

Sheeting, fixing, taping and stopping through new homes and extensions — walls and ceilings carried through to a straight, paint-ready surface.

02

Renovation plastering

Reworked walls and ceilings, new linings, patched openings and set joints for kitchens, bathrooms and full-home renovations.

03

Ceilings & cornice

Flat and bulkhead ceilings, raked and tray details, plus cornice supply and installation to finish the junction between wall and ceiling.

04

Setting & finishing

Bedding, taping, second and final coats, then sanding — the stopping that makes a wall flat enough to paint.

05

Crack & patch repairs

Cracks, damaged plasterboard, water-affected ceilings and knocked corners repaired and blended back to the surrounding finish.

06

Garages & soffits

Garage ceilings, eaves, alfresco and under-house soffits lined, stopped and finished.

How it runs

From first message to a paint-ready room

A straight, five-step run so you know what's happening on site and when.

  1. 01

    Get in touch

    Send a DM with the address and a few photos of the space, or what you're planning.

  2. 02

    Quote & measure

    We look at the areas, the linings and access, then come back with a clear quote.

  3. 03

    Sheet & fix

    Plasterboard measured, cut and fixed to walls and ceilings — straight, square and set to the frame.

  4. 04

    Stop, cornice & finish

    Joints bedded and taped, cornice fitted, coats built up and sanded to a paint-ready surface.

  5. 05

    Handover

    Broom-clean and walk-through, ready for the painter to take over.

Questions

What people ask before they book

What areas do you work in?

Newcastle, Lake Macquarie and the wider Hunter Region in NSW.

Do you take on new builds and renovations?

Yes — new build, renovation and repairs are the three areas of work the business is set up around.

How do I get a quote?

Send a direct message through Instagram with the address and a photo or two of the area, and we'll arrange a look and a quote.

Do you repair cracks and damaged walls or ceilings?

Yes. Cracks, damaged plasterboard, water-affected ceilings and corner damage are repaired and blended into the surrounding finish.

Do you install cornice and do the finishing?

Yes — cornice, setting and sanding all come under the plastering work, so the surface is handed over ready to paint.

What kind of finish will I get?

Walls and ceilings are setup, stopped and sanded to a flat, paint-ready finish for the painter to seal and topcoat.
