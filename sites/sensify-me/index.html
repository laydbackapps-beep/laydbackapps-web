Parties are personal. The right answer starts with your child, your celebration and a real conversation.

Ask your own question ↗
What does Sensify Me do?+

We create hands-on sensory play setups, custom party catering (including sensory-science and slime tables), and themed take-home sensory packs like our Spidey packs. Every celebration is tailored to your child’s age group and theme.

How much do parties or packs cost?+

Because every party setup and pack order is shaped around your guest count, theme and location, we quote each celebration individually. Send an enquiry below for a tailored, obligation-free quote.

Where do you travel, and what ages are suited?+

Pop your suburb or venue and your children’s ages into the enquiry notes and we’ll let you know travel availability and the best sensory bases for their age group.

What about allergies, sensitivities or accessibility?+

Every child is different! Let us know about any allergies, sensitivities or accessibility needs in your enquiry notes so we can share full material details and tailor the sensory bases before you book.

What’s in a Spidey pack?+

Each Spidey pack includes our custom-dyed red and blue sensory bases and themed play pieces. Because we make them fresh to order, let us know how many packs you need and your child’s age group so we can confirm exact inclusions and pricing.

Does enquiring make a booking?+

No. Your enquiry is a conversation starter only. Timing, price, suitability, scope and a booking all need direct confirmation from Sensify Me.
