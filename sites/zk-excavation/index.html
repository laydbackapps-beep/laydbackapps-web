About

Earthmoving on residential and civil sites

ZK Excavation takes on excavation and earthworks: stripping surface, cutting to level, opening trenches for services and drainage, then carting the spoil away and leaving a clean platform behind.

Preparation is most of it. Levels are checked before anything is dug, trenches are cut to fall, and the ground is left compacted and tidy so the next trade can start without reworking it.

  • Earthworks and site cuts to level
  • Trenching for services, sewer and stormwater
  • Drainage with pits and outlets set to fall
  • Spoil loaded out and carted away
  • Machine work kept tidy on tight blocks

Process

How an earthworks job runs

Walk it, cut it, drain it, hand it over on level.

  1. Walk the site

    Agree access, levels, the cut and where the spoil goes.

  2. Strip & cut

    Topsoil stripped, material cut and moved to design level.

  3. Trench & drain

    Service and stormwater trenches cut to fall, pits and lines set.

  4. Level & hand over

    Pads compacted, levels checked and the site left tidy.
