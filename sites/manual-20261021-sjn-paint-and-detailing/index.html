About SJN Paint & Detailing

SJN Paint & Detailing is a paint and detailing shop in Bathurst, working on cars, utes and vans.

Paint correction starts with preparation: the car is washed and decontaminated, the paint is measured, then the machine work is stepped down until the finish is even under a light.
