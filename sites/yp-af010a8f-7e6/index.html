About

Clipped hedges, cultivated grounds

Ray's Garden Works tends gardens across Fassifern and the surrounding Lake Macquarie area. We specialise in hedge trimming, garden maintenance, and landscape design — shaping green spaces with the same precision we bring to a formal topiary.

From routine pruning to full garden renewals, every job starts with listening: to the client, to the site, to the way light moves through the canopy.

Approach

Shape with patience

Good hedges aren't rushed. We cut to the growth habit, not against it — respecting the plant's form while guiding it toward the intended line. The same goes for gardens: work with the soil, the aspect, the existing structure.

We arrive on schedule, quote honestly, and leave the site tidy. That's the standard.
