# Allards — competitor research (Central Coast detailing), 26 Oct 2026

Read: uppereastauto.com.au, hobbsautospa.com, oceanglowcardetailing.com.au, vipcc.com.au, centralcoastmobiledetailing.com.au, onspotcardetailing.com.au, stevescentralcoastdetailing.com.

## What they all do
- **Named packages with inclusions and durations** (Premium Detail, Ultimate + single-stage correction, Maintenance Detail, Pre-Sale, Express Wash). Prices shown by some ($200 correction, $400 coating).
- **Explicit inclusion lists** — wash, clay, machine gloss, interior vacuum, tyre/trim dressing — so the buyer knows exactly what they get.
- **Add-ons**: leather conditioning, engine bay, headlight restoration, pet hair, sand removal, ozone/odour, hot water extraction.
- **"Why choose us"**: owner-operated, insured, security screened, 30+ years, IDA certified, product research.
- **Service-area suburb lists** (Wyong, Erina, Terrigal, Woy Woy, Tuggerah, Gosford…).
- **FAQ blocks** covering duration, what's included, mobile vs studio, "is it worth it".
- **Booking form** with vehicle type/model, service dropdown, extras.
- **Reviews** pulled from Google, and a "write a review" link.
- **New-car protection upsell** (PPF, tint, coating packs) and finance/offer hooks (free tint with full pack).

## Where they are weak (Allards' opening)
- Most are **mobile-first** — no controlled environment. Dust, sun, no lift, water restrictions.
- Copy is generic ("showroom shine", "passion", "affordable") and interchangeable between sites.
- No explanation of **process or lighting** — nothing about how defects are found.
- Package names are supermarket-tier ("Express Wash", "Mid-Range Detail").

## What Allards should carry, in its own voice
1. Packages framed around **condition, not price tiers** — Maintenance, Full Detail, Correction + Graphene — each with inclusions and honest duration.
2. **Add-ons** as a compact strip.
3. **"Why the workshop"** — controlled lighting, undercover, one car at a time, insured. This is the direct counter to every mobile competitor.
4. **Service area** list, phrased as drop-off suburbs.
5. **FAQ** answering duration, inclusions, booking lead time, boats/RVs.
6. Booking form with vehicle, service and add-ons.
7. Keep the 5.0 rating and review row.

## Voice rules
- Short declaratives. No "passion", no "showroom shine" cliché, no exclamation marks.
- Say what is done, in what order, and how long it takes.
- Red used only as a rule/accent, never as a fill.
