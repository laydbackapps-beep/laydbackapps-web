Graphene protection

Prep first. Coating second.

A coating only looks as good as the paint under it. So the car is washed and decontaminated, the paint is corrected, and only then is the coating applied and left to cure. That is the part that takes the time.

Ask about a coating
  • 01
    Wash & decontaminateRoad film, tar and bonded fallout lifted off before anything touches the paint.
  • 02
    CorrectSwirls and light defects polished back so the gloss is in the paint, not hidden under a coat.
  • 03
    Coat & cureApplied panel by panel and left to cure under cover, out of the weather.
