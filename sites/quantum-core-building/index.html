What we build

Residential work, done in order

Most jobs on a house are decided by three things: what the structure can carry, what the certifier needs to see, and what happens to the rooms while the work is going on. All three get worked out before demolition.

01

02

Extensions & additions

Questions

Asked before every quote

Do I need an engineer for a wall removal?
Usually yes. A structural engineer specifies the beam, the bearings and any temporary support. That detail is what the certifier and the builder work from — it is not something to work out on site.
Can we live in the house during the work?
Often, for part of it. Demolition and structural stages are the hard ones. The programme is set so the dustiest work happens in a block rather than dragging on for months.
How long does an extension take?
A single-storey rear extension is typically three to five months depending on approvals, weather and finishes. A second storey or a full renovation runs longer — the programme is given with the quote.
Who handles the certifier and inspections?
We coordinate the inspection schedule with your certifier and book the hold points into the programme, so framing and waterproofing are signed off before they are covered up.
Is the quote fixed?
The scope is fixed and priced by stage. Provisional items are named with an allowance, and any variation is priced and agreed before the work is done.
