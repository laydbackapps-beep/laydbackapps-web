What we do

Six scopes we build

From knocking out a wall to standing up a new extension — the structural work and the finish that follows.

01

Structural alterations

Load-bearing walls removed, beams and lintels set, and openings formed with the structure properly supported first.

02

Extensions & additions

New rooms, upper-floor additions and rear extensions — set out, framed and closed in to meet the existing house.

03

Renovations & fit-outs

Internal strip-outs and rebuilds, kitchen and bathroom fit-outs, linings, tiling and the finishes that go with them.

04

Concrete & formwork

Footings, slabs, paths and retaining walls formed, reinforced and poured as part of the build.

05

Framing & envelope

Timber and steel framing, roof structure, cladding and windows — the building made weathertight.

06

Set-out & demolition

Site set-out and the careful strip-out that comes first, with the parts of the house staying kept protected.
