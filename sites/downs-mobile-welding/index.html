What we take on

Mobile welding, repairs, gates, fabrication

Four kinds of job come up again and again: something steel has broken, something steel needs building, or something on wheels needs to hold together. All of it gets welded on your site.

01

Mobile welding

The welding gear comes to you — your yard, shed, block, farm or roadside. That means no removal, no freight and no waiting on a workshop booking: the job is welded where it sits, in the position it actually works in.

  • Yard & shed work
  • Farm & rural sites
  • Roadside & paddock repairs
02

On-site repairs

Cracked frames, torn brackets, snapped hinges, a gate that no longer swings, a trailer that has taken a knock. We look at the damage, cut back to sound steel, prep it and weld it properly rather than running a bead over rust.

  • Cracked & torn steel
  • Broken hinges & mounts
  • Trailer body repairs
03

Gates & trailers

Gate and gate frames built to suit the opening, plus repairs and strengthening on trailer bodies, drawbars, ramps and tie-down points — cut, welded and fitted to suit what you are actually carrying.

  • Gate frames & hinges
  • Drawbars & ramps
  • Tie-down points
04

Fabrication

Steel made to your measurements: frames, brackets, stands, benches, jigs and one-off pieces to finish a repair or fit out a shed. You give the dimensions and the job it has to do, and it gets built to that.

  • Frames & brackets
  • Stands & benches
  • One-off parts

How a job runs

Three steps, start to finished weld

Most of the information needed for a quote is a couple of photos, a rough size and where the job is.

Step 01

Tell us what you have

Send a photo or two, the size or measurements, and the location. If you are not sure what is wrong, describe what it is doing — a gate dragging, a trailer flexing, a frame that has opened up.

Step 02

We come to the site

The welding gear arrives with the steel, consumables and power arrangement that job needs, and the repair is assessed on the ground before anything is cut.

Step 03

Welded, ground, explained

Welding, grinding and clean-up done where the gate, trailer or frame already is — then a straight explanation of what was wrong and what was done about it.

About

Steel work that comes to the site

Downs Mobile Welding is a mobile welding business working across Bendigo and Central Victoria. It exists because a lot of steel never needs to leave the property to be fixed — it needs a welder who will turn up to it.

That covers the everyday jobs: a gate frame that has pulled out of square, a trailer that needs plating, a bracket nobody stocks, a machine that is too heavy to move and too expensive to be off the job. If it is steel and it is on your place, it can usually be dealt with on site.

Jobs are quoted on what the work actually needs — the steel, the site and the access — and you get a clear picture of the repair before it starts.
