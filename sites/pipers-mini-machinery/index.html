Services

Compact equipment for precision earthworks across residential, commercial and civil projects.

Site Preparation & Clearing

Vegetation removal, topsoil stripping, and site levelling for new builds, extensions and landscaping.

Trenching & Service Installation

Narrow trenching for stormwater, sewer, power and data - minimal reinstatement required.

Retaining Walls & Footings

Excavation for block walls, sleeper walls and concrete footings - accurate cut to engineer's spec.

Pool & Tank Excavation

Precise cuts for fibreglass pools, concrete tanks and water retention - laser-levelled bases.

Driveway & Hardstand Prep

Sub-base preparation, compaction and grading for concrete, asphalt and paver driveways.

Demolition & Removal

Small-structure demolition, concrete breaking and material removal - site left clean and ready.

Our Approach

Straightforward process from first call to site handover.

Site Visit & Scope

We walk the site, confirm access, identify services and agree the cut/fill plan with you.

Machine Match & Schedule

We select the right mini excavator or loader for the access and ground conditions, then lock in dates.

Excavation & Spoil Management

Work to agreed levels with spoil stockpiled on-site or removed - dust and noise kept to a minimum.

Verification & Handover

Final levels checked, site cleaned, and certification provided where required.
