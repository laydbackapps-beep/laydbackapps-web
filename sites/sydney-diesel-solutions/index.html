What the tune works on

Three things a diesel owner actually notices.

A tune that only looks good on a dyno sheet is a bad tune. These are the three areas a customised map is built around, in the order most owners care about them.

01

Torque where you tow

Weight is the point of a 4x4. A customised map brings the pull in earlier and holds it through the mid range, so a loaded trailer or a tray full of tools needs fewer downshifts and less right foot to keep moving.

02

Fuel use on the highway

Most of the fuel goes in at cruise and on cold starts, not at full throttle. Tuning the part-throttle area is where economy comes from, which is also the area a generic file tends to leave alone.

03

Reliability under load

More torque is easy to write and easy to regret. A tune for a working vehicle should stay inside what the turbo, injectors and cooling can support, and be honest about where the limit is instead of chasing a bigger figure.

Questions

Straight answers before you message.

Is a tune a problem for a daily driver?

It should not be. A daily driver spends most of its life at light throttle, so that is the part of the map that matters most for smoothness and fuel. Say that the vehicle is a daily and the tune is written around that use rather than a maximum-effort setting.

Will tuning help with towing, or just power?

Towing is a torque-shape problem: getting the pull in early so the gearbox is not hunting and the engine is not working at the top of the rev range. That is the part of a customised map that makes the biggest difference with weight behind you.

What about fuel efficiency, is that a real gain or a claim?

It is real when the part-throttle area is tuned for it and the vehicle is otherwise healthy. It is not a guarantee, and it is not the reason to tune a vehicle on its own. How you drive and what you carry still decide most of the number.

My 4x4 is modified. Does that change things?

Yes, and it needs to be in the first message. Exhaust, intercooler, intake and tyre size all change what the engine sees, so a file written for a standard vehicle is the wrong file for a modified one.

How do I start?

Send a Facebook message with the vehicle, what it tows or carries, and what you want changed. That is enough for a straight answer on whether a custom tune suits the vehicle and what the next step is.
