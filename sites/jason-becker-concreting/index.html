How a slab gets poured

Four steps, in order, every time.

Nothing is poured until the set-out and the steel are right. The finish is only ever as good as what is underneath it.

01 — Set out & steel

The site is set out to the plan and the reinforcement grid is tied and chaired in place before anything is poured.

02 — Formwork

Forms are set true to line and level, so the finished edge of the slab runs straight and the fall is right.

03 — Place the concrete

Concrete is placed and worked through the steel, filling the forms evenly and vibrating out the voids as it goes down.

04 — Screed, float & finish

The surface is screeded to level, floated and finished — broom, steel or aggregate — with joints cut ready for the next trade.
