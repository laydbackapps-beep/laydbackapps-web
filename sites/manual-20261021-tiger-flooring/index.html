About Tiger Flooring

Tiger Flooring supplies and lays timber, hybrid, vinyl plank and laminate flooring, and sands and polishes existing timber floors.

What is under the boards decides how the floor ages, so subfloor preparation gets done first: levelling, patching, and a flat surface to lay onto.

  • Timber, hybrid, vinyl plank and laminate
  • Subfloor levelling and preparation
  • Sanding and polishing of existing floors
