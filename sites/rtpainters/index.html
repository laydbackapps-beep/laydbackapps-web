What gets painted

Every job is quoted after looking at the surface, because what is under the paint decides what the job costs.

01

Interior walls & ceilings

Patched, sanded and primed, then two coats cut in by hand at the cornice and skirting.

02

Trim, doors & windows

Beads and sashes sanded back and painted so they still close without sticking.

03

Exterior repaints

Weatherboards and render washed, scraped and primed where the old coating has failed.

04

Feature walls

Colour on one wall, prepared and finished to the same standard as the rest of the room.

05

Rendered surfaces

Masonry sealed and painted with the right coating for the substrate.

06

Make-good & patch repairs

Cracks, holes and water stains dealt with before painting, not painted over.

How a paint job runs

Four stages, and the first two take longer than the painting.

Stage 01

Look at the surface

What is under the paint — flaking, mould, patches — decides the prep and the price.

Stage 02

Prepare

Sanded, filled, masked and primed. This is the stage that shows in year three.

Stage 03

Paint

Two coats, cut in by hand, with the sheen agreed before the first wall.

Stage 04

Check & clean

Walked with you under the light, touch-ups done, furniture back and floors clean.
