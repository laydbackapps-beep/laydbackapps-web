What we paint

Domestic repaints and refresh work, inside and out.

  • Interior repaintsWalls, ceilings and trim in low-sheen or wash-and-wear, with the cutting in done by hand.
  • Exterior paintingWeatherboards, render, eaves and fascia — washed, filled and primed before topcoats.

Questions

Do I need to move furniture?
Small items off the walls and surfaces is a help. Larger furniture is moved to the middle and covered.
How long does a room take?
A standard bedroom is usually a day of prep and a day of painting, depending on the condition of the walls.
Do you supply the paint?
Either. Paint can be included in the quote or supplied by you — the quantity is worked out from the area.
Can you match an existing colour?
Yes — from a sample, a code or a chip, matched at the trade counter before the job starts.
