Finishes

The right finish for the wall

Three ways render goes on — and the look each one gives.

Cement

Hard-wearing sand-and-cement render. Builds out uneven blockwork and leaves a solid base ready for paint or texture.

Acrylic

Flexible, crack-resistant acrylic render — the usual choice for newer homes and recladding where the wall will move.

Texture

Rolled or sprayed texture coats in the profile you want, over cement or acrylic render, to finish the elevation.

How a job runs

Look, prepare, render, finish

01

Look & measure

We walk the walls with you, check the substrate and measure up, so the quote is real rather than a guess.

02

Prepare

Wall cleaned, loose material knocked back, windows and frames masked so render grips properly.

03

Render

Render built up in coats to a consistent thickness, ruled and floated to a flat, true plane.

04

Finish & clean

The chosen finish worked in, edges cut sharp, and the site swept down before we leave.
