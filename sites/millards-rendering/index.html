What we do

Rendering services, start to finish

Four core trades; each applied with the same layout lines, tolerance checks and pride in a tight finish.

Cement rendering

Traditional sand-cement render on brick, block and concrete; floated, sponged or bagged to your spec.

Acrylic rendering

Flexible, crack-resistant acrylic systems in a broad colour range; no paint needed, UV stable.

Texture coating

Decorative texture finishes; fine, medium or coarse; for character and weather protection.

Repairs & restorations

Crack stitching, delamination fixes, patch matching and full re-renders on heritage and modern homes.
