How much does a build cost?

It moves with size, specification and the ground under it. Once the site is assessed you get a fixed written quote, so the number does not drift mid-job.

Yes. Licence number and certificate of currency are supplied with the quote and available on request.

Which areas do you cover?

Sydney metro, including the CBD, Parramatta, Liverpool, Penrith, Campbelltown, Chatswood and Hornsby.

How long does a build take?

A standard single-storey home generally runs six to nine months from slab to handover, subject to approvals, weather and finishes.
