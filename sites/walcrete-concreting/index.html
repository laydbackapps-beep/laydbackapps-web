Before you quote

What we need to price it

Concrete cannot be quoted accurately from a phone call. Two driveways the same length are different jobs once depth, access and reinforcement are counted — so the price is built from the four things below.

No site visit needed to start

Send photos and rough measurements and the first number comes back the same day. The measured price follows the site check.

  • Rough size and shape. Length, width and whether it is a straight run or has returns, steps or curves.
  • What it is for. A car, a truck, a shed, a pool surround or foot traffic — each takes a different thickness and finish.
  • Access. How close a truck or pump can get, gates, slopes and anything overhead. This decides whether it is barrowed or pumped.
  • The existing surface. Grass, gravel, pavers or an old slab that has to be broken out and carted away.
  • Finish and colour. Broom, exposed, trowel or coloured — and whether it has to match something already there.

Questions

Asked before a quote

Why can't I get a firm price over the phone?
Because the number is decided by depth, access, reinforcement and what has to be carted away. A rough guide is given straight away, and the firm price follows the site check.
How thick should the slab be?
It depends on what drives on it. A footpath and a driveway carrying a 4WD take different thicknesses and reinforcement, so that is settled before the pour rather than assumed.
Can you match concrete that is already there?
Usually close. Colour and aggregate depend on the original mix and how it has weathered, so the match is discussed before the pour instead of promised afterwards.
How long before I can drive on it?
Foot traffic is normally a day or so and vehicles around seven days, depending on the mix and the weather. You get a specific answer for your slab, not a generic one.
Do you take the spoil and the old slab away?
Yes. Breaking out, disposal and re-compaction of the base under the new slab are all part of the quote where an existing surface has to come up.
Do you handle crossover approvals?
Crossovers over a council footpath or verge need approval. That is sorted before the pour so the job is not left half-finished waiting on paperwork.
