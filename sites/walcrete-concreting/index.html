What we do

Concreting work for homes and businesses.

Driveways

Plain, coloured and exposed aggregate driveways poured to the right fall so water runs away.

Slabs & footings

House, shed, carport and patio slabs formed, steeled and poured level.

Exposed aggregate

Aggregate finishes that stay sharp rather than wearing smooth and grey.

Paths & patios

Paths, alfresco slabs and entry areas finished with a clean edge and a even surface.

Retaining & edging

Steel and timber edging plus small retaining walls that hold the levels.

Commercial hardstand

Hardstand, aprons and access concrete for commercial premises.

How a job runs

From the first look to the final clean up.

Look at the site

Access, levels and drainage are checked before a price is given.

Set out and prepare

The area is stripped, formed and steeled so the pour lands where it should.

Pour and finish

Concrete is placed, screeded and finished to the texture you chose, then cleaned down.

About Walcrete Concreting

Walcrete Concreting pours driveways, slabs, paths and exposed aggregate for homes and businesses.

Most of the difference is in what happens before the truck arrives — the set-out, the sub-base and the fall — which is why the site gets checked properly before it is quoted.

  • Driveways and slabs · the bulk of the work
  • Exposed aggregate · finished sharp, not worn smooth
  • Quoted from the site · access and levels checked first
  • Left clean · ready to walk on when cured
