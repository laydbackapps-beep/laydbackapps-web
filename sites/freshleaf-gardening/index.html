The year, by job

What gets done when — and why the timing matters more than the effort.

WhenJobWhy then
Late winterPruning & bare-root plantingDormant growth takes a hard cut without bleeding, and bare-root stock establishes before spring.
Early springFeed, mulch and plantSoil is warming; mulch now holds the moisture the summer will take.
Late springTurf and hedgingTurf knits before the heat, and hedges put on the growth that gets shaped.
SummerWatering & deadheadingLess cutting, more keeping alive — the wrong trim in summer burns new growth.
AutumnCut back, lift, divideThe season for soil work, dividing perennials and re-turfing bare patches.
WinterStructure & clean-upBeds cleared, edges cut back in, and the plan made for spring.

A garden, built in order

Drainage and structure first, planting last — the order that stops a garden failing in its first wet season.

Stage 01

Levels & drainage

Where water goes decides everything planted after it.

Stage 02

Structure

Retaining, edging and paths built before a plant goes in.

Stage 03

Soil

Compaction broken up, organic matter worked through, beds shaped.

Stage 04

Planting & mulch

Species chosen for the aspect, planted at the right depth, mulched and watered in.

About the work

Local gardens around Newcastle, kept on a schedule rather than rescued twice a year. Beds are edged, soil is fed and green waste leaves with us.

Planting is chosen for the aspect, the soil and the coast weather — species that will still be there in three years.
