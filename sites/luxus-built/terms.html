Terms

These are the terms that apply to a quote request sent through this site.

A quote is prepared from what can be seen at the property and the details you give. If the scope changes once work starts, the price is re-confirmed with you before any extra work goes ahead.

Safe access to the site, and power and water if they are needed, are provided by the property owner.

Payment

Payment terms are set out on the quote and confirmed before work starts.
