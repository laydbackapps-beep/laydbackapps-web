Process

Sequence of a build

Four stages, from the first conversation to the final clean up.

  1. 1

    Brief

    What you want, what the site allows and what the budget has to carry, worked out together before anything is priced.

  2. 2

    Design and detail

    Drawings and selections settled so the job is priced and built from the same set of documents.

  3. 3

    Build

    Work runs to the drawings, with the trades sequenced and the site inspected as it goes.

  4. 4

    Handover

    Finished, cleaned up and handed over with the details resolved and the paperwork closed out.

About

The practice

Luxus Built is a residential building and design practice working across Newcastle, Lake Macquarie and the Hunter Valley. New homes, renovations and extensions are handled from the design stage through to handover, so the drawings, the selections and the build all answer to the same set of decisions.

Detail settled on paper costs nothing to change. On site, it costs everything.

That is the whole method. The work is planned while it is still on paper, where changing it is cheap, rather than on site, where it is not.
