Roofing services

Restoration, repairs and safety rail for homes and commercial buildings on the Central Coast.

Roof restoration

Tiled and metal roofs brought back: broken and slipped tiles replaced, valleys and flashings cleaned out, the roof sealed and left watertight.

Re-bedding and re-pointing

Ridge and hip capping lifted, the failed mortar raked out, then re-bedded and re-pointed so the capping stays put.

Ridge capping and flashings

Ridge capping, valleys, barge and apron flashings repaired or replaced so water runs where it should instead of into the ceiling.

Leak repairs

Leak tracing and repair: cracked tiles, perished flashings, storm damage and roof penetrations made weathertight again.

Re-roofs and metal roofing

Strip and replace an aged roof with new sheeting, sarking, battens and flashings, or lay new corrugated metal on a new build or extension.

Roof safety rail

Guardrail and edge protection installed for roof access, maintenance and working at height.
