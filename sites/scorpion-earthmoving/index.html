What we run

Ground worked to design level.

Every job follows the same discipline: strip, cut, fill, trim, check. The pad is left ready for the slab, the footing or the next trade.

Bulk and detailed

Site cuts and pad preparation

Cuts taken to design level, tracked with laser receivers on a staked grid. Fill placed in layers, compacted and trimmed so the pad holds its edge and drains away from the build.

Depth and fall

Trenching

Service, drainage and footing trenches cut to the depth and fall marked out. Spoil stacked clear of the line, backfill on request.

Strip out

Land clearing

Vegetation removal, root raking and topsoil stripping. Green waste mulched on site or carted, ground left clean for the next stage.

Tight tolerance

Detailed excavation

Footings, piers, service pits and tight cuts. Minimal over-dig, material handled once, edges left straight enough to trim by bucket.

On and off truck

Driveways and hardstand

Access tracks, driveways and hardstand cut, formed and compacted, so plant and deliveries can get around a tight site.

Movement

Method

How the level gets held.

Grade is set before the machine starts and checked before it leaves. Cut and fill are worked as one operation so nothing is shifted twice.

Grade controlDual-grade laser on the pad, staked at a 5 m grid with receivers on the stick.
One machineThe same excavator cuts, pushes and finishes. No hand-offs, no arguments about level.
Spoil balanceCut volume worked back into the fill zones. Excess carted only where the design demands it.
Tight accessTracked machine that gets down a side boundary and works without taking the fence line out.
HandoverFinal trim by bucket, level read across the pad, spoil and ruts cleaned up before we go.
