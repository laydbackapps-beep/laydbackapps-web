Privacy

This page explains what happens to the details you send through the quote form on this site.

What is collected

  • Your name, phone number, email address and the message you write
  • The work you describe in the form

Why

Only to answer your enquiry and prepare a quote. Nothing else.

Who sees it

The enquiry is emailed to Pappy's Lawn and Yard Care. It is not sold or passed to advertisers.

Keeping it

Enquiry details are kept while the job is current, and for a reasonable period after so past work can be referenced.
