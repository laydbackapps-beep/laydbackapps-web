The work

Four trades, run on measured set-out. Every job is priced off the drawings, the wall build-up and the access in.

  1. 01

    Diamond core drilling

    Holes for services, anchors and penetrations, handheld or rig-mounted, wet or dry with extraction on the shroud.

    152 to 600mm
  2. 02

    Wall chasing

    Channels for cable, conduit and pipe, cut with twin-blade saws and blown out. Depth controlled so the block face behind stays intact.

    15 to 50mm wide
  3. 03

    Concrete cutting and sawing

    Flat sawing, wall sawing and wire sawing for door and window openings, expansion joints and slab cuts, taken to a set depth.

    Flat, wall, wire
  4. 04

    Controlled demolition

    Selective removal in tight access and vibration-sensitive buildings, by remote rig or hydraulic burst rather than impact.

    Selective
