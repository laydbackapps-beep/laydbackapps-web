How a job runs

Four steps, and the levels decided before the first bucket

STEP 01

Walk the site

We look at the ground, the access and where the water runs now. That is what the quote is built from.

STEP 02

Set the levels

Cut and finish heights are set and pegged before the machine moves, so the shape is decided once.

STEP 03

Cut, fill, compact

Material is moved in layers and compacted as it goes, not tipped in one lift and left to sink.

STEP 04

Shape and hand over

Batters, falls and clean-up finished, then the job is walked through with you on the ground.

About

Worked to the ground you actually have

Ranchline Contracting is an earthworks and contracting business working out of Ipswich QLD. Most jobs start with the same problem: the levels are wrong, the water is running the wrong way, or the access will not take a machine.

Questions

Asked before most jobs start

What area do you work?

Out of Ipswich QLD and into the surrounding areas. If you are not sure whether your place is in range, call and ask — it is a short conversation.

How do you quote a job?

On site. Ground, access and drainage change the price more than anything else, so the job is looked at before it is priced. Call or send the form through and we will organise a time to walk it.

Do you take on small jobs?

Yes. A single trench, a driveway, a dam repair or a pad cut is quoted and run the same way as a bigger block of work.

Can I hire the machine without an operator?

No — the machines go out with an operator. The cut, the levels and the compaction are the parts that decide whether the job lasts, so they stay with the machine.

What do you need from me before work starts?

Access for the machine, and a rough idea of the finished level or where the water should run. If there are services or a boundary to work around, point them out when we walk the site.
