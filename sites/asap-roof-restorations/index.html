Scope

What gets done, and where on the roof.

A restoration is a set of repairs in a fixed order. The roof is looked at first, so the list matches what is actually up there.

  1. 01

    Ridge and hip caps re-bedded and pointed

    Old mortar raked out of the bed, the caps re-set in a fresh bed, then pointed so the line runs straight along the ridge and down every hip.

  2. 02

    Broken and slipped tiles replaced

    Cracked and walked-on tiles swapped for matching ones, and slipped tiles re-hung so the courses line up again.

  3. 03

    Valleys and flashings repaired

    Where the roof meets a wall or drops into a valley, the flashing is the leak. It gets opened up, repaired and sealed.

  4. 04

    The roof looked at before it is priced

    No quote over the phone for a roof nobody has seen. ASAP quotes after an inspection, and the repairs are listed before any work starts.

What a failing roof looks like

What a roof looks like before it is restored.

You can usually pick these from the street or from the ceiling cavity.

  • Grit in the gutter. Sand and mortar along the gutter usually means the ridge caps are letting go.
  • A ridge line that dips or gaps. Caps sitting proud, cracked or missing along the top of the roof.
  • Stained ceiling or a damp patch. A valley or flashing above it is letting water in.
  • Tiles out of line. Slipped or cracked tiles visible as a break in the pattern of courses.
  • A ceiling that sags at the wall. Water getting behind the flashing at a wall junction.
  • Birds working the ridge. Lifted or loose caps give them a way in.
