Before water gets in

Signs the roof is due

Roofs rarely fail in one go. These are the things worth acting on before water is already inside the ceiling.

  • Ridge caps lifting or mortar crumbling in the gutter
  • Moss, lichen or black staining across the tile face
  • Faded or chalky colour that rubs off on your hand
  • Cracked or slipped tiles after wind
  • Damp patches or staining on the ceiling
  • Granules and grit collecting in the gutters

Questions

Before you book

How long does a roof restoration take?

Most houses are several days rather than one, because cleaning, bedding and coating each need their own conditions. You get the sequence up front.

Can you just fix the ridge caps?

Yes, if that is all that has failed. It gets quoted as its own job rather than talked up into a full restoration.

Will pressure cleaning damage the tiles?

Not when it is done at the right pressure for the tile type. Tiles that are already brittle are assessed first, and the coating is what protects them afterwards.

How long does the coating last?

It depends on the product and the roof, and it is worth asking which coating is being used rather than assuming. That gets explained at quote stage.

Do you clear the gutters afterwards?

Yes — debris from washing and bedding is cleared before the job is signed off.
