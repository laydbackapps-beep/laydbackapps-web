02 — Preparation

The part you don't see.

Paint only looks as good as what is underneath it. Filling, sanding and masking are the slow half of the job, and they are the half that decides how it looks in two years.

  • 01Wash and checkSurfaces cleaned back so the coating actually bonds.
  • 02Fill and sandNail holes, cracks and dents levelled before a brush touches it.
  • 03Mask and coverFloors, joinery and fixtures protected — no splatter left behind.
  • 04Prime and coatSpot priming then the full coats, cut in by hand at every edge.
  • 05Walk throughInspection together before the drop sheets come up.

04 — Questions

Before you book.

Do I need to move furniture out?

Not out of the house — everything gets moved to the middle of the room and covered. Fragile items and wall hangings are best cleared before the day starts.

How long does a repaint take?

A single room is usually a day; a full interior runs longer once prep and drying times are counted. You get a realistic window in the quote rather than the fastest possible answer.

Who supplies the paint?

Either way works. Supplying it through the trade account usually keeps the price down and guarantees the right product for the surface.

Can you work around a tenant or a live household?

Yes — rooms are worked in sections so the house stays liveable, and the site is left tidy at the end of each day.
