What we do

Every stage of the build, under one contractor

“All aspects of building” is how Marshbuild describes the work — new builds, extensions, renovations and the concrete and decking that finish them off, handled as one job rather than farmed out.

01

New builds

Slab to handover on new homes — set-out, framing, roof, cladding and fit-out coordinated as one job.

02

Extensions & additions

Extra bedrooms, living areas and second-storey additions tied properly into the existing structure.

03

Renovations

Full-house and single-room renovations, including stripping out, re-framing and making good before fit-out.

04

Kitchens & joinery

Kitchen and cabinetry installs — carcasses set level, benches templated, appliances and rangehoods integrated.

05

Decks & outdoor structures

Timber decking, framing, posts and stairs — built to fall away from the house and tie into the entry.

06

Concrete & formwork

Steps, slabs, paths and retaining — formwork set, steel placed, then poured and finished on site.

How the job runs

Four steps, no surprises on site

A straight process from the first phone call to the final walk-through — the scope is agreed before anything is ordered.

STEP 01

Call and describe it

Ring 0411 210 834 with the address and the work you have in mind, and you will get a straight answer on whether it suits.

STEP 02

Site visit & measure

A site visit looks at access, the existing structure and services, and measures up what is actually there.

STEP 03

Scope and price

You get the work written down — what is included, what is not, and the order it happens in.

STEP 04

Build & handover

The build runs to that scope, then a walk-through at the end to close out anything still on the list.

Questions

What people ask before they call

If your question is not here, ring and ask — it is quicker than email for most jobs.

Which areas do you work in?

Newcastle, the Hunter Valley and Tamworth. If you are on the edge of that area, call and ask — the answer depends on the size of the job.

What kind of work do you take on?

All aspects of building: new homes, extensions and additions, renovations, kitchen and cabinetry installs, timber decks, and concrete work such as steps, slabs and retaining.

Do you only do full builds, or smaller jobs too?

Both. The gallery carries deck framing and entry steps alongside full kitchen fit-outs — describe the job on the phone and you will get a straight answer.

How do I get a price?

Call 0411 210 834. Give the address and a description of the work, and a site visit gets booked to measure up before anything is priced.

Can I see more of your work?

The gallery above is pulled from Marshbuild's own job photos, and the Facebook page carries the latest posts from site.

How far ahead should I book?

Ask when you call — it changes with the job list. Getting the call in early means the site visit happens while you are still deciding on scope.
