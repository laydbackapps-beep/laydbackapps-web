About

Ray Linter provides bricklaying, block laying, brick repointing & repairs, feature brickwork, garden walls, and piers & columns across Belmont NSW.

01

Services

01

Bricklaying

Standard and face brickwork for new builds, extensions, and renovations. Running bond, stack bond, and custom patterns.

02

Block Laying

Concrete and clay block construction for structural walls, retaining walls, and partition systems.

03

Brick Repointing & Repairs

Mortar joint renewal, cracked brick replacement, and structural stitching for heritage and modern brickwork.

04

Feature Brickwork

Decorative arches, soldier courses, corbelling, and custom bond patterns for architectural detail.

05

Garden Walls

Boundary, retaining, and feature garden walls in brick and block with copings and pier details.

06

Piers & Columns

Structural and decorative piers, gate posts, verandah columns, and pier caps in matching brickwork.
