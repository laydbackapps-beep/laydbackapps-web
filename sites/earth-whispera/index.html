What we do

Earthworks and siteworks, start to finish.

Tighter than it needs to be, every stage of the job. The cut, the fall on the trench, the compaction under a slab.

  1. 01

    Bulk excavation

    House pads, shed pads, dams, site stripping and level cuts.

  2. 02

    Footings

    Strip footings, pier holes and slab thickenings, trenched straight and square.

  3. 03

    Trenching for services

    Water, power, comms and sewer runs, laser-graded to the required fall.

  4. 04

    Drainage

    Table drains, spoon drains, culverts and erosion control structures.

  5. 05

    Driveways & access

    Rural driveways and access tracks, crowned and drained so they hold.

  6. 06

    Retaining walls

    Cut batter support, sleeper and rock walls, designed backfill.

  7. 07

    Slab & hardstand prep

    Sub-base, compaction and formwork-ready pads for sheds and car parks.

  8. 08

    Landscaping & finish grade

    Final grade, topsoil spread and rock placement, tidied as we leave.

How a job runs

Same bloke, first walk to last pass.

No subcontracted operator arriving halfway through. Whoever sets the job out is the one who finishes it.

  1. Walk the block

    Aaron reads the levels, the access and where the spoil has to go before anything moves.

  2. Cut to the set-out

    Laser-guided excavation to the line, batter shaped as the cut comes out.

  3. Grade, compact, hand over

    Rolled, trimmed and left clean for the builder or concreter to walk onto.
