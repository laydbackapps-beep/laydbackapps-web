Questions

Why does my safety switch keep tripping?
Something on that circuit is leaking to earth — an appliance, water in a fitting or a damaged cable. It is tested circuit by circuit rather than guessed at.
Do I need a new switchboard?
If it still has ceramic fuses or no safety switches, yes for safety. Otherwise it depends on capacity for what you want to add.
Can you install a powerpoint outside?
Yes — weatherproof outlets with the right enclosure and protection for the circuit.
Do you supply the fittings?
Either. Supply your own or the job is quoted with the fittings included.
