Fault to fixed

How a job runs

Five steps, no surprises — from the first call to the circuit being tested and switched back on.

  1. 01Call and describe the fault

    Tell us what has stopped working and where. If it is urgent we will say so up front.

  2. 02Test and isolate

    The circuit is tested and made safe before any repair starts.

  3. 03Quote in plain terms

    You get the scope and the price before we go ahead — not after.

  4. 04Fix and re-test

    The fault is repaired, then the circuit is tested again to confirm it holds.

  5. 05Tidy up and switch on

    Site is left clean and the circuit is returned to service.
