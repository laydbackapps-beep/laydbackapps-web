The order the job runs in

No shortcuts hidden under a topcoat. Every room is run through the same sequence, and you get a written quote with a clear scope before anything starts.

STEP 01

Make good & mask

Surfaces checked, problem areas made good, floors and fittings masked and protected.

STEP 02

Fill, sand & prime

Gaps and dents filled, surfaces sanded and dusted down, then primed where they need it.

STEP 03

Coat the surfaces

Walls, ceilings and trim coated in the right finish for the room and the wear it takes.

STEP 04

Paper & detail

Wallpaper hung or feature finishes laid in, joins and edges worked to a straight line.

STEP 05

Cut in & hand back

Masking off, edges cut in clean, the site swept and everything put back where it was.

Painting questions

What people usually ask before booking.

What do you paint and decorate?

Interiors and exteriors, wallpaper and feature walls, plus doors, trim and joinery — and full repaints in a new colour scheme.

Is preparation included?

Yes. Surface preparation is part of the job, not an extra: filling, sanding, patching and priming before any finish coat goes on. If a surface needs more than that, it is written into the quote as its own line so you can see it.

Do you hang and remove wallpaper?

Both. Paper is hung to a straight line with the joins worked out across the room, and existing paper can be stripped and the walls made good before repainting.

How do I get a price?

Send the job details through the quote form — the rooms, the surfaces and the timing — and we will come back to you with a price. There is no phone line on this site, so the form is the fastest way to reach us.

Which areas do you cover?

Cobbitty and the surrounding Camden district.
