What we do

Concreting, start to finish

Every job gets the same order of work — set out, excavate, form, steel, pour and finish — whether it is a garden path or a house slab. That is what keeps a surface flat and an edge straight.

01

Driveways & crossovers

Plain, coloured and exposed-aggregate driveways, excavated, compacted and graded so water runs away from the house, then finished hard and even.

02

Slabs

House, shed and extension slabs set out to plan, boxed true and poured in one continuous run, with the right sub-base and reinforcement underneath.

03

Patios & alfresco

Patio, alfresco and carport slabs with tidy set-out and tight edges, broom or float finished to suit how the space gets used.

04

Exposed aggregate

Washed-back aggregate finishes with the stone showing through — a surface that hides marks, drains well and still looks sharp years on.

05

Decorative finishes

Coloured, stencilled and textured concrete for driveways, paths and entertaining areas, worked to a consistent pattern across the whole pour.

06

Paths & removal, replace

Paths, walkways and footpath sections, plus cutting out and replacing cracked or sunken concrete with a patch that blends into the slab around it.
