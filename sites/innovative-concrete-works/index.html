Process

How a pour runs

// EXPOSED AGGREGATE SEQUENCE, pour → wash → seal

  • Site set-out, formwork and reinforcement placed to the engineer's plan, levels checked before the truck arrives.
  • Concrete placed, screeded and bull-floated, timing the bleed water is critical for the wash pass.
  • Surface retarder applied, then the wash pass sweeps the cement skin away, aggregate exposed to a uniform depth.
  • Final rinse, cure and penetrating sealer, colour pop, stain resistance and a surface that lasts.
