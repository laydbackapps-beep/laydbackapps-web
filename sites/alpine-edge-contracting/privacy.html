Privacy

Privacy policy · last updated 19 November 2026

Short version: this site collects nothing. Longer version below.

What this site stores

Nothing. There are no analytics scripts, no advertising pixels, no cookies and no tracking of any kind on this site.

Calls and messages

Contact happens by phone, Instagram or Facebook. The details you give Alex are used to quote the job and arrange the work. They are not sold, rented or shared with anyone else.

This site has no form

There is no enquiry form on this site, because there is no verified business inbox to deliver one to. Calls and direct messages are the contact route.

Photos

Photos you send to discuss a job are used only to work out what the job involves.

External links

Instagram and Facebook are third party sites with their own privacy policies. Alpine Edge Contracting does not control what they collect.
