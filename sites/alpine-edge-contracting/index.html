When you ring

Three steps, no call centre

The job is talked through on the phone, looked at on site, then booked.

01 Tell

Describe the job on the phone: the fence length, the dam, the area to spray, or the pad. A photo or a pin helps more than a description.

02 Look

Access, ground and how much material moves decide the price. That gets looked at on site before a number is given.

03 Book

A price and a date, then the machine turns up. The job is talked through again on the day before the first cut.
