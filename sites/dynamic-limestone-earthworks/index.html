The gear matters less than the levels it leaves. We set out, cut and compact to the plan so the next trade isn't fixing our work.

  • Cut & fillGround taken down or built up to design level, compacted in layers, ready for slab or paving.
  • Site clearingVegetation, rubble and old hardstand stripped and removed, with the levels kept where they need to be.
  • TrenchingService trenches, footings and drainage lines dug to depth and line for the plumber or sparky to follow.

Why limestone

Limestone against the alternatives

Limestone

  • Natural material that suits the WA coastal look
  • Handles weather and salt without corroding or rusting
  • Curves and steps without needing a full cut on every unit
  • Cool-toned face that stays consistent job to job
  • Long service life with almost no maintenance

Timber sleepers & steel

  • Timber eventually rots, bows and needs replacing
  • Steel can corrode in coastal conditions
  • Neither finishes as cleanly on a visible boundary
  • Long runs need regular re-coating or repairs
  • Resale appeal is lower than stone on a garden wall

Questions

Before you book earthworks

Do I need engineering for a retaining wall?
Higher walls usually do, and your council or certifier will tell you the threshold. We'll flag it on the site visit rather than after we've started.
How deep do you need to dig for a wall?
Depends on the height of the wall and what it's holding back. A garden wall and a two-metre retaining wall are completely different footings, and both are worked out before a price is given.
Do you cart the spoil away?
Yes. Excavated material is removed from site as part of the job, and the disposal is included in the quote.
Can you work around an existing house?
Often, yes — access and clearance are the main things. Tell us the access width and we'll say whether the machine can get in or whether it needs to be done by hand.
Can you supply stone only?
Yes. Block, boulder and spall can be supplied and delivered for owner-builders and other trades looking to lay it themselves.
How do I get a price?
Send through the address, roughly how long the wall is or how big the area is, and a photo if you have one. Ring 0434 589 350 and it can usually be worked out on the spot.
