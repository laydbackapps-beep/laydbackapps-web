Quality Pools, the build

A pool is a structure first.

Everything visible, tile, waterline, the way the water sits, is decided by what happened before it. These are the stages a Quality Pools shell goes through, in order.

01Set-out & excavation

The pool is pegged against the house, the boundaries and the levels before anything is dug. Depth, steps and bench are all decided here, not on the day.

02Steel & spray

A tied cage goes in and the shell is sprayed over it, shaped to the set-out. This is the part that carries the water, everything else is finish.

03Render & waterproof

The shell gets rendered fair and made watertight before tile or interior coating goes anywhere near it. Water finds a pinhole, not a wall.

04Waterline, coping & interior

Tile band set level with the water line, coping bedded over the shell, then the interior finish. Level is what makes the water look still.
