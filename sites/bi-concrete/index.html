Scope of works

All aspects of formwork, steelfixing and concrete

Every structure on the job is set out, boxed, tied and poured by the same team. That is what keeps a slab flat, a wall plumb and a program on track.

Formwork

Method

Four steps, in this order, every time

01 / SET OUT

Set out

Datum and pegs, excavation checked, levels confirmed before a form is cut.

02 / FORM

Form & brace

Boxed to line and level, braced against the pour, release agent applied.

03 / FIX

Reinforce

Steel placed, tied and checked against the drawing before the pump is booked.

04 / POUR

Pour & finish

Placed, vibrated, screeded and floated, then cured and stripped clean.
