The three cleans we do

Every home is different, so the first thing we do is find out what shape it is in and what you want out of it — a one-off reset, a regular hand, or a clean that hands the keys back properly.

Deep clean

The full reset for a home that has not had one in a while, or for the parts of a house that never make it onto a normal clean list.

  • Kitchen including oven, range hood and splashback
  • Bathrooms, tiles, grout and glass
  • Skirtings, light switches, sills and frames
  • Inside cupboards, wardrobes and drawers

Regular clean

A routine clean on a set day — weekly, fortnightly or monthly — so the house stays on top of itself instead of needing a rescue every few months.

  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Bathrooms and toilets cleaned
  • Kitchen surfaces and sink
  • Dusting, mirrors and general tidy through

Move-out clean

Built for the handover — a bond clean or an end-of-lease clean on an empty property, worked through so the place reads properly when the inspection happens.

  • Empty-property clean, room by room
  • Oven, cupboards and wardrobes inside
  • Bathroom, laundry and toilet detail
  • Floors, skirtings and marks on walls

What's included

  • Beds made tidy and surfaces cleared as you want them
  • Bin liners, and rubbish taken out at the end

How booking works

Simple version: you tell us about the house, we tell you what it takes and when we can be there, then we turn up and clean it.

1. Message or call

Send the form or ring the number with your suburb, how many bedrooms and bathrooms, and which clean you are after.

2. We quote the clean

You get a clear answer on what the job involves and a price for it — no padded hours you did not ask for.

3. We clean

We turn up when we said we would, work through the list, and you walk around at the end to check it.
