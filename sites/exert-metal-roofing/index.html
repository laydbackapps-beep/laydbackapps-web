What we do.

Metal roofing is the trade, front to back — from stripping an old tile roof and laying new sheeting, to a single leaking flashing on a Friday afternoon.

01

New metal roofs

Old roof off, new Colorbond or Zincalume on: battens checked, new sarking and safety mesh laid, sheeting cut and fixed to the correct spans and laps.

02

Tile-to-tin conversions

Tiles stripped, battens inspected and replaced where they have moved or rotted, then a full metal roof installed — lighter on the frame and far lower maintenance than the tiles it replaces.

03

Roof repairs & replacements

Leaks traced to the actual source — a lifted sheet, a failed flashing, a rusted valley — and fixed, or the section re-sheeted when a repair will not hold.

04

Storm & leak repairs

Storm season does real damage in the Hunter. Sheets re-fixed, penetrations resealed, and roofs made watertight again before the next downpour.

05

Gutters, fascia & downpipes

New guttering and fascia cover sized to the roof, downpipes run to where the water should actually go, and guards fitted where trees are a problem.

06

Commercial roofing

Larger roofs and sheds handled the same way as a house — staged around your operation, with the sheeting, flashings and drainage specified to suit the span.

Why roofs get done right the first time.

A metal roof is a 30-to-50-year decision. Most of our work comes from word of mouth around Newcastle — which is exactly why the details get the attention.

Valleys, flashings and ridge capping are where a roof fails. Getting the sheeting on is easy — the details are what keep the water out.

Straight pricing

Asked before we start.

The questions that come up on almost every roofing quote in Newcastle.

What does a tile-to-tin conversion actually involve?

The old tiles come off and are disposed of, the battens and frame are inspected and replaced where needed, new sarking and safety mesh are laid, and a metal roof is installed over the same frame. It is lighter than the tiles, so the roof structure usually benefits, and you get a low-maintenance roof instead of one that cracks and leaks.

Do you do repairs, or only full roofs?

Both. A single leak, a lifted sheet, a rusted valley or a failed flashing are all worth fixing before they turn into ceiling damage. If a repair will not hold, we will say so and quote the section re-sheet instead.

What areas do you cover?

Newcastle, Lake Macquarie and across the Hunter — from the inner suburbs out to the surrounding towns. Ask if you are not sure whether you are in range.

What roofing materials do you install?

Colorbond and Zincalume steel in the usual profiles — corrugated, trimdek and standing seam — plus the flashings, ridge capping and guttering that go with them.

How long does a re-roof take?

A straightforward house roof is typically a few days on site once the materials are ready. Larger roofs, steep pitches and jobs that need new battens take longer, and we give you a timeframe with the quote.

Is the quote free?

Yes. We come out, get on the roof, measure it properly and give you a written price. No charge for the quote and no obligation to go ahead.
