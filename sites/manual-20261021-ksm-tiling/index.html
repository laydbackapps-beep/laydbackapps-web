How a KSM job actually runs

You know what happens at each stage, when we need a decision from you, and how many days the room is out of use.

01

Measure, set out, quote

Send room sizes or two photos. We confirm tile size, where the set-out lines land, which surfaces are being tiled and what the substrate is doing before we price it.

02

Strip, level, membrane

Old covering out, substrate levelled, screed falls run to the waste and the membrane down in wet areas — checked before a single tile is laid.

03

Large-format floors and mosaic sheets set to a true line, joints grouted, junctions siliconed, the room cleaned and handed back usable.

Tiling FAQ

Bathroom and wet-area tiling: waterproofing, timelines and tiling over old floors

Do you waterproof the bathroom?

Wet-area tiling starts with waterproofing preparation. Membranes, falls to the waste and hob details are sorted before the first tile is laid, and we will not tile over a surface that has not been properly prepared.

How long does a bathroom take?

A typical bathroom runs roughly 4 to 7 working days once the substrate is ready, depending on tile size, layout and how much prep is needed. Small-format and mosaic work takes longer than large-format tiles.

Can you tile over my existing tiles?

Sometimes. If the existing tiles are sound, firmly bonded and the finished floor height still works, tiling over them can be an option. Otherwise the old tiles come up and we start from the substrate.

Do you supply the tiles?

Either way. We are happy to lay tiles you have already chosen and paid for, or we can measure up and order them so the quantities and the layout work together.

How do I get a price?

Send room sizes, what you want tiled and a photo or two through the form below. For bathrooms and wet areas a site measure keeps the quote accurate, and the quote is free.
