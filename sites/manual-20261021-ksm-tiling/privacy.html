Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

What we collect

KSM Tiling collects the details you submit through the quote form — typically your name, phone number, email address and a description of the tiling you need.

Only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and arrange the work. We do not sell, rent or trade your information.

We do not share your details with third parties for marketing. Information may be disclosed where required by law.

Enquiries are kept in our business inbox and job records. You can ask us to correct or delete your details at any time through Instagram direct message to @ksm_tiling.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set advertising cookies. If analytics are added later, this policy will be updated before they are used.
