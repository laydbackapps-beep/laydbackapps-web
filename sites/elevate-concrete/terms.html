Quotes

Quotes from Elevate Concrete are based on the information available at the time, including the size of the job, the access to the site and the finish you have asked for. If conditions on site differ from what we were told, the price may need to be adjusted and we will discuss that with you before starting.

Work and scheduling

Work is booked by agreement. Concrete placing depends on weather, pump and truck availability, and your site being ready and accessible. Wet weather or a delay at a supplier can move a pour date, and we will tell you as soon as we know.

Your site

You are responsible for making sure the area is clear of vehicles, services and obstacles, and for telling us about any underground services or pipes before we start. Council approval or permits, where a driveway crossover or a structure requires them, remain your responsibility unless we have agreed in writing to handle them.

Payment

Payment terms are confirmed in writing with your quote. Concrete is ordered to suit your job, so a deposit can be required before the pour.

Finish and maintenance

Concrete is a natural material. Colour and texture vary from pour to pour, and hairline surface cracking or slight shade variation can occur. We will explain the finish you have chosen and how to look after it, including sealing where that applies.

Liability

Our liability is limited to the value of the work carried out. Nothing in these terms excludes or limits the rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law.
