About this website

This site describes the work Elevate Concrete does and lets you ask for a price. The service lists are a summary of the trade, not a fixed offer, and nothing here is a quote for your job.

Quotes and pricing

A price is only given after the work has been looked at, and it is confirmed in writing (by text or email) before anything starts. If the scope changes after a price is given, the change is priced and agreed separately before it is done.

Photographs

Photos of finished work are shown to give an idea of the standard and type of work. Unless a photo is captioned as Elevate Concrete's own job, it is included as an example of that kind of work.

Enquiries

Sending the quote form does not create a contract. A job begins when a price has been agreed and a start has been booked.
