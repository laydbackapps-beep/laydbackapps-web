How a pour runs

Same order every time, because a slab that is set out badly is a slab that holds water.

Set-out

Levels and pegs taken off your shed or house plans, with the fall you want the water to run.

Formwork

Boards, pegs and steel placed, then checked again before the truck is called in.

Placement

Concrete placed in sections, spread with a screed board and worked down to the pegs.

Finish

Floated, then broomed, exposed or coloured to the finish agreed before the pour.

Cure

Kept damp, cut with control joints and left to cure so the slab comes up without cracking.
