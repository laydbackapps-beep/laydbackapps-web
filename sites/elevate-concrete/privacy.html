Privacy policy

LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 2026

What we collect

Elevate Concrete collects the details you send us through the quote form, by phone or by email. That is normally your name, phone number, email address, the suburb the job is in and a description of the concrete work you want priced.

Why we collect it

Only to answer your enquiry, price the job and arrange the work if you go ahead. We do not sell, rent or trade your details.

Who we share it with

We do not pass your details to anyone for marketing. Information is only disclosed where the law requires it, or to suppliers and subcontractors where that is needed to quote or complete your job.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are stored securely and kept only as long as needed to quote the work and complete it, or for as long as the law requires records to be kept.

Your choices

You can ask to see the details we hold about you, ask for them to be corrected, or ask us to delete them by calling or emailing us. We will action a reasonable request within a few days.
