How a job runs

  1. 01

    Assess & quote

    Site visit to inspect substrates, measure areas and confirm the finish selection, followed by a written quote.

  2. 02

    Prepare & apply

    Surfaces cleaned, keyed, and sealed. Render mixed and applied in passes - float, stipple, sponge, or specialist finish.

  3. 03

    Cure & handover

    Controlled cure period. Final walk-through with the client. Site left clean on completion.
