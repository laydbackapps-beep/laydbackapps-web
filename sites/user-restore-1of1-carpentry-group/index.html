What we build

The work sits between framing and finishing: structures that have to be straight and true, and details that get looked at every day. If it is built from timber and it has to fit, it is our kind of job.

Framing & structural carpentry

Walls, floors and roof framing set out to the plan, with the tolerances that make the trades after us easy.

Decks, pergolas & outdoor timber

Substructure, bearers, joists and decking built for weather and for foot traffic, not just for the photo.

Custom joinery & built-ins

Shelving, robes and cabinetry made to the millimetre of the space, scribed to walls and ceilings that are never quite square.

Fit-out & finishing carpentry

Doors, trims, skirtings, stairs and the final fixing — the parts that decide whether a build looks finished.

How a job runs

Three stages, and nothing gets ordered before the stage before it is settled.

STAGE 01

Measure and listen

Site visit, real measurements, and an honest read on what the structure will let you do.

STAGE 02

Detail and price

Materials, sections and fixing method set out with a fixed price, so the quote matches the build.

STAGE 03

Cut, fit, finish

Built on site to those details, with the offcuts taken away and the joinery left ready to use.

Why one of one

Most carpentry problems are not carpentry problems. They are measuring problems: a cabinet built to a number on a drawing that the wall never agreed with, a deck set out before the fall of the ground was checked.

1OF1 Carpentry Group works the other way around. The space gets measured first, the detail gets settled against those measurements, and the job is built to that — one room, one set of dimensions, one outcome.

“If it is worth building, it is worth measuring twice.”
