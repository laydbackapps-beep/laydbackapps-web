About us

A concreting crew that finishes what it pours

LVL UPP Group Concrete pours slabs, footings, driveways, paths and alfresco areas. The set-out and formwork go in first, the steel is placed to the drawings, and the pour is screened and finished the same day.

Finishes are detailed on the day they are poured, trowelled, broomed or exposed aggregate, with control joints cut once the slab is ready for them.

  • Slabs, footings and pads
  • Driveways, paths and alfresco areas
  • Exposed aggregate and coloured finishes
  • Control joints cut after curing
Common questions

Questions we get asked

Do you do the formwork and steel as well as the pour?

Yes. Set-out, formwork, reinforcement and the pour are all part of the job, so the slab is ready to finish on the day it is poured.

What finishes can you do?

Trowelled, broom and exposed aggregate finishes, along with coloured and stencil options for driveways and paths.

Do you cut the control joints?

Yes. Joints are cut once the slab has cured enough to take the saw.

How do I get a price?

Send the plans, levels or a photo of the area through the quote form, or message us on Instagram or Facebook, and we will come back with a price.
