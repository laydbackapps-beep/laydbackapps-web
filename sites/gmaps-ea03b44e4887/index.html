The ground first

The pour is the easy part.

Most concrete complaints trace back to what happened before the truck arrived. These are the three things worth getting right.

Base
Soft spots dug out, base laid and compacted in layers.Concrete poured over an uncompacted base ends up cracking along whatever it settles into.
Fall
A slab needs somewhere for water to go.Fall away from the building, with drainage sorted before the surface is finished — not after the first downpour.
Joints & cure
Control joints cut where the slab wants to move.Then it's left to cure at a sensible rate instead of drying out hard and fast in the sun.
