Services

What we take on

Four kinds of work, one crew. Small garden trees through to tall removals next to a house or a fence line.

Tree removal

Sectional dismantling over roofs, sheds and power lines. Cuts lowered on rope, not dropped.

Pruning and thinning

Crown reduction, deadwooding and canopy thinning to bring light back through and take weight off limbs.

Stump grinding

Stumps ground out below grade so lawn and paving can go back over the top.

Arborist reports

Written assessments for council consent, development and risk, with the tree recorded and photographed.
Process

How a job runs

Same order every time, from the first look at the tree to the last rake of the driveway.

01

Assess

On site, we read the tree, the ground and what is underneath: wires, gutters, the neighbour's fence.

02

Plan

Rigging points, drop zones and a cut sequence set before anything starts, with the gear matched to the tree.

03

Clear

Cut, lowered, chipped and raked out. Timber can be left for firewood or taken away on request.
