Questions

What people ask before a mulching job

Is the timber burnt or trucked away?

Neither, unless you ask for it. Forestry mulching cuts the standing growth and turns it into chip on the spot, so there are no burn piles to manage and no loads leaving the block.

Will it handle dense lantana and thick regrowth?

That is the work the machine is built for. A mulching head takes scrub, saplings and thicket country down to ground level in passes rather than one bite.

What access does the machine need?

Something workable from the road to the block is usually enough. It gets checked on the site walk and we will tell you upfront if the entry is a problem.

What is left behind when you finish?

Ground-level stumps and a layer of mulch across the surface. New growth does come back on cleared country, so a follow-up pass is normal on blocks that are being kept open.
