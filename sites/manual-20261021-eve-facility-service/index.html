What a bond clean covers

Everything an agent opens a cupboard to check. The clean runs from a written room-by-room checklist, and the same checklist goes out with the invoice.

Kitchen, oven and rangehood

Oven, racks and glass, cooktop, rangehood filter, splashback, cupboard faces inside and out.

Bathrooms and wet areas

Shower screens and tiles descaled, grout, vanity, mirror, toilet, exhaust covers, floor to ceiling.

Floors, skirts and tracks

Vacuum and mop throughout, skirting boards wiped, window tracks and door frames cleared.

Walls, ceilings and fittings

Marks removed where the paint allows, light fittings, switches, air-conditioning and ceiling fans.

Windows and glass

Interior glass cleaned, tracks and sills done, screens dusted down.

Balconies and outdoor areas

Tiled balconies, courtyards and entry areas swept and washed.
