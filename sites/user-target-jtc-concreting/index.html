Our signature

Why exposed aggregate

Exposed aggregate is more than a look. The stone cast into the top of the slab gives the surface grip when it is wet, hides day-to-day staining, and stands up to frost and traffic far better than a plain finish. For a Hobart driveway it is the finish that keeps on going.

JTC washes the surface back the same day to reveal the stone consistently across the whole pour, then seals it. The result is even from the street to the garage door.
