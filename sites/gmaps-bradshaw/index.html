What we polish.

Concrete floors and slabs prepared properly — cut back in measured passes, densified, then sealed. The surface is the product.

01

Concrete polishing

New and existing slabs ground back to a polished finish, with the aggregate showing through and the floor reading like glass.

02

Concrete grinding

Diamond grinding to level the slab, cut back the surface and open the concrete so the polish takes evenly.

03

Sealing & densifying

A penetrating densifier and sealer harden the surface, shut out stains and make the floor easy to sweep and mop.

04

Honed & matte floors

Where a full gloss is not wanted, the same floor honed and sealed to a softer, matte sheen.

Three finishes, one surface.

05

Asked on every floor.

Do you polish existing concrete slabs?
Yes. Existing concrete can be ground back, repaired and polished — it does not have to be a new pour.
What finish can I get?
From a full high-gloss polish through to a honed satin or a flat matte, depending on the slab and the aggregate showing through it.
How is the surface sealed?
The slab is densified, then sealed, so the floor resists staining and wipes clean. The finish is walked with you before handover.
What does it cost?
The price depends on floor size, the condition of the slab and the finish you choose — we give a firm written quote after checking the slab.
Which areas do you cover?
Boolaroo and across Lake Macquarie, Newcastle and the surrounding suburbs — call to confirm your area.
