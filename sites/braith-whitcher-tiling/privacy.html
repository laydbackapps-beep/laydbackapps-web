Privacy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Braith Whitcher Tiling collects only what you enter in the quote form on this site, or what you give on the phone or by email: your name, phone number, email address, the suburb and a description of the work.

Why it is held

To answer the enquiry, measure and quote the job, and keep a record of work done. You are not added to a mailing list and your details are not sold, rented or traded.

Who sees it

  • Braith Whitcher Tiling, and nobody else, unless you ask us to deal with someone on your behalf.
  • The form is delivered by the website's enquiry service, which passes the message through to the business email.
  • Records may be kept where the law requires them, for example for taxation or warranty purposes.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no advertising cookies and runs no analytics or tracking pixels. The typeface is requested from Google Fonts, which means your browser's IP address is visible to Google when the page loads.

Removing your details

Email bwhitchertiling@outlook.com or call 0431 366 357 and ask. We delete what we are not required to keep, and tell you what was removed.

Complaints

Raise it with us first — most of it is fixed in a phone call. Formal privacy complaints can go to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
