Terms

Last updated 19 November 2026

Quotes

Quotes are based on the measurement and the condition of the room at the time of inspection. If the substrate needs work, the tile size changes, or the scope grows, the price is adjusted and you are told before any more tiling is done. Tile and adhesive prices are held for the period stated on the quote.

Site conditions

Tiling starts on a flat, dry, sound substrate. If the screed or sheeting is not ready, the start date moves — laying over a bad base is how a job fails two years later.

Scheduling

Dates are agreed in advance. Time can move for tile supply, other trades not being finished, or a wet area that has not cured. You will be told as soon as a date shifts.

Payment

Payment terms are set out on the quote. A deposit may be required to order tile and materials. Invoices are payable by the terms stated on them.

Workmanship

Tiling is carried out to the relevant Australian Standard for the installation of ceramic and porcelain tiles. Any defect raised within the period stated on the quote is inspected and, where it is our work, rectified.

Liability

Liability is limited to the value of the work performed. Nothing here excludes rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law.
