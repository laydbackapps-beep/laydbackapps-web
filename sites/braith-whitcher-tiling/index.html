Why the number gets shown

Anyone can advertise tiling. A licence number is the part you can check, so it belongs at the top of the page rather than buried in a footer.

  • The licence is held by the business quoting your job.
  • Work is quoted from a site measure, so the price matches what gets laid.
  • One tiler on the job from set-out to grout, not a different crew each day.
Sequence

How a tiling job runs

The order doesn't change much, whether it's one splashback or a whole bathroom.

  1. Step 01

    Measure & quote

    On site, free. Room dimensions, tile size, what's being removed and whether the screed is staying.

  2. Step 02

    Set-out & check

    Centre lines marked, substrate checked for flat, falls confirmed in the wet area before tiling starts.

  3. Step 03

    Lay

    Walls then floor, cuts kept to the edges, joints run true off the set-out line.

  4. Step 04

    Grout & clean

    Grout, silicone to the wet joints, tiles cleaned down and the room left ready for the plumber.
