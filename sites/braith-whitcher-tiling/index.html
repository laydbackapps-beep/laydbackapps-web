01 / Sequence

From datum to done.

A tiling job is a sequence of flat surfaces, clear reference lines and consistent joints. These are the basic stages.

  1. Measure & set out

    Measure the area, establish datum and centre lines, and plan where the cuts and joints will land.

  2. Screed & prepare

    Check the substrate, screed or prepare it where needed, and create a clean, suitable surface for tiling.

  3. Lay & check

    Set tiles to the layout, maintain joint spacing and check alignment and level as the work progresses.

  4. Grout & clean

    Fill the joints with grout, finish the edges and clean the tiled surface.
