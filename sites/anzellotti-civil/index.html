How we work

Set-out to handover

01

Site set-out & chainage

Survey-grade set-out: chainage marks, string lines, level pegs and string-line control for every pour.

02

Sub-base & formwork

Compacted sub-grade, formwork to engineers marks, reinforcement fixed and chaired to cover.

03

Pour, screed & finish

Concrete placed to grade, laser-screeded, finished to spec — broom, trowel, exposed aggregate or coloured.

04

Cure, joints & handover

Wet cure, control joints cut to schedule, site cleaned, handover records issued.
