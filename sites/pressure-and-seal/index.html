Pressure & Seal, the two passes

One job, two separate visits.

Cleaning and sealing are not the same appointment, and trying to run them together is where sealed concrete ends up cloudy or flaking. The slab has to be dry through before any sealer touches it.

01 / CLEAN

Wash the face back

Pressure work lifts the traffic film, moss and grey weathering out of the pores, with a pre-treatment on the stubborn runs and along the joint lines.

02 / DRY

Let it dry through

Cleaned concrete holds moisture for days. Sealer over a damp slab traps that water and goes milky, so the surface gets checked before the drums are opened.

03 / SEAL

Colour sealer, two coats

First coat soaks in and sets the tone, second builds the film. Joints and edges are masked so the sealer stops where the pour stops.

Pressure & Seal, what changes

What a sealed slab actually does.

Sealer is a wear layer, not a repair. It stops water getting in and keeps the colour even, it will not lift out a deep oil stain or fix a cracked slab.

01Water stays on top

Rain and wash-down bead on the surface instead of soaking down into the pores and coming back as damp or efflorescence.

02Colour depth, not paint

A colour sealer deepens the grey the concrete already is. Nothing is painted on, so a chip or scuff shows concrete, not a different colour.

03Joint lines stay put

Sealing over the joints knits the slab face together and stops the edges of the cut breaking down under traffic.

04It wears, and gets re-coated

Sealer is a surface. On a driveway it takes traffic and wants a fresh coat every few years, the clean comes first each time.
