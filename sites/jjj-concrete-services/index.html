How a pour runs

Measured, formed, poured, finished

Concrete does not give you a second go — the order of work is what keeps the finish flat and the joints where they should be.

STEP 01

Call it in

Ring 0409 260 051 with the address and roughly what you need poured.

STEP 02

Measure & quote

Site measured for area, levels and access — including whether the pump is needed to reach the pour.

STEP 03

Form and pour

Formwork set, levels checked, then the concrete placed by chute or line pump and screeded off.

STEP 04

Finish and clean

Float, broom or expose the surface as agreed, cut the joints and leave the site swept.
