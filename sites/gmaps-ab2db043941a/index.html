About

Built on Control Joints

Concrete moves. The art is deciding where. Every slab we pour works to a control-joint grid — saw-cut lines that guide shrinkage cracks to straight, sealed channels instead of random fractures. It's not decoration. It's structural honesty.

Local Knowledge

Based in Hunter NSW. We know the reactive clay sites around Maitland, the sandy coastal strips near Newcastle, and the council specs for every LGA in between.

Right Mix, Right Time

We order to spec — 20/25/32 MPa as the engineer calls it. Admixtures for hot weather, accelerators for cold. No water added on site. Ever.

Joints Cut, Not Cracked

Early-entry saws within the 4–12 hour window. Depth = 1/4 slab thickness. Sealant back-filled. Expansion joints at structural boundaries. That's the standard.

Clean Handover

Site left broom-clean. Curing compound applied. Joint sealant tooled. Documentation handed over — mix dockets, reinforcement certs, pour records.
