What we take on

Domestic and small-commercial tree work around Melbourne. Each job starts with a look at the tree, the fence and the power line before anything is cut.

Full tree removal

Whole trees taken down where they stand — sectioned from the top when there is no room to drop them in one.

Pruning & deadwood

Weight taken out of the crown and dead limbs cleared over roofs, driveways and boundary fences.

Stump grinding

Stumps cut below grade so lawn, paving or new planting can go straight back over the top.

Storm & fallen limbs

Branches already on the ground, or a tree leaning where it shouldn't after weather.

Green waste out

Branches, rounds and sawdust taken away with the job rather than left on the nature strip.

Narrow access

Work planned around cars, carports and side gates where a truck can't sit close.

Look first.
Then cut.

A removal is decided by what is under the tree, not by how big the tree is. Where the shed sits, which way the fence runs, what the wires do above it — that is what sets the order of work.

  • Site walked before quoting. Access, lean, targets and the drop zone agreed on the ground.
  • Sectioned not smashed. Timber lowered in pieces where a straight fell would reach the house or the street.
  • Clean ground at the end. Raked, blown down and loaded out before the crew leaves the address.
