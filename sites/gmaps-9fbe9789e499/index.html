Scope of works

Concrete construction and placement across residential and light-commercial sites in the Hunter region. Work is quoted, formed and finished to suit the site — from a single path to a full slab programme.

1.1Driveways
Vehicle-accessible paving laid to falls, with saw-cut control joints and edging. Plain, coloured or exposed-aggregate surfaces.
1.2Slabs & footings
Shed, tank and house slabs; strip and pad footings formed to line and level, prepared for steel and pour.
1.3Paths & alfresco
Walkways, patios and outdoor areas — broom, stencil or exposed finishes with correct drainage falls.
1.4Decorative finishes
Exposed aggregate, coloured oxides, stencilled and sealed surfaces selected with the client before pour day.
1.5Formwork & earthworks prep
Set-out, formwork, reinforcement placement and sub-base preparation ahead of the pour.
1.6Repairs & replacements
Removal of failed sections and re-pour to match existing levels and finishes where practical.

03
Fig. 03 — Sequence

Method of works

Site inspection & measure

Walk the site, confirm levels, access and intended use. Scope and finish options discussed on site.

Specification & quote

Written scope covering finish, thickness, reinforcement and drainage falls — agreed before works commence.

Set-out & preparation

Excavation to level, sub-base compacted, formwork set to line and steel placed as specified.

Pour & finish

Concrete placed, vibrated and finished to the selected schedule; control joints saw-cut to plan.

Cure & handover

Surface cured and sealed where specified, formwork struck and the site left clean.

Site noteEvery pour is different — access, gradient and ground conditions are confirmed on site before the pour is scheduled.
