What a restoration involves

Oxidised, yellowed lenses are brought back to clear with a multi-stage sand-and-polish, then sealed so they stay that way.

Wet sand & polish

Progressive wet sanding takes off the yellowed, oxidised layer, then the lens is machine polished back to clear.

Oxidation removal

The cloudy film that cuts your night vision is removed from the outside of the lens, left and right, as a pair.

UV / ceramic seal

A UV-stable ceramic top coat goes over the finished lens so it does not haze straight back over in the sun.

Done on the car

The headlights stay in the vehicle: no removal, no re-aim, no waiting on parts. It is a mobile job from start to finish.

Why cloudy headlights matter

Hazed lenses throw less light on the road and glare more for oncoming traffic. Restoring them is a visibility and safety job before it is a looks job.

Headlight restoration questions

What people usually ask before booking a mobile headlight restoration.

What does the restoration involve?

Wet sanding to remove the oxidised, yellowed layer, machine polishing to bring the lens back to clear, then a UV-stable ceramic seal so it does not haze over again.

Do you take the headlights out?

No. The work is done on the car, so there is no removal, no re-aiming and no waiting on parts.

Which vehicles can you do?

Cars, 4WDs, utes and wagons with cloudy or yellowed lenses. If the lens is cracked or full of water rather than hazed, tell us when you send the details.

Where do you work?

Across Newcastle and Lake Macquarie. Send the suburb with your enquiry and we will confirm the visit.
