What Corymbia does

One property. A regenerative focus.

Practical rural contracting centred on lantana removal and the longer-term health of the land and soil beneath it.

01

Lantana removal

Understory taken out, standing trees left where they hold a bank — then mulched so the ground is walkable and drivable instead of a slash heap.

02

Follow-up, not one pass

Lantana comes back from seed and root. The second look is about what regrows and where, so the block stays clear past one season.

03

Soil left covered

Ground stays covered instead of scraped bare, so soil and seedbed hold through the next wet rather than washing down the slope.
