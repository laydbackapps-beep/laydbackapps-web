What Eaton covers

The jobs worth getting right the first time.

Scope, pricing and timing are agreed directly with Eaton — every job is different, so we'd rather ask than assume.

Switchboards & safety

Board replacement and upgrades, RCD and safety-switch protection, plus circuits labelled so the next fault starts from a known point.

Lighting & power

Downlights that stop flickering and buzzing, weatherproof outdoor points, and GPOs where the appliances actually sit — not where the old wall was.

Fault finding

Breakers that trip the moment something else turns on, half-dead powerpoints, lights that pulse — tested circuit by circuit instead of swapping parts until it stops.

Fit-outs & additions

New circuits for renos, sheds, caravans and granny flats, sized for the load the place will carry rather than the smallest cable that passed.
