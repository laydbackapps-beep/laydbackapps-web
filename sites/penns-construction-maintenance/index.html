What we do.

Sheds are the backbone of the business, and the same crew handles the frames, the fit-out and the maintenance that follows.

01

Sheds

Steel-framed sheds built and erected — slab set-out, frame stood and squared, roof and wall sheeting on, and the doors hung and adjusted.

02

Carports & patios

Carports and patios framed and roofed to suit the house — posts set on proper footings, beams sized for the span, and the roof tied in and flashed to the existing building.

03

Extensions & structures

Framed extensions, rooms and outbuildings taken from the frame through to the lining and fit-out, so the new space is weatherproof and usable.

04

Construction work

General construction and carpentry for a wide range of jobs, small and large — framing, sheeting, linings and the fix-out that follows.

05

Property maintenance

The maintenance half of the business: repairs, adjustments and the jobs that keep a building working — from a stuck door to a leaking flashing.

06

Shed design & layout

Shed layouts and designs worked through before a frame is ordered, so the size, door placement and position on the block are right the first time.

Asked before we start.

The questions that come up on almost every shed and carport quote.

Do you build sheds, carports and patios?

Yes — sheds, carports and patios are the core of the work, along with the framed extensions and general construction around them. If you are not sure whether a job is one we take on, call and ask.

Do you handle the approvals?

Sheds and carports above a certain size normally need approval, and the position on the block matters as much as the size. We work the layout and drawings through with you before anything is ordered, so the build does not run into a problem later.

How long does a shed take to build?

It depends on size, site access and whether the slab is already down. A standard shed is typically a matter of days on site once the materials and approvals are in hand. You get a timeframe with the quote.

Do you do smaller maintenance jobs as well?

Yes. Maintenance is half the business — repairs and adjustments on existing buildings, not just new builds. No job is too big or too small.

What areas do you cover?

We are based on the Gold Coast and work across the surrounding area. Call or message with your suburb and we will tell you straight away.

Is the quote free?

Yes. We look at the site and give you a written price. No charge for the quote and no obligation to go ahead.
