How we finish

Hand-tool flushing,
joint by joint

// ON THE TOOLS - ceiling sheets fixed square, joints ready for tape and flush

  • Every joint gets paper tape bedded in compound - no mesh shortcuts, no visible ridges.
  • Three-coat flush: bed, fill, finish - each coat feathered wider, sanded between coats.
  • Hand tools only - broad knives and trowels, not spray texture. The finish reads flat under critical light.
  • Cornice snapped clean across the top - mitred returns, stopped ends, no gaps.
  • 20 years on the tools - residential and commercial plasterboard, installed and finished.
