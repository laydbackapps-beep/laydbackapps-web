Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

What we collect

Probuild Constructions Pty Ltd collects the details you give us when you send an enquiry: your name, your email address, the site or suburb, and a description of the work you need.

Why we collect it

Only to answer your enquiry, prepare a quote and arrange the work. We do not sell, rent or trade your information.

Who we share it with

We do not share your details with third parties for marketing. Information may be disclosed where the law requires it.

Storage

Enquiries are held securely and kept only as long as needed for the quote and any resulting work, or as required by law.

Contact

Probuild Constructions Pty Ltd. The quickest way to reach us is a message to our Facebook page, linked from the contact section of the site.
