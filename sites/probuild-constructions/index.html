Asked before we start.

The questions that come up on almost every basement and renovation quote.

What makes a basement legal in Oakville?

A legal secondary suite has to meet the Ontario Building Code and the town's zoning: minimum ceiling height, an egress window in each sleeping room, fire separation between the suite and the rest of the house, and a building permit with inspections. We assess the basement against those requirements before quoting, so you know what the job actually needs.

Do you handle the permit and drawings?

Yes. Where the work needs a building permit, the drawings and application are part of the job, and we coordinate the inspections through to sign-off. For work that does not need a permit we will say so plainly rather than charging for paperwork you do not need.

How long does a basement conversion take?

A straightforward basement finish runs a number of weeks on site once permits are in hand; a legal suite with a bathroom, kitchenette and separate entrance takes longer. You get a schedule with the quote, and the critical path — permit, rough-in inspections, drywall — is set out so you know what is holding what up.

What drives the cost up or down?

Ceiling height, whether the basement is already waterproofed, how far services have to be moved, the number of wet areas, and cutting an egress window through a foundation wall. Those change the price far more than finishes do, so they are what we look at on the site visit.

Do you take on smaller jobs?

Yes — a single bathroom, a kitchen refresh, or a wall removal. Smaller jobs are often the quickest way to see how a contractor works before committing to a full basement.

Where do you work?

Oakville and Halton first, then the rest of the GTA — Mississauga, Burlington, Milton, Etobicoke and Toronto — plus jobs further out such as King City when the project suits.
