One trade, every timber detail

New work or old, the brief is the same: measure it properly, set it out square, and fix it so it stays that way. We work directly with homeowners and alongside builders who need a carpenter they do not have to check up on.

  • Carpentry

    Framing, roof members, floor joists, sub-floor and structural timber set out off the drawings and braced as it goes up.

  • Renovations

    Kitchens, bathrooms, laundry and living areas stripped back and rebuilt, with the structure sorted before anything is sheeted.

  • Custom builds

    One-off structures, additions and small builds where the timber work is the build. Plans, engineering and certification handled with you.

  • Decks and pergolas

    Bearers, joists, decking, stairs, handrails and roofed structures in hardwood, treated pine or composite.

  • Fit-outs

    Shopfronts, offices and tenancies: partitions, counters, shelving, doors and trims finished to a commercial standard.

  • Doors, trims and fix-out

    Hanging, hardware, architraves, skirting, cornice and lining up. The last ten percent that shows.

How a job runs

Timber does not forgive a rough start. This is the order we work in, on a garden deck or a full custom build, so there are no surprises halfway through.

  • Walk the site and read the plansLevels, services, access and what has to move before we start. You get the scope and the sequence in writing.
  • Set out and check squareStrings, levels and diagonals fixed before a single member is cut. Get this wrong and nothing downstream fits.
  • Frame and braceMembers cut to the schedule, fixed and braced so the frame stays where it was put.
  • Rough-in and servicesOpenings, blocking and noggins placed for plumbing, electrical and whatever gets hung later.
  • Line, fix and finishSheeting, doors, trims, decking or cladding installed and adjusted so joints close and lines run true.
  • Snag, clean and hand overWe walk it with you, fix what you pick up, and leave the site swept. Nothing is left for you to finish.
