What we do

Every job runs on the same discipline: clean seams, correct fixings, proper laps. No shortcuts.

01

New metal roofing

Standing-seam and corrugated systems installed to manufacturer spec. Colorbond, Zincalume and custom profiles.

02

Re-roofing

Full strip and replacement. Old tiles or sheeting removed, substrate checked, new metal system fitted with flashings.

03

Roof repairs

Leaks, loose sheets, damaged flashings, storm damage. Fast response, proper fix - not a patch job.

04

Roof maintenance

Scheduled inspections, gutter clearing, fixing tightening, sealant renewal. Extends service life, prevents callbacks.

Eave flashing detail

Apron flashing turned down into the gutter, rib ends closed with stop-ends, fixings sealed at the pan. A single straight seam line - no water track, no wicking.
