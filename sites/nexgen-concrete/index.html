The approach

One integrated contractor, one accountable system — programme, quality and cost controlled from the ground up.

Exclusive finishes

Decorative concrete, engineered to last

Driveways, paths, patios and pool surrounds — durable, slip resistant and low maintenance.

Finish 01

Pattern cove

Hand-trowelled arcs in a refined, repeating motif — quiet rhythm and confident grip. A sophisticated texture for alfresco areas and porticos, offered in bespoke colours and sealed for easy care.

Finish 02

Exposed aggregate

Natural stone revealed within the concrete for a luxurious, slip-resistant surface. Tailor the blend and colour to your architecture — enduring, low maintenance, and perfect for driveways, entries and pool surrounds.

Finish 03

Random cove

Organic free-flowing cove swirls deliver a one-of-a-kind artisan look with excellent barefoot comfort. Subtle movement disguises everyday marks and elevates paths, patios and pool decks.

Finish 04

Honed

Diamond-honed to a satin finish that reveals a delicate "salt and pepper" of aggregate. Clean architectural lines indoors and out, protected by a premium penetrating sealer.

Finish 05

Stamped

Imprinted patterns capture the depth of stone, slate or timber in a single continuous pour — rich texture and tone with the durability of concrete and the elegance of premium paving.

How we work

Control from the ground up

Planning discipline, coordination and an understanding of structural performance under load.

Step 01

Plan & coordinate

Scope, sequencing and reinforcement coordination set before anything breaks ground — so the programme holds.

Step 02

Excavate & prepare

Site cuts, footings, trenching and detailed prep work, executed precisely to set the project up properly.

Step 03

Form & reinforce

Formwork and reinforcement placed and checked ahead of the pour — the structure the finish depends on.

Step 04

Place, pump & finish

Concrete placement and line pumping with our own equipment and operators, then the finish — decorative or heavy-duty.
