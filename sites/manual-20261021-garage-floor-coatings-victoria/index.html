Coating systems

Every job starts at the slab: grind it back, fix what has failed, then build the system up coat by coat. Flake, metallic or solid colour — matched to how hard the floor actually works.

Surface prep & grinding

The whole slab is diamond ground back to an open CSP profile the coating can key to, with dust extraction run off a vac so the garage stays usable.

Flake broadcast systems

Full-broadcast vinyl flake into the wet base coat, sealed under two clear topcoats. Hides slab imperfections and puts grip underfoot.

Solid colour & metallic

High-build solid colour for a clean uniform floor, or metallic pigment swirled through the topcoat for a marbled sheen.

Crack, spall & oil repair

Cracks routed and filled with epoxy mortar, spalled edges rebuilt and oil contamination ground out before any coating goes down.

Moisture test & primer

Slab moisture read, then a penetrating primer rolled on once the concrete is clean and sound, so the build coats bond instead of blistering.

Topcoat, cure & handover

Gloss, satin or anti-slip topcoats including UV-stable options, with cure times in writing: foot traffic in 24 hours, vehicles after full cure.

How a floor gets done

Five stages, in this order, every time. Check the finished floor against it.

  1. 01

    Slab check & moisture test

    The concrete, its moisture reading and any existing coating get checked first, then the job is quoted off what the slab actually needs.

  2. 02

    Grind & repair

    Diamond grinding to an open profile, cracks routed and filled, spalled edges rebuilt, dust controlled throughout.

  3. 03

    Prime & base coat

    A penetrating primer, then the pigmented base coat rolled out to an even film build — the layer the flake holds to.

  4. 04

    Flake broadcast

    Flake broadcast to refusal into the wet base coat and scraped back once set — full coverage, no thin patches.

  5. 05

    Topcoat, cure & handover

    Two clear topcoats, cure times in writing, and a walkthrough on cleaning and looking after the floor.

Coating questions

What people usually ask before booking a garage floor coating.

What epoxy finishes can I choose from?

Flake, metallic or solid colour epoxy. Flake gives a speckled, slip-resistant surface, metallic a marbled sheen, and solid colour one flat finish for a clean working floor.

How long before we can park on it?

Foot traffic in around 24 hours and vehicles once the coating has fully cured. Grinding, priming, base coat, flake broadcast and two topcoats run over consecutive days, and the cure time for your system goes in writing at handover.

Which finish suits a working garage?

Flake for a hard-wearing speckled surface that hides dust and marks, metallic for a feature floor, solid colour for a clean uniform working bay. Sheds, workshops and commercial bays are quoted the same way.
