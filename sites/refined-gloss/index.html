The process

A considered, step-by-step approach.

01 / ASSESSMENT

Inspect

We inspect paint condition, defects and overall finish to determine the appropriate level of correction.

02 / DECON

Decontaminate

A thorough wash and paint decontamination removes bonded contaminants and prepares the surface.

03 / CORRECT

Correct

Machine polishing reduces swirl marks, oxidation and defects — restoring clarity, depth and gloss.

04 / PROTECT

Coat

A high-performance ceramic coating enhances gloss, hydrophobic performance and long-term protection.

05 / FINAL

Inspect again

Every vehicle receives a detailed final inspection to ensure a refined finish before handover.
