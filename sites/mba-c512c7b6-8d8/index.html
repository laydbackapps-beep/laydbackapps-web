Scope of works

Four courses of the one build.

General building works across the residential and small commercial scope — each job set out, supervised and handed over by the same firm that quoted it.

Course I

New homes

Ground-up residential builds — setout, structure and finish, coordinated through a single point of contact from slab to handover.

Course II

Renovations

Alterations and additions to existing dwellings, worked to the fabric of the original structure and finished as one coherent whole.

Course III

Extensions

New floor area and living space added to existing homes — structurally integrated, weather-tight and detailed to match.

Course IV

Project management

Coordination of trades, approvals and program on building projects — keeping scope, sequence and budget on one ledger.

How a build runs

Every project set the same way — from the keystone down.

01

Site meeting

We walk the site, hear the brief and record what the land, access and existing structure ask of the design.

02

Scope & estimate

A written scope and a clear estimate — what is included, what is excluded, and where the decision points sit.

03

Approvals & program

Construction certificates, certifications and a build program sequenced so each trade follows the last without idle days.

04

Build & handover

Works supervised on site, progress kept visible, and handover delivered with the finish checked course by course.
