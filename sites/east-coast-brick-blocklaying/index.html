01 — Set out

Set out to the bond.

Brickwork only reads right when the bond is set out first. The bond decides how the bricks lap, where the piers fall and how the joints break course to course — and it is the first thing we set out on a Newcastle job.

03 — Materials & method

Brick, block, mortar and how it goes down.

The face of a wall is decided before the first brick is laid: the brick, the mortar, the joint profile and where the articulation joints fall.

  • Bricks laid to line and gauge. Each course is run off the profile and checked against a taut string line, so the joints stay even the length of the wall.
  • Mortar beds and joints. Mortar spread to a true bed and joints finished to match — flush, ironed or raked, kept consistent through the face.
  • Quoins and corners. Corners run up with quoins formed square, so the reveal stays sharp and the next course sits true.
  • Articulation joints. Long walls are broken with articulation joints so movement is taken up instead of cracking the face.
  • Core-fill and reinforcement. Piers, corners and returns are cored and grouted solid where the wall needs the strength.
  • Damp course. A damp course is laid at the base so moisture is cut off before it climbs the wall.

05 — Method

How a job runs.

Straightforward bricklaying, from the first call to a cleaned-down wall.

  1. 01

    Enquire

    Call or send through what you are after — a fence, a wall, a pier or house brickwork. Bricks, height and access all shape the price.

  2. 02

    Set out & quote

    We look at the job, the brick and the bond, work out where the piers and joints fall, and give you a clear price before anything starts.

  3. 03

    Lay to line

    The bond is set out, the first course run true, and each course laid to the string line with the gauge held off the profile.

  4. 04

    Core-fill & cap

    Piers and corners are core-filled where needed, articulation joints left where they should be, and the wall capped off neat.

  5. 05

    Clean down

    Brickwork washed down, mortar off the face, and the site left tidy when the job is finished.
