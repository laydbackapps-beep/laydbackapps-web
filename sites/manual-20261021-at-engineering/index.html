Custom fabrication, engineering and delivery

AT Engineering is a hands-on fabrication and engineering team based at Whitton in the NSW Riverina. We work with mining, agriculture, manufacturing, meat processing and food production clients, and we are set up to take a job from design through to manufacture and delivery.

The workshop handles stainless tank and vessel fabrication, structural steel, agricultural equipment, conveying and material handling systems, plus CNC cutting, folding and forming, so the work stays under one roof from drawing to dispatch.

  • Stainless steel and tank fabrication in house
  • Structural steel, platforms and shed frames
  • Agricultural equipment and custom modifications
  • CNC cutting, folding and forming
  • Projects delivered across Eastern Australia
