What we do

Six things, done properly

Nothing is quoted off a photo. The board or the circuit gets looked at, then the number gets given.

01

Switchboard upgrades

Ceramic fuses and old panel boards replaced with RCBO protection. Circuits identified, tested and labelled, and the reasons for each change explained before the power goes off.

02

Fault finding

Safety switch tripping, half a house dead, a circuit that only fails in the rain. Insulation resistance and continuity testing to find the actual fault instead of replacing parts until it stops.

03

Powerpoints & circuits

New outlets in kitchens, garages and sheds, dedicated circuits for appliances, and weatherproof outlets outdoors with the right enclosure and protection.

04

Lighting

Downlights, battens, exterior and sensor lighting, exhaust fans and dimming — including replacing halogen fittings and fixing the insulation clearance around them.

05

Air-conditioning & appliance wiring

Dedicated circuits, isolators and cable sizing to the unit's spec, so the air-con or oven runs on its own protection rather than an overloaded general circuit.

Solar & EV readiness

Board capacity, spare ways and cabling checked before an inverter or charger goes on, so the addition does not mean rebuilding the board a second time.

Questions

The ones asked most

Why does my safety switch keep tripping?
Something on that circuit is leaking to earth — often a failing appliance, water in an outdoor outlet, or a damaged cable. It is found by testing the circuit and then each item on it, not by replacing the switch.
Do I need a new switchboard?
If the board still has ceramic fuses, no safety switches, or no spare ways and it is full of double-ups, it needs replacing. If it already has RCBOs and spare capacity, a repair is usually the cheaper answer.
Can you add a powerpoint outside?
Yes. Outdoor outlets need a weatherproof enclosure and their own protection, and the cable route has to suit the wall or the slab it runs on.
Do you issue certificates?
Yes — a Certificate of Electrical Safety is issued for the work where one is required, and the test results stay with the job.
Can you work with an existing solar or air-con install?
Yes, provided the existing wiring is up to it. Board capacity and cable sizing are checked first, and anything that fails is shown to you before it is altered.
