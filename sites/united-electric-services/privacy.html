Privacy Policy

This website is a preview for United Electric Services, an electrical contractor in Newcastle, NSW. It explains, in plain terms, what happens to the information you send us.

What we collect

When you use the quote request form we collect the details you enter — your name, a phone number or email address, and a description of the work you need. Nothing more is collected than you choose to provide.

How it is used

Your details are used only to respond to your enquiry and to prepare a quote. They are not sold, rented or shared with third parties for marketing.

Storage

Enquiry details are stored securely and kept only as long as needed to deal with your request and any resulting work.

Contact

To ask about the information you have sent, or to have it removed, message the business on Instagram at @unitedelectricservices_.
