Questions

Before you book

Do you soft wash or pressure wash?

Both. Delicate surfaces like roofs and painted render get a low-pressure soft wash; concrete and hardstand take pressure.

Can you quote from a photo?

Usually, yes. Send a photo of the surface with the enquiry and we can price most jobs without a site visit.

Do you clean solar panels?

Yes — panels are cleared of leaf litter, dust and grime so they can perform properly again.
