Plant hire questions

What people usually ask before booking plant with Corfe Plant Solutions.

Do you hire with or without an operator?

Both. Dry hire if you have your own crew and tickets, or wet hire with an experienced operator if you would rather hand the seat over and get on with the rest of the build.

What plant and equipment do you hire?

Excavators for trenching, footing cuts and general earthmoving, tipper trucks for cartage, and attachments including buckets, augers, breakers and grabs.

Where do you deliver?

Around Grafton and the Clarence Valley, with machines floated to and from your site so the plant arrives when the job is ready for it.
