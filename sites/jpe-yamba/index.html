How a job runs.

From the first call to the final test, here is how a job runs.

01

Get in touch

Call or message with what is happening and where the property is. A photo of the switchboard or the fault often saves a trip.

02

Look & quote

We look at the job, work out what it needs and put the price in writing before any cable is pulled.

03

Set out & rough in

Circuits are run and set out to the plan or the layout, with the cable protected and the work left tidy each day.

04

Connect & test

Fittings go on, circuits are connected, and everything is tested — polarity, insulation resistance and safety switches — before it is handed back.

05

Sign off

The job is left working and labelled, with the certificate and paperwork sorted and no loose ends.

Asked before we start.

The questions that come up on almost every electrical quote.

What areas do you cover?

Yamba and the Clarence Valley and surrounding areas. If you are further out, call or message with your suburb and we will tell you straight away.

Do you do commercial and industrial work as well as houses?

Yes — domestic, commercial and industrial. That covers everything from a house full of power points to a commercial fit-out and its maintenance.

Can you upgrade an old fuse box?

Yes. Old fuse boxes and re-wireable boards are replaced with modern switchboards with safety switches and correct circuit protection, and the circuits labelled so they make sense.

Do you do new builds and renovations?

Yes — rough-in and fit-off for new builds, and rewires and new circuits for renovations and extensions, set out to the plan and tested at the end.

Is the quote free?

Yes. Tell us what the job is and we will give you a written price. No charge for the quote and no obligation to go ahead.

Do you take on small jobs?

Yes. A single power point, a light that will not work or a tripping circuit are all worth doing properly — small jobs are a big part of the work.
