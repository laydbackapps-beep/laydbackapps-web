When it gets done

The work follows the season, not a fixed calendar — this is roughly what each part of the year looks like.

Spring

  • First big cut of the season
  • Hedge shaping before growth takes off
  • Mulch and bed tidy

Summer

  • Fortnightly mowing
  • Gutter check before storms
  • Irrigation and watering checks

Autumn

  • Leaf and debris clearing
  • Gutter clean-out
  • Pruning before dormancy

Winter

  • Reduced mowing rounds
  • Drainage and water damage fixes
  • Planning the spring jobs
