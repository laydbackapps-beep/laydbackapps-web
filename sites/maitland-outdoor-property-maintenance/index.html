Who does the work

Small local crew, not a franchise call centre — the person who quotes the job is the person on the mower.

Maitland Outdoor & Property Maintenance looks after yards, blocks and small commercial grounds around Maitland and the villages either side of it. Most of the work is routine: a regular mowing round, hedges kept to a line, the gutters cleared before the storms, and a tidy-up when a place needs to look sharp for a sale or an inspection.

The rest is the sort of work that has no trade of its own. A gate that won't swing, a letterbox knocked over, a fence paling or two that let go in the wind, mulch that needs spreading before summer, a garden bed that has filled in with weeds. None of it is a big job on its own, which is exactly why it tends to sit on the list for months.

Everything is quoted off what the property actually needs — how big the block is, how overgrown it has got and how easy it is to get a mower and a trailer to the back. If a place is a lot worse than it was in the last photo, that gets said straight rather than padded out later.

Green waste goes with us. Clippings, prunings, hedge cuttings and the bagged debris out of the gutters are loaded and taken away, so nothing is left sitting in a pile against the fence waiting for a council clean-up that is not booked.

Rounds are worked so the same few streets fall on the same day, which keeps the price down and means you can plan around it. One-off visits are just as welcome if you would rather handle the small stuff yourself and only call when it gets away from you.

Asked before most jobs

Do you take the green waste away?
Yes. Grass clippings, prunings, hedge cuttings and the debris out of the gutters are loaded on the trailer and removed — nothing is left piled up behind the shed.
Can you do a one-off clean-up instead of a regular round?
Yes. One-off visits are common, particularly before a sale, a rental inspection or a family gathering. If it turns into something you want kept on top of, a regular day can be set from there.
How big a block do you take on?
Standard suburban yards through to larger acreage blocks, plus small commercial and strata grounds. If access makes a difference to what the job needs, that is worked out before a price is given.
What do you need to quote?
The address, roughly how big the block is, and whether you want a regular visit or a one-off. If it is a regular round, you will be told which day of the week it falls on.
