02 — who does the work

A small local crew, not a call centre

The person who quotes the job is the person on the mower. Maitland Outdoor & Property Maintenance looks after yards, blocks and small commercial grounds around Maitland and the villages either side of it.

Most of the work is routine: a regular mowing round, hedges kept to a line, the gutters cleared before the storms, and a tidy-up when a place needs to look sharp for a sale or an inspection.

The rest is the sort of work that has no trade of its own. A gate that won't swing, a letterbox knocked over, a fence paling or two that let go in the wind, mulch that needs spreading before summer, a garden bed that has filled in with weeds.

None of it is a big job on its own, which is exactly why it tends to sit on the list for months.

04 — rounds & pricing

Priced off the property, not a menu

Everything is quoted off what the property actually needs — how big the block is, how overgrown it has got and how easy it is to get a mower and a trailer to the back. If a place is a lot worse than it was in the last photo, that gets said straight rather than padded out later.

Green waste goes with us. Clippings, prunings, hedge cuttings and the bagged debris out of the gutters are loaded and taken away, so nothing is left sitting in a pile against the fence waiting for a council clean-up that is not booked.

Rounds are worked so the same few streets fall on the same day, which keeps the price down and means you can plan around it.

One-off visits are just as welcome if you would rather handle the small stuff yourself and only call when it gets away from you.

06 — questions

Asked before most quotes

Do you do regular rounds or one-off visits?
Both. Most properties settle into a round — weekly or fortnightly in the growing months — and drop back over winter. One-offs are fine too, and they are priced the same way.
What happens to the clippings and green waste?
They leave with us. Mowing, hedge cuttings, prunings and anything bagged out of the gutters are loaded and taken away, so the property is clean when we go and nothing is left against the fence.
Can you get a trailer to the back yard?
It depends on the block, and it is one of the things that gets looked at before quoting. Narrow side access, steps or a steep drive changes the method, not whether the job can be done.
Do you look after commercial grounds?
Yes — small commercial and strata grounds on a set schedule, worked outside trading hours where it matters.
How is the price worked out?
Block size, how overgrown it is, and access for the mower and trailer. It is quoted as a price for the work, not a rate that drifts once the job starts.
Are you insured?
Yes, and the certificate can be provided with the quote.
