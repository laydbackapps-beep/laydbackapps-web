03

Asked on every job.

Do you chip the tree on site or haul it away?
We chip on site by default - the mulch is yours for beds or paths. If you want it removed, we load and take it.
Can you work near houses, fences or power lines?
Yes - sectional rigging and controlled drops let us work in tight spots. Power line clearance follows Western Power rules.
Is the mulch fresh or aged?
Fresh from your tree. It breaks down fast, feeds soil and holds moisture. For formal beds we can screen fines on request.
Do you grind stumps below grade?
Standard is 150-200 mm below grade. Deeper on request - ready for turf, pavers or a new tree.
What areas do you cover?
Perth metro and outer suburbs - north to Yanchep, south to Mandurah, east to Mundaring.
Can you clear a whole overgrown block?
Yes - scrub and regrowth are mulched in place or loaded out. Tell us the block size and the access and we will quote the clearing.

Tree in, mulch out.

The chipper goes where the tree comes down. Arisings are chipped on site, then the mulch is either spread on your beds or loaded out - and the grindings fill the stump hole so the ground is level when we leave.

1. Fell

Sectional drops or a straight fell, rigged where the tree is tight against the house, fence or lines.

2. Chip on site

Fed through the chipper as it comes down - no hauling whole limbs off the block, no wait on a truck.

3. Mulch & level

Mulch spread where you want it, stump ground below grade and the grindings raked back over the hole.
