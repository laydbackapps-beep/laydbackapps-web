  • Sand-cement base float plus tinted acrylic texture topcoats

DISCIPLINE 02 · ARCHITECTURAL MOULDINGS

Rusticated Pillars, Quoins & Entry Porticos

Custom stepped columns, recessed horizontal banding, and EPS profile detailing under steel porch beams and carport soffits. Each groove is set out with laser levels for uniform shadow lines from footing to beam.

  • Fibreglass alkali-resistant mesh embedded across all EPS profiles

  • Stone-effect and custom architectural render detailing

Internal Float, White-Set & Renovation Work

  • Wet-area cement render screeds prepared true for tiling
