Pergolas & alfresco structures, Port Stephens

MWD Carpentry builds timber pergolas, verandahs and covered outdoor areas around Corlette and Port Stephens — framed square, tied into the house properly and finished to suit the block.

Do you build pergolas and verandahs?

Yes — pergolas, verandahs, covered alfresco areas and the screening that goes with them. Framed and fixed to the structure, not propped up.

Can you match an existing house?

Timber species, profiles and finishes are matched to what is already there so the new work reads as part of the house rather than an add-on.

Do you handle the framing as well?

Yes. Roof and wall framing, posts, beams and bracing are all part of the job — one carpenter from the frame through to the finish.

Do you work on holiday rentals?

Yes. A lot of Port Stephens work is on rentals and second homes, so jobs are scheduled and finished to a date.

What this covers

  • Timber pergolas and alfresco structures
  • Verandahs and covered outdoor areas
  • Posts, beams, bracing and roof framing
  • Timber slat screening and privacy
  • Balustrades and steps
  • Repairs and re-oiling on existing structures
