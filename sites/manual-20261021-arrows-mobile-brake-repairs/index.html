Brakes, done on site

No workshop drop-off, no waiting room, no catching a lift home. We bring the tools and the parts to the car.

Brake pads

Front and rear pads pulled, measured and replaced, with the rotor checked while the wheel is off.

Rotors & discs

Scored, lipped or warped rotors replaced or machined so the pedal comes back smooth underfoot.

Brake fluid flush

Old fluid bled out of each corner and replaced, then the pedal bled up properly before the car goes back.

Calipers, lines & hoses

Seized or weeping calipers freed or swapped, perished hoses and rusty lines renewed.

Handbrake & park brake

Cable, shoe and lever adjustment so the park brake actually holds on a slope.

Inspection & diagnosis

Squeal, shudder, a soft pedal or a light on the dash — traced to the cause before anything gets swapped.
