What we pour

Every job starts with the ground it sits on. Base, fall and steel are decided before a truck is booked, because that is what a driveway or slab is judged on years later.

  1. 01

    Driveways & crossovers

    Set out to the street fall, formed, steeled and finished broom or aggregate to suit the house.

  2. 02

    House & shed slabs

    Excavated to the detail, compacted, steel on chairs, poured and cut in the same week.

  3. 03

    Exposed aggregate

    Washed back evenly across the pour so the stone reads consistently from the street to the door.

  4. 04

    Paths, steps & ramps

    Laid on a compacted base with the fall running away from the building.

  5. 05

    Retaining & sleeper walls

    Footings sized to the height, with drainage and gravel behind every wall.

  6. 06

    Removal & replacement

    Old concrete broken out, carted and replaced — including the base that failed under it.

How a pour runs

Four stages, in this order, every time — the sequence is why the finish holds.

Stage 01

See the site

Access, levels and what is under the surface are checked before a price is given.

Stage 02

Prep the base

Excavated, compacted and formed square — the part that decides whether the slab moves.

Stage 03

Pour and finish

Steel checked, concrete placed, screeded to fall and finished while it is workable.

Stage 04

Cut and clean

Control cuts made at the right time, edges cleaned and the site left tidy.
