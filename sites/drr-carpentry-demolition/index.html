D•R•R carpentry and demolition

Based on the Sunshine Coast, D•R•R carpentry and demolition is a hands-on crew of carpenters and demolition contractors. We take on framing, decks, pergolas, renovations, fitouts and strip-outs — and we pull down houses, sheds and garages — finishing every job with the site swept clean.

Our approach is direct: a clear scope, an honest price, and a tidy site at the end of every day. Whether it is a deck out the back, a kitchen stripped back to the frame, or a shed taken down and hauled away, we treat every job the same way.

  • Fully insured and licensed
  • Own tools, ute and trailer — no waiting on hire
  • Careful work around occupied homes and existing structures
  • Site kept safe, tidy and cleared as we go
