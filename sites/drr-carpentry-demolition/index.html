What we do

Demolition and carpentry on the same ticket, so nothing sits half finished waiting on a second trade to turn up.

Strip-out

Internal strip-outs ahead of a renovation: lining, joinery, wet-area tiles, flooring and services made safe, taken out and sorted for disposal.

House demolition

Houses and outbuildings brought down, footings and slabs broken out, materials separated and the block left clear and swept.

Framing

Wall and roof framing set out and stood square, bracing and tie-downs done to the plan, and the frame left straight for the sheeting trade.

Decks

Bearers and joists fixed back to the house, decking laid with consistent gaps and the edges finished off so the boards stay tight.

Renovations

Second-fix carpentry, doors, architraves, skirtings and shelving, working in behind the other trades and keeping the house liveable where we can.

Rubbish removal

Bins arranged, the load sorted so the green waste and the hard waste do not go out together, and the site left tidy at the end of each day.

How a job runs

01 — WALK THE SITE

Look at it before we price it

What is load bearing, what is inside the wall, where the trucks can get to and where the bins sit. The quote comes off that, not off a description over the phone.

02 — STRIP AND DEMOLISH

Controlled, not smash and grab

Anything being kept is protected or taken out clean, services are isolated, and the demolition is worked so the rest of the house and the neighbours are not damaged.

03 — REBUILD

Carpentry straight behind the demolition

Framing, decks and the fit-out go in while the job is still fresh, so the site is not left opened up waiting for another crew.

04 — CLEAR UP

Bins gone, site swept

Rubbish is loaded out as the job goes, and the last pass is a sweep and a walk-through with you before we call it done.

Can you take the house down and rebuild it?

That is the usual job. Strip-out, demolition and the carpentry that follows come off the one crew, so the gap between the demolition and the rebuild is as short as it can be.

Do you organise the bins?

Yes. Bins are arranged as part of the job and the load is sorted so hard waste and green waste do not go out together.

Will the quote change once you start?

Not for the scope we priced. If you add work, or something is found behind the lining that nobody could see, we stop and price that change before doing it.

Are you on site every day of the job?

On the demolition and carpentry stages, yes. We will tell you in advance when nothing is happening on site, such as while material is on order.
