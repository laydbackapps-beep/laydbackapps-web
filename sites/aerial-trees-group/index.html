What we do

All tree services & stump grinding

From a single overhanging limb to a full removal and stump grind — one crew, one job.

01

Tree removal

Whole-tree removals, sectioned down safely where access is tight or the tree sits over a roof, fence or shed.

02

Trimming & pruning

Deadwooding, canopy lifting and pruning to take the weight out of overhanging limbs and make the property safer.

03

Stump grinding

Stumps ground out below ground level and the site cleared, so the area can be replanted or covered.

04

Chipping & mulch

Branches chipped on site, with mulch left for the garden or taken away.

05

Aerial / elevated access

An elevated work platform for trees that can't be climbed or that sit near buildings and powerlines.

06

Green waste removal

Everything cut on site cleared away, leaving the yard tidy.
