Why it matters

Containment that stands up to inspection

Encapsulation is a safety and compliance item before it is a visual one. A wrap that is loose, torn or under-tensioned fails the look and fails the checklist — so the tension, the laps and the tie-off points get done properly the first time.

01

Single point of accountability — quoting, installing and re-tensioning are all the same crew.

02

Worked to the scaffold programme, so levels are wrapped as they go up, not after.

03

Containment that protects finishes, keeps trades working through weather and keeps the street clear.
