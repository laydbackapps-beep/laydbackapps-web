Framing, decks, joinery.

Carpentry is a sequence — get the frame true and everything after it is easier. VBS takes on both the structural end and the finish end of that sequence.

01 / Frame

Framing & extensions

Wall and roof framing for new work and extensions. Walls plumbed and straightened before bracing goes on, so the plasterboard sits flat later.

02 / Deck

Decks & boardwalks

Subframe set to fall, joists spaced to the board, then decking laid with the gap consistent the whole way across — including around posts and steps.

03 / Outdoor

Pergolas & patio structures

Timber pergolas, posts and beams cut and housed to fit the space, with the fixings detailed for the weather rather than for the photo.

04 / Inside

Built-ins & robes

Robes, shelving and window seats scribed to the actual walls — not to the plan — because older Perth homes are rarely straight.

05 / Joinery

Custom joinery & trim

Skirts, arcs, cavity sliders and door sets hung so the gaps are even. The parts nobody photographs are the parts that give a build away.

06 / Fix-out

Doors, windows & hardware

Replacement doors and window units fitted and flashed, then hardware set so everything closes on the first push.

The joint is the tell.

Anyone can hide a gap with filler. A clean joint — cut tight, aligned, no squeeze-out — is what separates a built-in from a bought one.

Half-lap / dry fit

Measured, dry-fitted, then fixed.

Every run gets dry-fitted before it's fixed. That's the step that gets skipped on fast jobs, and the one you notice five years later when a deck board cups or a robe door rubs.

  • Timber selected for the job — treated for outside, dressed for inside
  • Boards laid to consistent gaps, screwed not nailed where it matters
  • Cuts and fixings detailed so water runs off instead of sitting
  • Site cleaned at the end of each stage, not just at handover

How a job runs.

Three stages, and you know which one you're in. No week of silence between the quote and the first day on site.

Stage 01

Measure & quote

On-site measure with photos of the existing structure, then a written scope — what's included and what isn't.

Stage 02

Build

Materials delivered, set out checked against the house, and the frame or subframe built true before anything is clad.

Stage 03

Finish & hand over

Trim, hardware and final fix, then a walk-through with you before the tools go in the ute.
