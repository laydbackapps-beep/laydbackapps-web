Questions

Can you fix a leak without replacing the roof?
Usually. Most leaks are a flashing, a valley or a handful of broken tiles — that is repaired and the rest of the roof assessed.
Do you do insurance work?
Yes — storm damage is documented with photos and a written scope for the claim.
How long does a re-roof take?
A typical house is three to five days depending on the roof and the weather.
Do you work at height safely?
Yes — harness, edge protection and a licensed operator where the roof pitch needs it.
