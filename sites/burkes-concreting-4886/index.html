How we work

Four stages, in this order

You know what happens on which day before anyone lifts a shovel.

01

Walk the job

We walk the site, take levels and talk through finish, drainage and access. You get the scope in writing.

02

Set out and form

Excavation, compaction and formwork set true to the line, staked and checked before the pour.

03

Place and finish

Concrete placed and screeded to level, then floated so the surface comes up uniform.

04

Finish and clean up

Broom finish for slip resistance, control joints cut to spec, forms stripped and the site left clean.
