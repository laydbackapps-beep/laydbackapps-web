Before you call

What to
have ready.

Four things that decide how a pour is set out, costed and scheduled. Ring through with these and the conversation starts on the job.

  1. 01

    Site access

    Where a truck and excavator can get in, and how far the pour sits from that point.

  2. 02

    Size

    A rough length and width, or the plan you are working from.

  3. 03

    Finish

    Plain, exposed aggregate, or a surface you have seen and want matched.

  4. 04

    Fall

    Where water needs to run to, and what it runs into at the edge.
