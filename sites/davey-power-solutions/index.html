Services

One electrician for the whole job

Electrical, solar, refrigeration and comms under the one licence — which means one trade on site, one point of contact, and no gap between the sparky and the air-conditioning installer.

01

Switchboard upgrades

Old fuse boxes replaced with modern boards — RCBO protection, spare capacity for solar and air conditioning, labelling done properly.

02

Solar & charging

Solar and inverter installs, system checks on existing arrays, and charging points wired back to a board that can carry them.

03

Air conditioning & refrigeration

Split-system supply and install, condensers sited for airflow and access, plus servicing and commercial refrigeration work.

04

Data & communications

Data cabling, comms points and NBN connections — terminated and tested, not just pulled through the ceiling.

CCTV & security

06

Repairs, maintenance & callouts

Fault finding, compliance and safety checks, and emergency callouts when something has tripped and will not reset.
