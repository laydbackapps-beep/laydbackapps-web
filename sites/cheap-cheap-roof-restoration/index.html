The four-step restoration

What a tile roof restoration actually involves, in order.

01Clean

Moss, lichen and dirt pressure-cleaned off the tile courses.

02Re-bed

Ridge capping re-bedded in flexible bedding compound along the peak.

03Re-point

The re-bedded capping pointed and finished so water runs off the ridge.

04Seal

The cleaned roof sealed and coated to protect the tile and finish the job.
