Process

Prep, seal, tile, finish

Four stages, and the waterproofing is signed off before a tile goes down.

Stage 01

Assess & set out

Falls, substrate and the existing waterproofing checked; the job set out before demo starts.

Stage 02

Make watertight

Membrane, flashing and upstands installed to the standard and left to cure.

Stage 03

Lay the tile

Tiles set on a consistent bed, courses straight, joints even, grout to follow.

Stage 04

Seal & hand over

Grout sealed, edges finished and the area left clean for the next trade.

Questions

Before you book

Is waterproofing included with the tiling?+

Yes. In wet areas the membrane goes down before the tile and is left to cure — the tiling is only as good as what sits under it.

Can you repair a leak instead of redoing the room?+

Usually, yes. The source is found first, then the smallest correct repair — re-tiling the whole area is only needed when the membrane has failed across it.

Do you replace broken roof tiles?+

Yes, including slipped, cracked and broken concrete or terracotta tiles, matched back into the existing course.

Do you supply the tiles?+

We can quote with the tiles supplied by you or sourced for the job — either way the set-out and the laying are ours.
