HOW IT GOES

Groundwork to finish, in order.

01

Site & set-out

The job is measured, set out and formed — levels and falls decided before anything is poured.

02

Formwork & steel

Formwork built square and braced, reinforcement laid to the plan. The prep decides the pour.

03

The pour

Concrete placed, screeded and finished by the crew — textured, swirl or plain, to the agreed finish.

04

Cure & handover

Forms stripped, edges cleaned, surface cured properly. You get the finish you signed for.
