About GreenLine Turf & Irrigation

GreenLine Turf & Irrigation supplies and lays turf and installs the watering to keep it alive — working across new house builds and established yards around Bundaberg and the surrounding areas.

That covers the lot: ground prepared and levelled, turf laid, garden beds and planting in, and sprinkler or drip irrigation set up and running before the job is finished.
