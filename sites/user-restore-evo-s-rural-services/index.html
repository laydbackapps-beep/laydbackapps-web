The jobs we get called for.

Rural work is rarely one thing. Most calls start with one job and end with a list, so we quote the whole property rather than one line item.

Fencing and repairs

Broken runs, sagging wire, strained posts and gates that no longer line up. Patched where it will hold, rebuilt where it won't.

Slashing and paddock upkeep

Long grass brought back down around the house, sheds, fence lines and lanes — then kept on a run if you want it regular.

Ground work and access

Levelling out a rough patch, topping up a driveway, cutting a track through to a shed or a back paddock so a vehicle can get in.

Yard and shed clear-outs

Old fencing wire, machinery offcuts, general rubbish cleared out and taken away, so the shed is usable again instead of a storage problem.
