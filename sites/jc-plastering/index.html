What we do

The lining package, start to finish.

01

Plasterboard fixing

Walls and ceilings sheeted to the frame, set out so joints land where they should and the finished surface stays flat.

02

Setting & stopping

Three-coat stopping, taped joints and screw heads filled flush, sanded ready for primer and paint.

03

Cornice & shadowline

Standard cornice, shadowline and square-set junctions, cut and fitted clean through corners and bulkheads.

04

Patch repairs

Holes, cracks, water damage and removed fixtures patched and blended so the repair disappears under paint.

05

Ceilings & bulkheads

Ceiling linings, dropped bulkheads and ceiling roses, sheeted and set with the framing worked out first.

06

Renovation make-good

Strip-out, re-sheet and finish after a kitchen, bathroom or extension, coordinated around the other trades.

How a job runs

One crew, one sequence.

Look at it

On site, measure the rooms and talk through what the finish needs to be. The quote comes back in writing.

Sheet and set

Fixing, stopping and cornice run in order, with sanding done once the setting has cured rather than rushed the same day.

Hand over clean

Offcuts out, floors swept, surfaces ready for the painter. Nothing left behind the skirting.
