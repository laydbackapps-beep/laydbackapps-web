What we do

Tree & garden services

From a single overhanging limb to a full block cleared and left ready for the next stage.

Tree removal

Trees taken down in sections where there is no room to drop them, rigged away from roofs, fences and paving.

Pruning & shaping

Deadwooding, reduction and canopy shaping to clear buildings and lines while keeping the tree healthy.

Stump grinding

Stumps ground below the surface and the hole backfilled, so the lawn or paving can go straight back over.

Land clearing

Blocks cleared of scrub, regrowth and unwanted trees, with the material mulched or carted away.

Garden & landscaping

Retaining, paving, garden beds and turf reinstated around the work so the yard is finished, not just cut back.

Storm & emergency

Storm-damaged and fallen trees made safe and cleared, including limbs left hanging over structures.

Questions

Before you book

Trees close to the house — can you still remove them?+

Yes. Those trees are climbed and taken down in sections, with the pieces rigged and lowered rather than dropped, so nothing lands on the roof, fence or paving.

Do you remove the stump as well?+

Stumps are ground out below the surface and the hole backfilled with the grindings — ask for it as part of the quote and it is done in the same visit.

What happens to the green waste?+

Branches and foliage are chipped on site and the mulch can be left for your garden or carted away, whichever you prefer.

Do you handle storm damage?+

Yes. Storm-damaged, split and fallen trees are made safe and cleared, including hanging limbs and trees resting on structures.
