What we build & fix

Contracting work, start to handover

Six things we get called for most. If your job is a mix of them, that is normal — the same crew runs it end to end instead of handing you between trades.

01

Concreting

Slabs, footings, driveways, paths and pads — set out, formed, poured and finished on site.

02

Site works

Clearing, cutting, levelling and preparation, so what goes on top of the ground goes on something solid.

03

Structural repairs

Repair work where concrete or structure has cracked, dropped or moved and needs putting right.

04

Retaining

Retaining walls to hold ground where it needs holding, built to take the load behind them.

05

Fencing

Boundary and yard fencing, including gates, lined up and set straight.

General contracting

The whole job carried by one contractor — demolition through site work to the final trim.
