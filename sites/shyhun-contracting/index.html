Steel water tank sales

Delivery to your site

The tank is brought out to you and placed where it is going, so you are not left working out how to move it yourself.

Site preparation

The ground gets prepared first: a level base and the pad set out so the tank sits true and stays that way.

Installation

Tanks are set on the prepared pad and levelled, with the base ring and footing right before anything goes on top of it.

Plumbing connections

Inlet and outlet pipework is run and connected as part of the job, so the tank is ready to take water when we leave.

Questions

Before you call

Do you install the tanks as well as sell them?

Yes. Sales and installation of steel water tanks is what the business specialises in, so the tank you buy is the tank we put in.

What tanks do you supply?

Steel water tanks: the round corrugated type you can see throughout the photographs on this page. Call 0423 771 074 and we will work through what suits your place and how much water you need.

Do you deliver to site?

Yes. The tank is brought out and placed where it is going as part of the job, then set on the prepared pad.

What do you need to give me a price?

The details of the job: where the tank is going, how much water you are looking to store, how easy the site is to get into, and roughly when you want it done. Photographs of the area help.

Do you connect the plumbing?

Inlet and outlet pipework is run and connected as part of the installation, so the tank is ready to take water at handover.
