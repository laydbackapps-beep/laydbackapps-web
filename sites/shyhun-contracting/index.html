How a job runs

Quoted from the site, run to the scope, checked before handover.

Step 01

Walk the job

Access, existing structure and the plan are checked on site before a price.

Step 02

Agree the scope

Written scope with what is included and what is not, and a start date.

Step 03

Build it

Sequenced trades, site kept secure and weatherproof at the end of each week.

Step 04

Check it over

Walked with you before handover, with the snag list closed out.

Who you are dealing with

Shyhun Contracting Services is owner-run. The person who quotes the job is on site for it, which is why the scope and the finish match what was agreed.

Work is priced from a site visit with a written scope, and variations are agreed before they happen rather than appearing on the invoice.
