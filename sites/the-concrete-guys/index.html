How a pour runs

Ground work first, then the concrete

Most of a good slab is decided before the truck arrives. This is the order it happens in.

01

Setout & levels

Levels and falls taken on the ground, setout marked and checked square before anything is cut or dug.

02

Excavate & compact

Base dug to depth and compacted in layers, so the slab has something solid to sit on instead of soft fill.

03

Formwork & steel

Formwork set to the setout and braced, mesh or bar placed and tied, cover checked before the pour.

04

Pour & screed

Truck in, concrete placed and spread in one run where access allows, then screeded back to the levels.

05

Finish & joints

Finish worked in while the concrete is still green, joints cut on the setout, then the slab is left to cure.

06

Clean up

Site swept and offcuts taken away when we leave — you are not left with the mixer wash or the formwork.

The job

Concrete poured to sit flat and stay put

A slab is only as good as what is under it and how the joints are set. We work to the setout, keep the falls running away from the building, and put the joints where the slab will move — so you get a surface that drains, does not pond, and does not crack through the middle of the panel.

Sizes range from a single path to a full driveway and shed slab. If you have plans, a setout or just a photo of the area, send it through and we will take it from there.

Questions

Before you get a price

How do you price a concrete job?

From the site. Size, thickness, access for the truck, how much digging and how much steel, the finish, and where the joints need to go all move the number. Give us a call or send the form and we will work out what the job actually needs.

What do you need from me to quote?

A rough size of the area and what it is for — a driveway, a shed slab, a path. Plans, a setout or a photo of the area helps us get it right first time.

Do you do exposed aggregate and sawn finishes?

Yes. Aggregate is picked before the pour and washed back evenly once the concrete has picked up, then sealed after curing. Broom, plain and sawn finishes are all done.

How far out are you booked?

It moves with the weather and the schedule. Once a quote is accepted we book the job in and confirm the date with you — concrete does not go down in the wrong conditions.

Where do you work?

Perth and the surrounding metro suburbs. Call or text 0481 221 003 with your suburb and we will confirm from there.

Do you take away the old concrete?

Removal and make-good can be included in the job so the area is ready for the new slab. Tell us what is there now when you send the details.
