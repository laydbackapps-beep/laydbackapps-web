Privacy Policy

What we collect

The Concrete Guys collect the contact details you submit through our quote form — typically your name, phone number, email address, suburb and a description of the concrete work you need.

Why we collect it

Only to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote and arrange the work. We do not sell, rent or trade your information.

Who we share it with

We do not share your details with third parties for marketing. Information may be disclosed where required by law.

Storage

Enquiries are stored securely and kept only as long as needed for the quote and any resulting work, or as required by law.
