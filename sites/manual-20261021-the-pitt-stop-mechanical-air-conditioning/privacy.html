Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

What we collect

The Pitt Stop Mechanical & Air Conditioning Pty Ltd collects the contact details you submit through the booking form — typically your name, phone number, email address, vehicle details and a description of the work you need.

Why we collect it

Only to respond to your enquiry, agree a time and carry out the work. We do not sell, rent or trade your information.

Who we share it with

We do not share your details with third parties for marketing. Information may be disclosed where required by law.

Storage

Enquiries are stored securely and kept only as long as needed for the booking and any resulting work, or as required by law. The form is transmitted through a hosted form service.

Cookies

This site sets no tracking or advertising cookies. Web fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of that request.
