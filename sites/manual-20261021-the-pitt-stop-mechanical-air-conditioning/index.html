02 — Air-conditioning

Why a regas often doesn’t fix it.

A car air-conditioner is a sealed loop. If it is blowing warm, gas has usually gone somewhere — a hose, a seal, the condenser, the compressor. Topping it up without finding the leak is the expensive way to stay cool for a month.

  1. 01
    Pressure and vent temperatureWe measure what the system is actually doing before touching it.
  2. 02
    Leak testFind where it is escaping — that is the actual fault.
  3. 03
    Repair, then regasOnly once the loop holds pressure is the gas worth putting back in.
