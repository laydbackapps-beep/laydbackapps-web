Privacy

Plain English. This page covers the quote form on the Empire Pro Painting site.

What is collected

Only what you type into the quote form: your name, phone number, and, if you add them, your email address, your suburb, what needs repainting and the message you write.

Why it is collected

To answer the enquiry — a time arranged to look at the surfaces and price the repaint. Nothing else.

Who sees it

Empire Pro Painting. The details are sent straight to the business through the site's quote form. They are not sold, rented or passed on to anyone else.

How long it is kept

For as long as the enquiry is live. Once it is finished with, it is deleted.

Your choice

You can ask to see what has been kept, have it corrected, or have it removed at any time. Call 0400 250 074 or say so in your next message.

Fonts and tracking

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. The page loads a font file from Google Fonts, so Google sees the request for that file when the page opens.

What this site is

A page describing the interior and exterior painting Empire Pro Painting takes on. Nothing on it is a quote — colour, surface condition and repairs are judged in the room.
