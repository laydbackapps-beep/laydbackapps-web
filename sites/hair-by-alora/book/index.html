Booking questions

How do I book Hair by Alora?

Choose your services, pick a live available time, and confirm your details. You'll get an email confirmation with a reference and calendar link.

Where is Hair by Alora?

Inside Aura Hair Collective at Shop 7/17 Laycock Street, Carey Bay NSW 2283, on the western shore of Lake Macquarie.

What are the opening hours?

Tuesday 10am–9pm, Thursday 10am–5pm and Friday 10am–4pm. Closed Monday, Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday; Saturday is by request.

How much does balayage cost?

Balayage with toner, cut, dry and style is $340 for short to medium hair and $360 for long or thick hair, with a 3.5–4 hour appointment.

What is the cancellation policy?

Please give at least 48 hours notice by replying to your confirmation email or calling 0476 427 024.
