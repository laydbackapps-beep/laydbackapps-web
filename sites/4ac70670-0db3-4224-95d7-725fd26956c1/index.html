SHEET 08 · NOTES & QUERIES · RIVALI BUILT

Before you commit

The questions worth asking a builder

Is the quote fixed price or cost plus?

Most work is quoted as a fixed price from a full set of drawings and a specification, with a provisional sum noted for anything that cannot be known until the frame is open. If the scope changes, the variation is priced in writing before it is built — so the final invoice is not a surprise.

Do you handle the council approval?

Yes — either a development application through council or a complying development certificate where the site and design allow it. We work with the certifier and the consultant team from the start so the approval is not sitting in a queue while the program slips.

How long will my build take?

A renovation is measured in weeks, an extension in a few months, and a new home typically eight to twelve months on site depending on the design, the weather and trade availability. You get a program at contract stage and a straight answer each week once we are on site.

Who do I actually deal with?

Dave runs the job and is the person you ring — not a call centre and not a rotating site manager. The trades are crews we have built with for years, and the site is left tidy at the end of every week.

Can you price from our own drawings?

Yes. If you already have drawings and engineering we will price from them and flag anything that will cost more on site before you commit. If you do not, we can point you to the draftsperson and consultants we work with regularly.

Are you licensed and insured?

Rivali Built Pty Ltd is a registered company — ABN 19 623 720 490 — building across Newcastle, Lake Macquarie and the Hunter. Licence and insurance certificates are provided with the quote, so you can check them before any work starts.
