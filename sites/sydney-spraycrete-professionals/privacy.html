Privacy Policy

Sydney Spraycrete Professionals — spraycrete & concrete resurfacing, Sydney NSW

This page explains what happens to the information you send through this website.

What is collected

Only what you type into the quote form: your name, your phone number, your suburb, the surface you are asking about and anything you choose to add about the job.

How it is used

Your details are used to answer your enquiry and to quote the work. They are not sold, rented or passed to anyone else.

Third parties

This site sets no advertising cookies and runs no third-party tracking scripts. The quote form is delivered to the business by the site's own form service so the enquiry can be answered.

Access and correction

You can ask for the details you sent to be checked, corrected or deleted at any time by calling 0411 068 286.
