Privacy Policy

Sydney Spraycrete Professionals

This page explains what happens to the information you send through this website.

What is collected

Only what you type into the quote form: your name, the detail you give us, and whatever you choose to include about the job. The preview form on this site is not yet connected to a mailbox, so anything typed into it is not transmitted or stored anywhere.

How it is used

Enquiry details are used to answer your enquiry and quote the work. They are not sold, rented or passed to anyone else.

Third parties

If you contact the business through its Facebook page, Facebook handles that message under its own privacy policy. This site itself sets no advertising cookies and runs no third-party tracking scripts.

Access and correction

You can ask for the details you gave to be checked, corrected or deleted at any time by messaging the business through its Facebook page.
