What we resurface

Spray-on concrete resurfacing for homes across all Sydney suburbs, from a single driveway to a full courtyard.

Driveways

Worn, faded or cracked driveways sprayed with a new textured surface that covers the concrete underneath.

Paths and walkways

Pathways and side access resurfaced in a slip-resistant finish, cut in cleanly along the edges.

Patios and alfrescos

Entertaining areas resprayed in the colour and texture you choose, ready for the season.

Pool surrounds

Pool surrounds resurfaced and sealed, with a surface that is easier underfoot around water.

Garage floors

Dusty, stained garage slabs cleaned back, sprayed and finished in a hard-wearing surface.

Courtyards

Courtyards and tight access areas resurfaced with the mess kept to a minimum.

Resurfacing, not replacing

The sound concrete stays where it is. The surface on top is what gets rebuilt.

No demolition

No breaking out, no skips, no formwork and no curing a new slab. Your driveway or patio is back in use in the same week it is sprayed.

Prepared properly

How long a resurfaced slab lasts is settled before the spray gun comes out. Every job is cleaned, ground back and primed first.

Dulux materials

All Dulux materials supplied, so the colour you pick is a specified product rather than something mixed on the day.
