About us

A demolition crew that leaves the site tidy

X Demolition Pty Ltd takes on residential and commercial demolition, asbestos removal and strip-outs. Whether it is a house coming down or an interior taken back to bare frame, the job gets a clear scope before anything starts.

Material is sorted as it comes out and the spoil is loaded away, so you finish the job with a cleared site instead of a pile of rubble to deal with later.

  • Clear scope and price before we start
  • Asbestos removal handled as part of the scope
  • Debris sorted, loaded and carted away
  • Site left clean and accessible
Common questions

Questions we get asked

Do you remove asbestos?

Yes. Asbestos removal is a core part of what we do: sheeting, fencing and lagging come out as part of the demolition scope and leave site bagged.

Do you take the rubbish away?

Yes. Demolition spoil, green waste and general rubbish are loaded out as part of the job, so nothing is left sitting in the front yard.

Can you strip out an interior before a renovation?

Yes. We take interiors back to frame and slab and make the services safe, so your trades start on a clean shell.
