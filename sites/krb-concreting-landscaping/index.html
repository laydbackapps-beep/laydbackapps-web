Concrete finishes and garden builds from recent work: the sort of detail that decides whether a job still looks right in five years.

How a job is run.

Straight through from the first look at the yard to the final clean-up.

Step 01

Walk the site

Access, fall and what is already in the ground decide the detail, so the number comes from standing on it.

Step 02

Set out & prepare

Levels set, area cut and boxed out, base compacted, drainage behind walls sorted.

Step 03

Pour & finish

Formwork and steel in, concrete placed, then finished broom, steel-trowel or aggregate.

Step 04

Garden & clean up

Beds, turf and paving brought to the new levels, site swept and left tidy.
