What we do

Concreting work for homes and businesses.

Driveways

Set out with the falls working away from the house, poured and finished to a hard, even surface.

Slabs & shed floors

Slabs for sheds, garages and extensions, steel and thickness matched to the load.

Exposed aggregate

Washed-back finishes that grip underfoot and hold their colour.

Paths & crossovers

Pathways and crossovers formed, poured and edged clean.

Site prep & formwork

Levels, drainage and boxing done before a metre of concrete is ordered.

Repairs & extensions

Cracked or sunken concrete cut out and repoured to match.

How a job runs

From the first look to the final clean up.

Measure and set out

Levels, falls and the pour plan worked out before quoting.

Prep and form

Base compacted, steel placed, formwork set.

Pour and finish

Poured, screeded, finished and cut, then cleaned up.
