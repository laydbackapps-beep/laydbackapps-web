Common questions

Before you book a pour

How long before I can drive on it?

It depends on the mix, the weather and the finish, but we’ll tell you the safe window on the day so nobody marks a fresh slab.

Do you do exposed aggregate?

Yes — exposed aggregate is one of our regular driveway finishes, and we’ll show you sample finishes before you commit.

Can you replace just part of a driveway?

Often, yes. If the base and the surrounding slab are sound, we can cut out and re-pour the failed section to match.
