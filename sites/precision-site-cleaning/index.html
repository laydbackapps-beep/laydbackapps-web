Stages

Cleaned in passes, because dust settles

One pass over a site never holds. Dust from the ceiling lands on the floor you just did, so the work runs in the order the trade work ran.

Stage one

Rough strip-out

Offcuts, packaging, tiling offcuts and trade rubbish cleared off the floor and out of the rooms first — you can't detail a floor you can't see.

Stage two

Top-down dust

Ceilings, cornices, top of frames and high ledges wiped down so everything above is clean before anything below is touched.

Stage three

Detail

Glass, tracks, hinges, cabinetry internals, gaskets, power points and the joins where two materials meet — the parts an inspection looks at.

Stage four

Handover walk

Final floors, then a walkthrough with you. Anything picked up on the walk is fixed before the site is signed off.

Who you're dealing with

A cleaning crew that understands the build

We're set up around construction, not domestic housework — the scheduling, the access and the mess are all different.

The clean is booked around the programme. If the tiler slips a day, the clean moves — not the other way around, because cleaning a wet floor is wasted money for everyone.

Machines, products and waste bags come with the crew. Site power and water need to be available, and access has to be clear for the gear to get in — if it isn't, we'll tell you before the day.

We work to inspection and handover dates. If you've got a certifier or a client walk scheduled, the clean is planned backwards from that date with enough time for the site to be walked and any defects sorted.

Everything hazardous leaves through the right stream and is documented. Builders don't want a sharps bin turning up in general waste, and neither do we.

Questions

Before you book

How is a builders clean priced?
By floor area, the stage of the build and how much detail is needed. It's quoted after the site has been looked at — a three-bedroom at frame stage is a completely different job to the same house ready for handover.
Do you supply the equipment and products?
Yes. Machines, products and waste bags come with the crew. Site power and water need to be available, and access has to be clear.
Can you work to a handover deadline?
Yes — the clean is programmed around the handover or inspection date, with the walkthrough built into the schedule before it.
Do you remove sharps and hazardous waste?
Yes, collected and disposed of through the correct stream, not into general waste.
How many cleans does a build normally need?
Two is common — a builders clean once the trades are out, then a sparkle clean just before handover. Bigger projects sometimes want a third pass after the final trade.
Can you clean part of a site only?
Yes. A single floor, a bathroom run or just the external hard surfaces is fine — tell us the scope and it'll be quoted on that.
