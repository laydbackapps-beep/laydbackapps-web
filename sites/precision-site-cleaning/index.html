Questions

How is a builders clean priced?
By floor area, the stage of the build and how much detail is needed — quoted after the site is looked at.
Do you supply equipment and products?
Yes. Machines, products and waste bags come with the crew; site power and water need to be available.
Can you work to a handover deadline?
Yes — the clean is programmed around the handover or inspection date.
Do you remove sharps and hazardous waste?
Yes, collected and disposed of through the correct stream, not into general waste.
