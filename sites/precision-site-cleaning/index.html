What we clean

The cleaning stage that has to be finished before a client walks through — and the mess nobody else wants to touch.

Builders clean

The full clean after trades leave: dust off every surface, stickers and splatter removed, floors scrubbed and windows cleaned inside and out.

Sparkle clean

The detail pass before handover or an open inspection — cupboards inside, tracks, sills, light fittings, marks off paintwork and glass finished streak-free.

Post-construction detail

Paint spots, render film, silicone smears and adhesive residue taken off tiles, glass, joinery and concrete without damaging the finish.

Hazardous & sharps removal

Site waste, sharps, glass and contaminated material cleared and disposed of properly, with the paperwork that goes with it.

Pressure washing

Driveways, paths, slabs, bin areas and facades washed down after the build, including the mud the trades tracked through.

Ongoing site presentation

Regular visits during a build so the site stays presentable for inspections, neighbours and the client's walk-throughs.

How a clean is run

Scoped from the site, dated against your handover, and checked before we leave.

01

Walk the site

Access, power, water and what stage the build is at decide the crew size and the time it takes.

02

Clean to scope

Top down, room by room, with the waste removed as we go rather than piled in one room.

03

Check and sign off

Walked with the site manager before we leave — anything missed is done then, not after handover.
