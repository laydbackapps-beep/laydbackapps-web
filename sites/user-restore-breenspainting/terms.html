Quotes

Quotes are based on the information available at the time — the drawings, the site inspection and the ground we can see. If conditions on site differ from what was described, or the scope changes, the price is adjusted and you are told before the work continues.

Work and scheduling

Work is scheduled by agreement. Weather, machine breakdowns, material availability and site access all move a program, and wet ground stops earthworks outright. Any date we give is a target, not a guarantee, and we will tell you as soon as we know it has moved.

Site access and services

You are responsible for telling us about anything in the ground that isn't marked — private lines, tanks, irrigation, old footings — and for arranging any permits the job needs. Underground services that are not located remain a risk on any excavation.

Payment

Payment terms are set out in the quote. Deposits may be required to secure a machine and materials. Invoices are payable by the terms stated on them.
