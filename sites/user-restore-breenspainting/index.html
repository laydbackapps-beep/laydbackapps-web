What gets painted

Interior and exterior work, quoted from a room-by-room list of surfaces rather than a single lump price.

Surfaces & scope
01Interior walls & ceilingsRepaints and colour changes, with filling and sanding done over the whole surface before a roller touches it.
02Exterior & facadesWeatherboards, render, eaves and fascias, prepped for the substrate and coated for the weather it faces.
03Trim, doors & windowsSills, architraves, frames and doors cut in by hand, with the sheen chosen per surface.
04Decks, fences & gatesRe-coats and colour changes on timber, with the prep matched to how weathered the surface is.

How it runs

Three stages, in this order. The quote is settled on the surfaces, and the finish is checked in daylight at the end.

  1. Stage 01Walk and quoteEvery surface listed room by room, with the colours and sheen agreed before a drop sheet goes down.
  2. Stage 02Prep and maskFilling, sanding, caulking and masking. This is the part that decides how the finished wall actually looks.
  3. Stage 03Coat and checkCoats cut in and rolled, then a walk-through in daylight with a touch-up list closed out before handover.
