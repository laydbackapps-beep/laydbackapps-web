About Elite Spraycrete

Elite Spraycrete specialises in premium concrete resurfacing — taking existing concrete that has faded, cracked or gone grey and giving it a durable new surface.

Resurfacing keeps the concrete you already have, which means less mess, less waste and less time off the driveway than a full replacement.
