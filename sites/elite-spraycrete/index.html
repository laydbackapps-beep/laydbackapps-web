Questions

How long before I can drive on it?
Foot traffic after 24 hours, cars usually after seven days — it depends on the mix and the weather.
Do you take the spoil away?
Yes, excavation spoil is either spread where it is useful or carted off.
Can you match my existing concrete?
Colour and finish are matched as closely as the existing pour allows — it is checked before quoting.
