What we do

Everything a tired tiled roof needs, done in the one job.

Roof Pressure Cleaning

Years of dirt, lichen and moss lifted off the tiles with a controlled high-pressure clean before any restoration work starts.

Re-Bedding & Re-Pointing

Ridge and hip capping lifted, re-bedded in fresh mortar and re-pointed so the capping lines are straight and watertight.

Ridge Capping

Loose, cracked or drummy capping removed and replaced, then finished to match the rest of the roof.

Roof Re-Spray & Sealing

A tinted roof membrane sprayed over the cleaned tiles to bring back the colour and protect the surface.

Broken Tile Replacement

Cracked and slipped tiles swapped out before the re-spray so the finished roof is sound, not just recoloured.

Gutters, Valleys & Flashings

Gutters cleared, valleys checked and flashings re-sealed around penetrations and ridges.

About Interlocked Roof Restoration

Interlocked Roof Restoration works on tiled roofs: cleaning them back, securing the capping and giving the whole roof a fresh, even colour.

The work is planned around the roof itself. The clean comes first, then any broken tiles and capping, then the spray. That order is what makes the finished roof look right and last.
