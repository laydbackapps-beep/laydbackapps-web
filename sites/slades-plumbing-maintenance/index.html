What we fix

The jobs that come up most

Most callouts are one of these six. If yours is not listed, ring and describe it — plumbing problems are usually found by symptoms, not by name.

01

Blocked drains & toilets

Slow drains, gurgling, backing up or a toilet that will not clear. Cleared with the right tool for the blockage rather than a plunger and hope.

02

Leaks & burst pipes

Dripping taps, leaking joints, water where it should not be. Found, isolated and repaired — including under-sink and behind-wall leaks.

03

Hot water

No hot water, not enough of it, or a unit leaking or tripping. Repairs and replacements, with the options explained before anything is ordered.

04

Taps, toilets & cisterns

Dripping, running, noisy or not filling. Washers, cartridges, cistern valves and full replacements where the fitting is past saving.

05

General maintenance

The small jobs that get put off — a tap here, a gutter there, an outside tap that has seized. Booked together so one visit covers them.

06

Storm & overflow

Downpipes, stormwater and overflow problems after heavy rain — cleared and checked so the water goes where it is meant to.

Two kinds of callout

Water going where it should not

There is a difference between a problem that can be booked and a problem that is happening right now. Both get answered, but they are handled differently.

Active leak or burst

Water is running, a ceiling is bulging, or a drain is backing up into the house. Ring — do not wait for a form reply.

  • Turn the water off at the meter if you can
  • Move what is under the leak
  • Ring and describe what you can see

Booked repair

A dripping tap, a slow drain, a hot water unit on its way out. Not urgent today, but it will not fix itself.

  • Send the details and a photo
  • Get a window and a price
  • Booked jobs are grouped by area
How a callout runs

Four steps, no guesswork

STEP 01

Describe it

What it is doing, where, and how long. A photo of the fitting or the water stain saves time.

STEP 02

Find the cause

On site, the actual cause is confirmed before parts are quoted — the symptom is not the problem.

STEP 03

Price it

Parts and labour explained up front. If it changes once it is opened up, you hear it before work continues.

STEP 04

Fix & clean up

Work done, tested, and the area left as it was found — not with the old parts left on the floor.

Questions

Asked before every callout

Do you charge a callout fee?
The price is explained before work starts, including any callout component. You are told what it will cost before anything is pulled apart.
Can you come today?
Active leaks and blocked drains are prioritised. Ring rather than email — a booking form does not get read while water is running.
Do you repair or replace hot water units?
Both, depending on the unit and the fault. If a repair is throwing money at a failing unit, that is said plainly rather than quoted anyway.
Can you do several small jobs in one visit?
Yes, and it is cheaper that way. List everything when you book so the visit is planned for the whole lot.
Are you insured?
Yes — plumbing work is carried out insured, and the details can be provided with the quote.
