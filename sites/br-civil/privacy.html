Privacy

Last updated 19 November 2026

BR CIVIL collects only what you put into the quote form on this site, or what you give us on the phone or by email: your name, phone number, email address, the site suburb and a description of the work.

Why we hold it

To answer your enquiry, quote the job and keep a record of the work. Nothing else. We do not sell, rent or trade your details, and you are not added to a mailing list.

Who sees it

  • BR CIVIL, and nobody else, unless you ask us to deal with someone on your behalf.
  • Enquiries are delivered through the website's enquiry service, which passes the message to BR CIVIL's email.
  • Records may be kept where the law requires them — for example for taxation or warranty purposes.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no advertising cookies and runs no analytics or tracking pixels. Typefaces are requested from Google Fonts, which means your browser's IP address is visible to Google when the page loads.

Having your details removed

Email Blayne@brcivil.com.au or call 0427 370 805 and ask. We delete what we are not required to keep, and tell you what was removed.

Complaints

Tell us first — most of it is fixable in a phone call. Formal privacy complaints can go to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
