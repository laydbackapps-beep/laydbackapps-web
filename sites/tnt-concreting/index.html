A driveway only stays put if the layers under it are right. TNT Concreting strips, compacts, ties the steel and holds the levels before a single barrow of concrete hits the ground — which is why the finished surface stays flat and crack-free.

04 — How we pour

Same five steps, every job

STEP 01

Set out

Mark it on the ground and confirm the levels and falls before anything is dug.

STEP 02

Excavate & compact

Strip the area, remove the old surface and compact a stable base.

STEP 03

Form & steel

Box it up square, place and tie the mesh or bar to spec.

STEP 04

Pour

Pour, screed and float the slab to a consistent, level finish.

STEP 05

Finish & clean

Finish the surface — broomed, trowelled or aggregate — then strip and clean up.

06 — Straight answers

Questions we get

How much does a driveway cost?

It comes down to the area, the access, the thickness and the finish. Send the size and a rough idea of the finish and TNT Concreting will price it properly.

Do you do exposed aggregate?

Yes — aggregate cast into the slab and washed back the same day to leave the stone showing. Popular on driveways, paths and alfresco areas.

Can you replace old concrete?

Yes. Cracked, sunk or tired concrete can be broken out and replaced, reusing the levels and tying the new pour back into what is staying.

What areas do you cover?

Bellbird, Cessnock and out through the Hunter Valley. If your job is nearby, call and we will tell you straight.
