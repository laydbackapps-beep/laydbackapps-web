From the first look to the final cut.

Every pour is worked back from the levels on site. Nothing gets set out until access, fall and drainage have been sorted.

Step 01

Look at the site

Access, fall and what is already in the ground. The quote comes off standing on it.

Step 02

Set out & prepare

Levels taken, area boxed out and trimmed, base compacted ready for formwork.

Step 03

Steel & pour

Form up, steel placed, concrete poured and screeded to the levels set out.

Step 04

Finish & cut

Troweled, broomed or washed back, then cut, cleaned down and left tidy.
