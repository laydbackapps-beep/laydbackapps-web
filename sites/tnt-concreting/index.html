What we do

Concrete work for homes and businesses across Bellbird, Cessnock and the surrounding Hunter Valley districts.

Driveways & crossovers

New driveways and council crossovers formed, poured and finished in plain, coloured or exposed aggregate.

House & shed slabs

Slabs set out and poured for homes, sheds, garages and extensions, with the levels and fall done right.

Paths & footpaths

Pathways, footpaths and entertaining areas poured and finished to a clean, even surface.

Retaining walls

Concrete and block retaining walls built to hold the ground back and keep the yard usable.

Exposed aggregate & coloured

Aggregate finishes and oxide colours for driveways, paths and patios that need to look sharp.

Saw cutting & removal

Saw cutting, breaking out and removal of old concrete, carted away and the site left tidy.

How a job runs

From the first look at the site to the final clean up.

We quote the job

Tell us the size, the finish and the access, and you get a clear price up front.

Set out and prepare

The area is set out, levels checked, and the base prepared and compacted before anything is poured.

Pour, finish, clean up

Steel and formwork in, concrete placed and finished, then the site is cleaned and left tidy.
