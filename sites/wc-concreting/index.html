Who we are

One crew, the whole job

WC Concreting handles residential concrete from the first set-out to the final clean-up. Driveways and crossovers, house and shed slabs, paths and walkways, patios and alfresco areas, exposed aggregate and decorative finishes — the same people form it, pour it and finish it.

Everything is set out and levelled before the truck arrives, so the pour follows the plan and the surface finishes even. Newcastle and Central Coast based.

How it goes
