What we render.

Sand and cement

Traditional render, ruled and floated to a flat, even surface. The base coat that takes the weather.

Acrylic render

Flexible acrylic systems for brick and panel, with the texture and colour chosen to suit the house.

Texture coats

Roll on, spray or hand-applied finishes. Consistent pattern across the wall, not patchy under the eaves.

Bagging

Bagged brickwork that reads uniform: joints filled and finished to a single tone across the elevation.

Repairs

Cracking, hollow patches and failed render. Cut back, matched and sealed so the repair doesn't announce itself.

New builds and renovations

Working in with the builder and the other trades to the program, not against it.
