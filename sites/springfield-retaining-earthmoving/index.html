In section

How a wall actually sits in the ground

Every retaining wall is a small piece of engineering sitting in the ground. The line of the wall is the visible part; the drainage and the backfill behind it are what decide whether it lasts.

  • The wall courses — stepped down the slope, sitting on a footing, not just stacked on the surface.
  • Drainage and backfill — the water behind the wall has to go somewhere, so it is drained and filled to spec.
  • Cut and terrace — ground cut back to a batter above, and filled and compacted to a usable level below.
