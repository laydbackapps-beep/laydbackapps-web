Questions

Do I need council approval for a retaining wall?
It depends on height and position — generally walls over one metre, or near a boundary, need engineering and approval. That is checked before quoting.
How long does a wall take?
A typical backyard wall is one to three days including excavation and drainage. Larger cuts take longer.
Do you remove the spoil?
Yes — spread on site or carted away, whichever suits the block.
Can you work around an existing pool or shed?
Usually. Access is the deciding factor, so it is looked at on site.
