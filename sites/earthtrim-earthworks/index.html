The ground work, start to finish

One machine and one crew from the first cut to the final grade — so the levels, the drainage and the spoil are all handled by the same pair of hands.

  1. 01

    Excavation & earthmoving

    Digging out for new builds, extensions, shed slabs and pools, with spoil carted away or placed where you want it.

  2. 02

    Site cuts & batters

    Cut and fill to the levels set by your plans, with batters left stable and the pad ready for the concreters.

  3. 03

    Trenching & drainage

    Trenching for water, sewer, power and stormwater, with pipe laid on a prepared bed and backfilled and compacted after.

  4. 04

    Driveways & base prep

    Stripping, boxing out and compacting the base so a concrete, asphalt or gravel driveway starts on solid ground.

  5. 05

    Retaining walls

    Excavation and prep behind timber, concrete sleeper and block walls, including the drainage gravel and ag line behind them.

  6. 06

    Cartage & spoil removal

    Truck and dog hire to take spoil out, or to bring fill, gravel, sand and topsoil in.

  7. 07

    Clearing & site prep

    Vegetation clearing, topsoil stripping and site clean-up before the build starts.

  8. 08

    Final trim & levelling

    Laser levelling and final trim for turf, yard regrades and pads that need to be level to the millimetre.

Tight access, small jobs, real ground

Backyard regrades, wall footings, shed pads and drainage runs behind an existing house are all part of the week. Where access is tight, the machine is sized to the gap — not to the job list.

Every job starts with a look at the site: where the water goes, where the spoil can sit and how the truck gets in and out without chewing up what is already finished.

How a job runs

No guesswork on site: the ground is looked at, the numbers are written down, and the work is set out before the bucket goes in.

  1. Part 01

    Look at the site

    Access, levels, existing services, where the water runs and where the spoil goes.

  2. Part 02

    Scope it in writing

    What gets dug, where the levels finish, machine time and cartage — set out before anything moves.

  3. Part 03

    Set out and dig

    Work is set out to the levels, then cut, trench, cart and shape as the site needs.

  4. Part 04

    Trim and hand back

    Final trim, compaction and a clean site, with the levels left where the plans asked for them.
