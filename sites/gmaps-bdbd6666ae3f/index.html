Methods

The right saw for the cut, not the other way around.

Openings, penetrations and removals — sequenced so the structure keeps carrying load while the work happens.

01

Wall sawing

Track-mounted vertical and horizontal cuts in walls, beams and columns — doorways, windows and structural openings, cut square and to depth.

02

Floor sawing

Flat sawing for expansion joints, service trenches, slab removal and penetration openings in floors, driveways and pavement.

03

Core drilling

Clean holes for services, anchors, drainage and sampling — drilled at any angle, wet or dry, to the diameter the job specifies.

04

Wire sawing

Depth without a length limit: bridge decks, heavy foundations, thick walls and sections too big or too awkward for a blade.

The cut

Every job is a line, cut true.

Set out, run a rail or a blade to the mark, then take the section out. Water-fed blades keep the dust down and the slurry gets vacuumed up before we leave.

Slab & floor sawing

Blade on a straight run for expansion joints, service trenches and slab removal - wet cut, trench depth to suit the drawing.

Wall sawing

Track-mounted blade for doorways, windows and structural openings - vertical and horizontal cuts, square to the line.

Core drilling

Clean holes for services, drainage, anchors and sampling - any angle, to the diameter the job calls for.

Wire sawing

No length limit on the blade: bridge decks, heavy foundations and thick sections too big or too awkward for a saw.

Dust & slurry control: water-fed blades, containment on the cut, slurry vacuumed and offcuts taken away - the slab is handed back ready for the next trade.

Process

Scoped, set out, cut, cleared.

Same sequence on a two-hour penetration and on a week of slab removal. Nothing is cut until the line is agreed.

Scope

Site walk, hazard identification, method statement and blade selection matched to the material and depth.

Prepare

Layout and marking, water containment, exclusion zones and equipment set on the line.

Cut

Controlled execution with the cut monitored the whole way — depth, alignment, slurry.

Clear

Slurry vacuumed out, offcuts removed, site handed back ready for the next trade.
