How it is built

The slab, and the ground around it

Concrete is only half the job. The other half is what happens at the edge of it.

Concrete / slab structure

Set out, formed, poured

The structure decisions happen before the truck backs in.

  • Ground trimmed and compacted, levels taken on site and fall set at roughly 1:100 away from the building.
  • Formwork set and checked square, with edges tooled so the slab reads as a straight panel from the street.
  • Control joints cut at roughly three-metre centres, laid out so the shrinkage cracks land in the joint, not across the panel.
  • Reinforcement and vapour barrier placed under the pour where the job calls for it, held on chairs off the sub-base.
  • Finish agreed before the pour: broomed, exposed aggregate washed back, or sealed.
Landscaping / bed and foliage

Turf, beds and planting

The grounds are finished to the concrete, not left shy of it.

  • Garden beds cut tight to the slab edge, soil level kept just under the concrete so nothing spills over the joint.
  • Turf laid to the paving line with the roots kept off the slab, so no cutting into a control joint later.
  • Planting set out after the pour, nothing walked over curing concrete, root barriers at the beds nearest the slab.
  • Drainage falls to the garden bed rather than back against the house or under the slab.
  • Mulch, edging and the final tidy done last, once the concrete has cured and the ground is settled.

Questions

Before we quote

Do you take on commercial and domestic work?

Both. Melbourne's Finished Concrete & Landscaping runs commercial and domestic concrete and landscaping work — driveways, slabs, exposed aggregate, paving and pool surrounds, plus the turf and garden works that finish around them.

How is the quote worked out?

From the site. Levels are taken on the ground, access and drainage checked, and the finish agreed with you before anything is priced. You get one figure for the concrete and the ground works together.

Can you do the concrete and the landscaping as one job?

Yes, and it is the better way to do it. The slab goes in first, then turf, planting and garden beds are set out against the finished edge, so the levels and the paving line line up instead of being worked around later.

What areas do you cover?

Melbourne VIC and surrounding suburbs. If you are further out, call 0451 993 098 and we will tell you straight away whether it is work we can get to.

How soon can you pour?

It depends on the ground, the access and the weather. Concrete is poured in the right conditions, not on the first free morning — we book the day around the forecast and confirm it with you beforehand.
