04

Asked on every job.

How much does a concrete driveway cost?
It depends on size, access and finish — exposed aggregate typically runs $65–$100+ per m². Pridecon quotes firm prices after a site measure, so there are no surprises.
Do you do exposed aggregate?
Yes — it’s one of our specialties. Every photo in the gallery is a real Pridecon pour.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes — Pridecon Concrete Constructions Pty Ltd (ABN 50 666 436 497) is licensed and fully insured.
Which areas do you cover?
The Central Coast through to Newcastle — if you’re on the marquee below or near it, we can get a crew to you.
How long before I can drive on new concrete?
About 7 days for cars, longer for heavy vehicles. Every pour comes with a cure schedule.
Do you work for builders?
Yes — slabs and structural pours for builders and construction companies, quoted per stage or per project.
