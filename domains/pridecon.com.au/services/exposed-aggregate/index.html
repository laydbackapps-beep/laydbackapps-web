Exposed aggregate

Exposed aggregate driveways, paths and courtyards.

Exposed aggregate gives you texture and grip instead of a slick finish. The stone is washed back at the right time so the aggregate reads evenly across the whole pour, with the edges and joints cut clean. A good fit for driveways, pool surrounds, paths and alfrescos where a plain slab would look flat.

What the job involves

  • Levels and falls set before the pour
  • Base prepared and compacted, not poured over rubbish
  • Reinforcement and thickness to suit the load
  • Cut and finished cleanly, site left tidy

Q

Exposed aggregate — the questions we get.

What is exposed aggregate?

A finish where the top layer of concrete is washed back to reveal the stone underneath. It is one of Pridecon’s specialties — every photo in the gallery is a real Pridecon pour.

What does exposed aggregate cost?

Exposed aggregate typically runs $65–$100+ per m² depending on size, access and the stone chosen. Pridecon quotes firm prices after a site measure.

Is exposed aggregate slippery?

The exposed stone gives a textured surface, which is why it is popular for driveways, paths and pool surrounds. The finish and seal are discussed at quote stage.
