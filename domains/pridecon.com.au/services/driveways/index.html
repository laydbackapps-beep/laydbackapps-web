Driveways

Concrete driveways poured and finished on the Central Coast and in Newcastle.

Driveways are the slab most people see every day, so the prep decides whether it still looks right in ten years. We set out the levels and falls so water leaves the slab, build the base up properly, pour to the right thickness and finish it while it is still workable. Plain, coloured or exposed aggregate finishes.

What the job involves

  • Levels and falls set before the pour
  • Base prepared and compacted, not poured over rubbish
  • Reinforcement and thickness to suit the load
  • Cut and finished cleanly, site left tidy

Serving Central Coast & Newcastle, NSW

Q

Driveways & crossovers — the questions we get.

How much does a concrete driveway cost?

It depends on size, access and finish — exposed aggregate typically runs $65–$100+ per m². Pridecon quotes firm prices after a site measure, so there are no surprises.

Do you pour plain and exposed aggregate driveways?

Yes. Plain concrete, coloured concrete and exposed aggregate are all poured by the same crew, with the finish agreed before the pour.

Do you handle the crossover and council requirements?

The driveway is set out and formed to the levels the site needs, and Pridecon quotes the job from a measure so the scope is clear in writing before work starts.
