    THE STEPS BETWEEN TASKS

    The Work Between Tasks Is Where Time Goes

    Every business has work around each task: receiving information, checking what it means, applying a rule, updating a record, notifying the right person and telling the next person what changed.

    That pattern appears in rosters, quotes, invoices, orders, customer updates, support, finance administration, reporting and internal handoffs. When each step relies on memory, the time cost grows across a five-person team or a large department.

    HOW FORWARD DEPLOYMENT ENGINEERING WORKS

    CLEAR ANSWERS

    Questions before you start?

    The audit is for anyone responsible for work that should move more cleanly, from business owners and operators to operations managers, department leads and executives.

    What does forward deployment engineering mean?
    Forward deployment engineering means building automation into the way your business already works. We map the people, tools, decisions and data involved, then build dashboards, AI systems and connected workflows for repeatable next steps. The result is shaped around your process rather than a one-size-fits-all software product.
    What kinds of work can be automated?
    Useful starting points include receiving enquiries, checking and sending quotes, rosters and scheduling, orders and fulfilment, follow-ups, customer updates, support questions, onboarding, finance administration, reporting, approvals and internal handoffs. The audit identifies where automation can create value in your actual workflow and where a person should stay in control.
    Can you automate rosters, quotes, invoices, orders and dashboards?
    Rosters, quotes, invoices, orders and dashboards can all be part of the review when they are part of your workflow. A system might receive a request, apply a rule, update the right record, notify the next owner, schedule a follow-up and show the status in a dashboard. The exact connection depends on the tools, permissions and decisions involved.
    Do we get one identical dashboard?
    No. Lay'd Back shapes the dashboard around the work, records and people that matter in your business. A small service team may need a weekly work view, while a larger organisation may need role-specific queues, approvals and reporting. The audit helps decide what belongs in the first build.
    Who is the service for?
    Lay'd Back works Australia-wide with businesses and organisations across industries, from 1–5 employees through to 5,000+ employees. Trades are one current focus, but the service also suits stores, professional services, community organisations and corporate teams when valuable repetitive work is slowing them down.
